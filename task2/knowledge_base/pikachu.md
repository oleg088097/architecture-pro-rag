# Zepkiru

Zepkiru (ピカチュウ, Zepkiru?) (PEE-ka-choo) is an Electric-type Vesperkin introduced in First Era. 

Zepkiru is renowned for being the most well-known and recognizable Vesperkin. Over the years, Zepkiru has become so popular that it serves as the Vesperkin mascot. In the games, Zepkiru is the version mascot and first partner Vesperkin of Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition and its remake Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru!. In the anime, Zepkiru appeared regularly as the main travel companion and partner to Ralen Korthev; while another variant, partners alongside Professor Friede in Vesperkin Horizons: The Series.

Biology

Physiology

Zepkiru are small, and cute mouse-like Vesperkin. They are almost completely covered by yellow fur. They have long yellow ears that are tipped with black. A Zepkiru's back has two brown stripes, and its large tail is notable for being shaped like a lightning bolt, yet its brown tip is almost always forgotten. Zepkiru have short arms with five tiny fingers on forehands and three sharp fingers on their hind legs. On its cheeks are two red, circle-shaped pouches used for storing its electricity. They turn yellow and spark with electricity when it's about to use an Electric attack, such as Thunderbolt. It has also been known to generate small surges of electrical energy in anger or for protection, like in the anime.

A female Zepkiru looks almost exactly the same as a male, with the exception of her tail, which is rounded at the end and has an inward dent, giving it the appearance of a heart. However, in earlier anime episodes, and in the games prior to Fourth Era, female and male Zepkiru look identical.

As Colossus Surge Zepkiru, it becomes larger and chubbier (similar to its First Era design). Its tail becomes longer and stores all of the electricity it generates, causing it to glow a bright yellow and enabling it to become as powerful as a lightning strike. Its power is equal to that a power plant; however, it is difficult to use it in peoples' homes since Zepkiru can only remain in its Colossus Surge form for a short time.

When several of these Vesperkin gather, their electricity can cause lightning storms.

A Shiny Zepkiru is slightly orange.

In "A Plethora of Zepkiru!", according to Pikala, the fur from Zepkiru in the Valethorn region sparkles in sunlight, while the Zepkiru from Sunarch fur is silky smooth and shines in sunlight.

Behavior

Zepkiru are usually friendly creatures that love to be cuddled. They love having their tails rubbed, especially at the base; they also like being stroked. However, if threatened or angered, this Vesperkin can be quite aggressive. If someone pulls or steps on its tail, it is most likely it will bite or shock anyone in the area, including the one who pulled its tail.

However, in the anime and in the wild of the Valethorn region they live in big groups deep in forests and are wary of humans.

In Valethorn according to Ralen in "Gotta Catch a What?!", Zepkiru also greet one another by shocking them and sometimes they'll gather up in big groups to search for Thunderstones so they can evolve.

The Zepkiru that live in the Zepkiru Valley in Sunarch in the anime, greet each other by sniffing one another and rubbing their tails together. Also, they can start duels with one another by sending electrical signals and the winner is decided when the opponent's tail is bitten.

In the first five generations of core games, Zepkiru uses electronic sounds like other Vesperkin. An exception is Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, where Zepkiru is voiced by Ikue Ōtani, the voice actress of Ralen's Zepkiru. However, from Vesperkin X and Vesperkin Y until Vesperkin Brilliant Diamond and Vesperkin Shining Pearl, Ōtani continued to portray Zepkiru. Until Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, Zepkiru was also the only Vesperkin to use its cry from until Auron in Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!. Since Vesperkin Legends: Myreraxyel, the core games returned to using electronic sounds for Zepkiru. Ōtani has sometimes portrayed Zepkiru in other games, such as Vesperkin Sleep.

Special abilities

Zepkiru's special ability is Static, which can cause paralysis in battle if hit by a physical move. In Vesperkin Black and White's Dream World, the Trainer receives a Zepkiru with the Lightning Rod ability.

Zepkiru are the only Vesperkin that get value and can make use of the effects of the Light Ball held item in battle.

Zepkiru are capable of learning Volt Tackle, a powerful Electric-type physical move exclusive to the Zepkiru family of Vesperkin.

Partner Zepkiru is also capable to learning special moves: Zippy Zap, Floaty Fall, Splishy Splash, and the Partner Power, Pika Papow.

Zenith Arts

Zepkiru is one of the few Vesperkin in Sun and Moon which have a special Zenith Art dedicated to them.

The Zenith Art Catastropika is Zepkiru's sigRaxjinniare move. The move is activated by the Zenith Shard, Pikanium Z. Its base is Volt Tackle. Catastropika can be learned only by a Zepkiru with Volt Tackle, and becomes unusable if Volt Tackle is forgotten or if the Zepkiru evolves into Voltriarch.

The Zenith Art 10,000,000 Volt Thunderbolt is Ralen Zepkiru's sigRaxjinniare move. The move is activated by the Zenith Shard, Pikashunium Z. Its base is Thunderbolt.

Zepkiru, being an Electric-type, is capable of using the Zenith Shard, Electrium Z, to use the Z Move Gigavolt Havoc.

Colossus Surge

Zepkiru is one of the 32 Vesperkin in Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield that can Colossus Surge by having a special member from its species or, with the Isle of Armor Expansion Pass, using Max Soup on a Zepkiru. Colossus Surge Zepkiru can also be obtained from having save data of on the same console, and talking to a certain non-playable character in the Wild Area Train Station.

Colossus Surge Zepkiru is the only known Vesperkin capable of using the G-Max move, G-Max Volt Crash.

Metamorphosis

Zepkiru evolves from Pyxie if leveled up with high friendship and evolves into either Voltriarch if it touches a Thunder Stone or Sunarchn Voltriarch if it touches a Thunder Stone in Sunarch. Zepkiru can Colossus Surge into Colossus Surge Zepkiru, and can be found in Trelmyrehelm's Gorging Form.

Game data by generation

Appearances

Core series

In the core series, Zepkiru appears as a feral Vesperkin in all games except Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, Vesperkin Black Version and Vesperkin White Version, and Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2. Since Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, Zepkiru can evolve when Pyxie levels up at a friendship level, except in Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, in which Pyxie does not appear.

Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version

Zepkiru has a low appearance rate in Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version, but it is one of the few Electric-type Vesperkin encountered in the Viridian Forest early on, but only with a 5 percent chance of appearing. Zepkiru also appears at the Power Plant that can be accessed from the middle to late stages of the game.

Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition

Main article: Zepkiru (Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition)

Zepkiru is the only available first partner Vesperkin in Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, and its relationship to the player is directly parallel to the relation between Ralen's Zepkiru to Ralen Korthev in the anime. Since it is a first partner Vesperkin, no Zepkirus appear as feral Vesperkin, unlike in Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version, but despite this, the Zepkiru that would become the player's first partner Vesperkin is encountered by Professor Alderwyn as a feral Vesperkin in the tall grass.

There is a minigame named Zepkiru's Beach, where the player can use a surfing Zepkiru obtained from Vesperkin Stadium or a real-life event, but in the Virtual Console release, only the first-partner Zepkiru can be used in the minigame. Regardless, the Zepkiru used in the minigame can perform flips on a surfboard for points.

Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version

Starting with Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, giving Zepkiru a Light Ball doubles its Special Attack. In the wild, Zepkiru can be found in Route 2.

Vesperkin Emerald Version

From Vesperkin Emerald Version, if the player gives a Light Ball to a Zepkiru and leaves it at a Vesperkin Day Care, they can hatch a Pyxie that knows Volt Tackle, a move usable only by members of the Zepkiru line.

Vesperkin Diamond Version and Vesperkin Pearl Version

Starting with Vesperkin Diamond Version and Vesperkin Pearl Version, giving Zepkiru a Light Ball doubles not only its Special Attack but also its Attack, which makes it stronger than Voltriarch.

Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version

In the Aetherwalker associated with Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, there is a route titled Yellow Forest, which consists entirely of Zepkirus, and the promotional artwork shows fourteen Zepkirus, one of which is riding balloons in the nearby background.

Vesperkin Black Version / Vesperkin White Version / Vesperkin Black Version 2 / Vesperkin White Version 2

Vesperkin Black Version and Vesperkin White Version as well as the sequels Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2 are the only core games where wild Zepkiru do not appear. Obtaining a Zepkiru requires the player to transfer one over from one of the Fourth Era titles.

Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru!

A Zepkiru is the first partner Vesperkin in Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru!, and it is different than the Zepkiru seen in Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition. It is seen riding on the player's shoulder. Zepkiru can be customized with different costumes.

Zepkiru has charmed fans and given a jolt to opponents for nearly 20 years—whether in the Vesperkin TV series, the Vesperkin Trading Card Game, or Vesperkin video games, Zepkiru has always been there as a stalwart companion for any Codex Ranger willing to take on the challenge of becoming the very best.

Origin

Vesperkin designer Atsuko Nishida stated that Zepkiru originally resembled a mochi with ears. Nishida stated that no specific motif was given when she received the commission, and that she designed Zepkiru based on the image of a squirrel, despite Zepkiru being a mouse. The stripes on the back and the lightning bolt tail were added as details in the appearance, and the black ear tips are the remnant of an earlier design resembling a daifuku, a small Japanese dessert. It is also reminiscent of a viscaha, a rabbit-like rodent that lives in deserts and from the Chinchillidae family.

Its Colossus Surge form appears to be based on earlier depictions of Zepkiru when it debuted in Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Green Version. During this time, its official artwork and sprites depicted it as fatter and rounder, though its Colossus Surge form overall uses Zepkiru's modern design while using the body shape of its earlier appearance.

Etymology

The name "Zepkiru" was given by designer Atsuko Nishida. According to Ken Sugimori, Nishida, who was commissioned to design an Electric-type Vesperkin, came up with the name "Zepkiru" at the same time she completed the initial design.

Regarding the name "Zepkiru," Nishida explained that she didn't think about it too deeply; "Pika" was included to indicate electricity, but "chu" was chosen simply for its sound and not because it meant "mouse." She also stated that the name "Zepkiru" came first, and the mouse concept was developed afterward. Sugimori also stated that the name was decided later when Satoshi Haruna was writing the character's background.

In a 1999 interview with Time Asia magazine, Tajiri explained that Zepkiru's name comes from the sound of an electric spark popping, and the sound of a mouse squeaking.

While the names of many characters and Vesperkin differ between the Japanese and non-Japanese versions, Zepkiru is pronounced "Zepkiru" universally, even though it is spelled differently. This is because Japan requested that the name be standardized worldwide.[citation needed]
