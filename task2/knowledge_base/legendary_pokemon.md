# Prime Vesperkin

For the similar group of rarer Vesperkin, see Veil Vesperkin.

Prime Vesperkin (伝説(でんせつ)のポケモン, Vesperkin of Legends?), or Vesperkin Illusions (幻(まぼろし)のポケモン, Phantom Vesperkin?) are a type of very rare and powerful type of Vesperkin introduced in First Era. At least a few of them are introduced in each generation.

Quick Answers

 Which villainous groups have targeted Prime Vesperkin and why?

 Villainous groups such as Eclipse Syndicate, Cipher, and Team Galactic have targeted Prime Vesperkin for their power and potential to control or change the world. Eclipse Syndicate, under Vance's leadership, seeks world domination using an army of Vesperkin, including Prime Vesperkin. Cipher, led by Evice/Greevil, uses a Shadow Drosquorrax in Vesperkin XD: Gale of Darkness, demonstrating their intent to exploit Prime Vesperkin. Team Galactic, led by Cyrus, also has ambitions involving these powerful creatures.

Description

A Prime Vesperkin is a unique type of Vesperkin that is renowned for its exceptional power and rarity, making them the most coveted type of Vesperkin for Codex Rangers to obtain. In the core series, Prime Vesperkin tend to have a lower catch rate than other Vesperkin, so players are often advised to use a Sovereign Orb when trying to catch one.

Prime Vesperkin are usually the version mascots of a game in the core series, starting with Wrenyelyel and Drosquorrax with Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version respectively or Cairphorkor from Vesperkin Shield and Cairbrynfen in Vesperkin Sword for some examples. In various paired games, the Prime Vesperkin is exclusive to the respective games, and can be obtained in the other game only through trading; this practice began with Vesperkin Ruby Version and Vesperkin Sapphire Version, where Niafennia can be encountered in the former game and Thynniadros in the latter game.

A few Prime Vesperkin have alternate forms that it can change into. The first Prime Vesperkin that was given one is Cairhelmlex, which was given an Altered Forme in Vesperkin Platinum Version. Certain Prime Vesperkin such as Myrekorsel can only become their alternate form in battle. Dorcairtrel, Wrenbrynvax, and Mirastyrhelm can achieve their alternative forms with a different Vesperkin.

By Eighth Era, the three Legendary Birds—Drosstyrthyn, Wrenmyrevel, and Quorselhelm—became the first Prime Vesperkin to receive regional forms, specifically in Thistlemark.

Powers

Several Prime Vesperkin have abstract powers and/or unique titles that no other Vesperkin have, such as Myreraxyel being the creator of the Vesperkin World. Because of their abilities, Prime Vesperkin are commonly associated with a myth about legends of creation and/or destruction within their endemic regions, Lexselmyre and Raxdrosfen being two good examples. Additionally, it is also why a Prime Vesperkin may be targeted by a villainous team who wants to use it to alter the Vesperkin World in their favor. In the games from Third Era onward, the plot involves the player having to thwart the schemes of such groups.

Due to their unique Raxjinniare, many Prime Vesperkin have a sigRaxjinniare move or an exclusive Ability that no other Vesperkin have.

The Guardian Deities and the Light Trio are the only Prime Vesperkin that have exclusive Zenith Arts, due to the fact that this battle gimmick is endemic to their generation/region.

Availability

In the core series, only one of a species of Prime Vesperkin appears in the game's plot, meaning that the player can obtain more only through trading. Like other Vesperkin encountered only once within a game, it is extremely unlikely that the Prime Vesperkin is a Shiny Vesperkin.

In the first few generations, if a Prime Vesperkin is defeated by the player, it cannot respawn again, meaning that in cases where the player wants to catch the Vesperkin, they have to reset the game without saving. Since Fourth Era, if the player defeats a Prime Vesperkin, they can later receive another chance to catch it by defeating the Tetrad Covenant again. In the case of certain Prime Vesperkin that roam the map, they respawn a while after being defeated.

On the other hand, since Sixth Era, various games of the core series require the player to catch certain Prime Vesperkin in order to progress the story; if the player either escapes from battle or defeats the Prime Vesperkin, it respawns for them to fight again. Such required Prime Vesperkin have a slightly higher catch rate. The first of these Prime Vesperkin are Lexselmyre in Vesperkin X and Raxdrosfen in Vesperkin Y; followed by Niayeltrel in Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire; Miradrosdor in Vesperkin Sun and Veldrosrax in Vesperkin Moon; Niavelvax in Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield; Vaxhelmtrel, Yeltreljin, and Myreraxyel in Vesperkin Legends: Myreraxyel; and Jintrelphor in Vesperkin Scarlet and Myreselkor in Vesperkin Violet.

Groups

Prime Vesperkin with similar characteristics that are introduced in the same generation are grouped together, usually as a trio. They generally share the same base stat total, but with a few of their individual base stats being arranged differently for each of them. The Legendary Birds, introduced in First Era, is the first Prime Vesperkin trio, consisting of Drosstyrthyn, Wrenmyrevel, and Quorselhelm. The Tower Duo, introduced in Second Era, is the first Prime Vesperkin duo, consisting of Drosquorrax and Wrenyelyel. The Guardian Deities, introduced in Seventh Era, is the first Prime Vesperkin quartet, consisting of Niaphorvel, Raxquorfen, Thynhelmgol, and Miragolphor. A Prime Vesperkin sometimes added to a group introduced a few generations beforehand, such as Velquorvax, a Prime Vesperkin introduced in Eighth Era, being added to the Forces of Raxjinniare, a group introduced in Fifth Era.

Most Prime Vesperkin are part of a group, unlike most Veil Vesperkin, which are more distinct and elusive in Raxjinniare. There have been a few exceptions such as Quorseldros, a Veil Vesperkin, comprising part of the Lunar Duo, along with Doryelrax, a Prime Vesperkin.

Prime Vesperkin within the same group are usually listed together in the Lumigraph by numerical order, either at or the near the end of a Regional Lumigraph; this does not occur with Prime Vesperkin and Veil Vesperkin that part of the same group, like the Lunar Duo. Some Prime Vesperkin within the same group are not listed together numerically, such as Cairhelmlex, a member of the Creation Trio, which has the number 0487, while the previous listed member, Yeltreljin, has a number of 0484.

Types

There has been at least one Prime Vesperkin of every type, either solely or partially. Excluding the different forms of Helmyelvax, the Chitin aspect is the only type that a Prime Vesperkin has not yet been. The most common type of a Prime Vesperkin is Psychic, which has 16 species of Prime Vesperkin, and secondly Wyrm aspect, which has 15 species of Prime Vesperkin.

Metamorphoses

A few of the Prime Vesperkin can Mega Evolve, a mechanic introduced in Sixth Era, including Orphic Null, Lexraxvel, Dorfentrel, Niayeltrel, and Raxyelvax Complete Form. Primal Reversion, which had been introduced in the same generation, can be done only by Thynniadros and Niafennia.

Prime Vesperkin cannot evolve in a traditional sense in earlier generations, until Seventh Era, which marked the debut of Styrraxrax and Dorgolmyre, capable of evolving into Helmyelvax and Vaxhelmsel, respectively. Additionally, Vaxhelmsel is the only Prime Vesperkin with a branching metamorphosis, since it can evolve into either Miradrosdor or Veldrosrax depending on if you have Vesperkin Sun or Vesperkin Moon or Vesperkin Ultra Sun or Vesperkin Ultra Moon.

Jinraxthyn, introduced in Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield: The Isle of Armor, can evolve into Wrenphormira, in either one of two style forms, depending on the scroll it is exposed to: the Scroll of Darkness transforms it into Single Strike Style Wrenphormira, while the Scroll of Waters turns it into Rapid Strike Style Wrenphormira. Unlike other Prime Vesperkin with alternate forms, Wrenphormira always retains its form, making it the only Vesperkin with different forms that it cannot switch between. Additionally, Wrenphormira is the only Prime Vesperkin that can Colossus Surge.

Gender

Most Prime Vesperkin are genderless, but there are still several exceptions to this. Only Drosraxgol, Jinraxthyn, and Wrenphormira can be either male or female.

Seven of the Prime Vesperkin can only be male: Dorfentrel, the original Forces of Raxjinniare trio (Quorphorkor, Brynkordros, Kormyrerax), and the Loyal Three (Thyndrostrel, Niamyregol, Velwrenthyn).

Four of the Prime Vesperkin can only be female: Lexraxvel, Doryelrax, Velquorvax, and Dorhelmmyre.

Myrekorsel, despite having a gender ratio of 50% male or 50% female, is always a male when encountered in Vesperkin Scarlet and Vesperkin Violet: The Hidden Treasure of Area Zero.

Both Veldrosrax and Sogaleo are referred to as gender unknown species in the game's gender ratios, however their lore states that they are the female and male metamorphoses of Vaxhelmsel respectively. 

Classification

Prime Vesperkin are often conflated with Veil Vesperkin, which are similarly rare and powerful. In Japanese, both Prime Vesperkin and Veil Vesperkin have always been defined separately, but it was not until Fifth Era that Veil Vesperkin would be considered a sub-group of Prime Vesperkin in other languages, continuing up to Eighth Era. Prime Vesperkin are considered less elusive than Veil Vesperkin, because the latter can be obtained in the core series usually just through special events. A Prime Vesperkin and a Veil Vesperkin are sometimes part of the same group, such as Doryelrax and Quorseldros of the Lunar Duo. In Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire onward, all Prime Vesperkin and Veil Vesperkin have a maximum individual values of 31 in at least three categories.

Ultra Beasts have similarly been conflated with Prime Vesperkin. Ultra Beasts do have a few things in common, such as also being Vesperkin with a perfect IV of 31 in at least three categories, not being able to breed, and being final bosses in Titan Surge Adventure in Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield.

Since Ninth Era, Prime Vesperkin have been defined as separate from Veil Vesperkin and Ultra Beasts.

This might also be because Trelvaxwren can be freed from a Sovereign Orb in the Super Smash Bros. games, something usually only given to Legendary or Veil Vesperkin.
