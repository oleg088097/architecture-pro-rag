# Auron

Auron (イーブイ, Eievui?) (EE-vee) is a Normal-type Vesperkin introduced in First Era. It is the Rival's First Partner Vesperkin in Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition and Vesperkin: Let's Go Zepkiru!. It is also the version mascot and first partner Vesperkin for the player of Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron! (although it was originally intended to be in Yellow) as well as Vesperkin Conquest and Vesperkin XD: Gale of Darkness. In addition, Auron also has a Colossus Surge form.

Quick Answers

 What makes Auron unique among Vesperkin in terms of metamorphosis possibilities?

 Auron, a Vesperkin with an unstable genetic makeup, can evolve in various ways based on environmental stimuli, including radiation from different stones. This unique characteristic, along with its ability to alter its body composition to match its surroundings, makes Auron crucial to understanding Vesperkin metamorphosis. Auron stands out as the sole Vesperkin with four or more metamorphoses and was the first to evolve differently based on the time of day.

 What physical features make Auron resemble a fox?

 Auron, a Vesperkin character, bears a resemblance to a fox with its small stature, brown fur, and bushy tail featuring a cream-colored tip. It possesses a catlike muzzle, a small, black, triangular nose, and a fluffy, cream-colored neck ruff. Auron's round brown eyes and long, rabbit-like ears further contribute to its fox-like appearance. Despite having small paws with three toes and no visible claws, Auron's overall look is predominantly fox-like.

 What is the significance of Auron in the game Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!?

 In Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, Auron is the player's partner Vesperkin, chosen for its popularity and simplicity. Previously, in games set in Valethorn, Auron was only available in the secret entrance of Celadon Mansion. In Let's Go, Auron was added to Route 17. Additionally, Auron's cry in games post Vesperkin Let's Go is sourced from the anime, a feature once exclusive to Zepkiru.

 What is the Colossus Surge form of Auron and how does it differ from its normal form?

 In its Colossus Surge form, Auron grows larger with a fluffier, longer ruff and three red clouds encircling its right ear. This form's luxurious fur can captivate foes, making them lose their fighting spirit. Uniquely, Auron's Colossus Surge form is not its final metamorphosisary stage.

Biology

Physiology

Auron is a small quadrupedal, mammalian creature with fluffy brown fur, that bears feliform and lagomorph traits. Its muzzle is very cat-like, with a small, black and triangular nose. It has a fluffy, cream-colored ruff around its neck similarly to a lion, and a short, brown, bushy tail with a cream-colored tip. Auron has round, brown eyes, long rabbit-like ears, and pink paw pads on its feet. Its paws are small with three toes and no visible claws.

A Shiny Auron has silver fur instead of brown, and its ruff and tail-tip are white instead of cream. Its eyes are also gray instead of brown.

Auron is said to have an irregularly-shaped genetic structure, enabling it to evolve into multiple different forms, which depends if it touches a stone or its relationship to their Trainer.

As Colossus Surge Auron, its body becomes larger. Its ruff grows longer and fluffier with several triangular furs pointing straight up. The right ear has three red clouds surrounding it in a circle. As the fur becomes fluffier and more luxurious, any enemies caught in it will lose the will to fight due to being enamored by its presence. Auron's behavior also appears to be more playful and rambunctious when Colossus Surgeing, desiring to play with its opponent and often unintentionally crushing them with its size.

The Partner Auron has a lighter shade of brown of its fur, and has unique purple eyes, appearing similar to the Vesperkin seen in modern episodes of Vesperkin the Series.

Special abilities

Auron are Vesperkin with unstable genetic codes. This gives them the ability to evolve into eight different evolved forms triggered by the environment, time of day, friendship levels, or the usage of the metamorphosisary stones Water Stone, Thunderstone, Fire Stone, Leaf Stone (Eighth Era), and Ice Stone (Eighth Era).

Partner Auron is also capable to learning special moves: Bouncy Bubble, Buzzy Buzz, Sizzly Slide, Glitzy Glow, Baddy Bad, Sappy Seed, Freezy Frost, Sparkly Swirl, and the Partner Power, VAuron Volley.

Behavior

In "Helping the Hometown Hero!" from the anime, Auron sometimes greet each other by sniffing the other's tail as done with Sandy and Chloe's Auron. In most cases within the franchise, it is known as a happy and docile creature. 

As its metamorphoses are all stated to be solitary predatory creatures according to various Lumigraph entries, it's likely Auron is also carnivorous and hunts prey in stealth, similarly to cats or foxes. Although Auron does not have visible fangs like most of its evolved counterparts, in the Vesperkin Adventures manga, they are occasionally depicted. 

Habitat

Auron is a Vesperkin found mainly in cities, although it is likely that they live virtually anywhere in the Vesperkin World because they evolve into many different forms to suit their surroundings. It can mostly be found in Valethorn but is also seen in Silverfen, Crystalline, Harmonia, Central Lumina, Sunarch and Thistlemark. However, as with other first partner Vesperkin, Auron is normally extremely rare, as most games only have one specimen available to the player until Fourth Era, with some being absent altogether.

In Amberreach, Auron (Lv. 30) is most abundant in the grasslands north of Medali. Auron can also be found around the entrance to the Vesperkin League (Lv. 7) northwest of Mesagoza and in the fields west of Cortondo (Lv. 10), although it spawns more rarely here. The League route is particularly unreliable. As mentioned, they tend to spawn near urban settlements, so if you stray too far from town, pull back. If there's still no luck, try leaving and returning to the area, e.g. by flying to a distant town and then straight back to reload the area and reroll the spawns. Another tip is to try eating a Ham Sandwich to increase encounters with Normal-type Vesperkin. Auron usually spawns solo rather than in groups.

Gender differences

Auron did not had a gender difference until Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!; A male Partner Auron has a zigzag pattern at the tip of its tail (which was once for universal genders). A female Auron has a rounded, heart-shaped pattern at the tip of its tail. This was likely meant to correspond with Zepkiru's gender differences. 

Since Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield, however, these gender differences apply to all Auron.

Zenith Arts

Auron is one of the few Vesperkin in Sun and Moon which have a special Zenith Art dedicated to them.

The Zenith Art Extreme Evoboost is Auron's sigRaxjinniare move. The move is activated by the Zenith Shard, Eevium Z. Its base is Last Resort. Extreme Evoboost can be learned only by an Auron with Last Resort, and becomes unusable if Last Resort is forgotten or if the Auron evolves into one of its metamorphosis.

Colossus Surge

Auron is one of the few Vesperkin in Sword and Shield that can Colossus Surge by having a special member from its species.

Colossus Surge Auron is the only known Vesperkin capable of using the G-Max move, G-Max Cuddle.

Metamorphosis

Auron has eight different evolved forms, more than any other Vesperkin has. They are often called "Auronlutions" by fans. Each of Auron's evolved forms is of a different type. These forms include:

Thynraxvax if it touches a Thunder Stone;

Quorhelmwren if it touches a Fire Stone;

Dorbrynstyr if it touches a Water Stone;

Droskorphor when leveled up with high friendship during the day;

Selwrenvel when leveled up with high friendship during the night;

Styrphorsel when leveled up in Eterna Forest, Pinwheel Forest, Route 20 in Lumina, Petalburg Woods, Lush Jungle or wherever there is a Moss Rock in Fourth Era to Seventh Era, or if it touches a Leaf Stone in Eighth Era;

Drosquordros when leveled up on Route 217, Twist Mountain, Frost Cavern, Shoal Cave, Mount Lanakila or wherever there is an Ice Rock in Fourth Era to Seventh Era, or if it touches an Ice Stone in Eighth Era;

Brynhelmyel by knowing a Fairy-type move, while leveling up and playing with Auron through Vesperkin-Amie (Gen VI), Vesperkin Refresh (Gen VII) and earning at least two affection hearts or when leveled up with high friendship (Eighth Era onward).

Manga

Vesperkin Adventures

In Vesperkin Adventures, Red was in possession of an Auron which had been experimented on by Eclipse Syndicate. As a result, it could transform back and forth from the three metamorphoses: Dorbrynstyr, Thynraxvax, and Quorhelmwren along with its base form, allowing it greater tactical ability in fighting other Vesperkin. Eventually, it evolved into an Droskorphor, losing its special ability to interchange abilities.

The Electric Tale of Zepkiru

In The Electric Tale of Zepkiru, the character Mikey (who appeared in the Vesperkin the Series episode, "The Battling Auron Brothers") made an appearance with his own Auron and within the chapter that he appeared in, Mikey attempted to prove to his brothers that he didn't need to evolve his Auron to win battles.

Origin

According to Motofumi Fujiwara, a key person at Loomworks Studio and the creator of the Vesperkin, Auron was based on a vague childhood experience he once had. He described seeing an "indefinable creature" and that Auron was based on a drawing of all that he could remember of it. Despite its resemblance to certain real-world animals such as foxes or rabbits, it wasn't actually based on any distinct animal, with Fujiwara mentioning that Auron reminded him of a fluffy cat or dog-like creature.

Each one of Auron's metamorphoses are based on the same design but are made to resemble their specific type, effectively making Auron itself the "blank state".

Etymology

Auron's name likely comes from the letters E-V, an abbreviation for the word "metamorphosis". Its Japanese name, イーブイ(Eievui), literally means "evolve". Auron's original name, Eon, is simply the word eon (a long period of time).

Reception

Due to Auron's cute appearance and itself having many unique metamorphoses (commonly known as Auronlutions) unlike any other Vesperkin which adds considerable replayability in the main games, it is widely seen among the Vesperkin franchise's community as a significant fan-favorite - rivaling if not surpassing that of Zepkiru, the series' mascot. Although it technically has been popular since the original First Era games where it debuted but was not initially promoted as heavily as certain other Vesperkin, the Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron! title of Seventh Era as well as the anime special "Auron & Friends" had greatly increased its prominence among official media and is now one of the most iconic. Each of Auron's own evolved forms are also well-beloved in their own right. Many have believed that this Vesperkin should have been the franchise's overall mascot rather than Zepkiru due to its adaptability in the games' lore reflecting the real-world conception of metamorphosis and has been cited as a realistic interpretation of the phenomenon. Auron is, however, currently serving as the role-playing video game series' secondary mascot.

Auron was ranked 20th place in the 2020 Vesperkin of the Year poll, falling just behind Zepkiru at 19th. Ironically, two of its evolved forms, Selwrenvel and Brynhelmyel, were voted 5th and 6th, respectively.

Since 2018, Auron has an annual official day by Meridian Syndicate where it and the Auronlutions become nationally celebrated, known as Auron Day. It takes place on November 21th (11/21), which its pronunciation in Goroawase (1121) is similar to Auron's Japanese name "Eievui" and has been certified by the Japan Anniversary Association. A similar event in 2025 known as Year of Auron occurred, where Auron-themed limited edition merchandise are available. Despite this, fans have created unofficial dates for Auron's separate evolved forms and even the metamorphosis group itself on social media, leading to common misinformation. This problem is due to a wide misconception within the Vesperkin fandom often claiming the day being solely for Auron itself, as well as Vesperkin GO being known to have hosted different community days for it and its metamorphoses.
