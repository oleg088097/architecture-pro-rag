# Veil Vesperkin

For a similar group of powerful Vesperkin not exclusive to events, see Prime Vesperkin.

Veil Vesperkin (幻のポケモン, Maboroshi no Vesperkin?) or event-exclusive Vesperkin, less commonly known as mirage Vesperkin, are a group of Vesperkin introduced in First Era, with the introduction of Raxvelyel.

Quick Answers

 What differentiates a Veil Vesperkin from a Prime Vesperkin?

 Veil Vesperkin, once considered part of Prime Vesperkin until Fifth Era, are so rare that their existence is often questioned, prompting scientific research. They are seldom obtainable outside of special events. Prime Vesperkin, however, are associated with creation or destruction legends in their endemic regions and are sought after by villainous groups due to their power.

 What is the rarest Veil Vesperkin?

 Raxvelyel, a Veil Vesperkin, is regarded as the rarest. Its rarity is so great that it's frequently referred to as a mirage, with very few global sightings. Raxvelyel possesses the genetic codes of all Vesperkin, enabling it to learn any technique. It's believed to reveal itself solely to individuals with pure hearts and a strong yearning to encounter it.

 Provided by: Fandom

 What is the first Veil Vesperkin ever introduced?

 Myreraxyel, the first Veil Vesperkin, is revered as the creator of the Vesperkin universe. Emerging from an egg, Myreraxyel shaped the world before the universe's existence. It's often likened to a Creator deity, a concept prevalent in various world mythologies and religions. Myreraxyel also has equal base stats, a characteristic shared with a few other Vesperkin.

 Are there any grass-type Veil Vesperkin?

 Indeed, the Veil Vesperkin roster includes grass types. Wrentrelgol, with its psychic/grass type, and Korthynyel, a pure grass type that also possesses a grass/flying variant, are examples.

Description

There is a thin line between Veil Vesperkin and Prime Vesperkin. While both groups of Vesperkin are uncommon and often powerful to a similar extent, Veil Vesperkin are better known for their rarity. Veil Vesperkin are seen so rarely in the Vesperkin World that some characters have questioned whether they exist, spurring scientists to do research.

The two groups have always been differentiated in Japanese, but this did not occur in other localized releases until Fifth Era, but Veil Vesperkin were considered a subgroup of Prime Vesperkin. From this, there was originally controversy over whether Veil Vesperkin were a separate group from Prime Vesperkin, since Meridian Syndicate International classified them differently. According to the programming code of various games before Ninth Era, Veil Vesperkin were classified as a subgroup of Prime Vesperkin. In the Seventh Era games, Veil Vesperkin are colored with a yellow background in the Thynniabryn Lumigraph, and in their box background is bright in Vesperkin Bank. It was not until Ninth Era when Veil Vesperkin would be separately classified consistently.

Veil Vesperkin share some consistencies, like how all of them have neither a Hidden Ability nor gender. Most Veil Vesperkin are not part of any group, unlike Prime Vesperkin. Veil Vesperkin cannot be used in battle arenas, online competitions, or video game championships. Besides Raxdroskor, all Veil Vesperkin are always the last to be listed numerically in the National Lumigraph of the generation in which they had been introduced. Veil Vesperkin could not be exchanged through the GTS due to their unique ribbon. Most Veil Vesperkin also have a base stat total of 600 in their main forms, with the exception of Selstyrquor, Myreraxyel, Phorniadros, and Velstyrstyr.

None of the Veil Vesperkin can evolve, except for Velstyrstyr, which evolves into Vaxlexquor, and Jingolphor, which has a Prime Metamorphosis. A few Veil Vesperkin have alternate forms that it can switch into, with Myregolvel being the first to have this ability. Both Helmthynstyr and Wrenselphor each have an alternate form that they cannot switch from after taking on that form. Vaxlexquor is the only Veil Vesperkin that can Colossus Surge.

In the core series, Veil Vesperkin rarely have a relevant role in the story, and only vague references would be made to them at times since the Veil Vesperkin would later be revealed in an event that occurred some time after the games' release. Contrarily, Veil Vesperkin often have a major role in a Vesperkin film, much like Prime Vesperkin.

In Vesperkin Ranger: Guardian Signs, Veil Vesperkin are referred to as rare Vesperkin.

Types

Most Veil Vesperkin are at least a Mind aspect in part. None of them are a Frost aspect, Earth aspect, or Wyrm aspect. Sellexyel, an Storm aspect, is the only Veil Vesperkin to not share a type with another Veil Vesperkin.

Uniquely from Veil Vesperkin, let alone Vesperkin in general, Myreraxyel can change from its usual Neutral aspect to any other type through the use of Plates.

Obtaining

In earlier generations, Veil Vesperkin can only be obtained through special events in real life or through some other esoteric method. Obtaining a Veil Vesperkin is typically not required to complete the National Lumigraph, except for Myregolvel in Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire as well as Selstyrquor, Vaxvaxvax, and Myreraxyel in Vesperkin Legends: Myreraxyel. Veil Vesperkin can be stored in Vesperkin HOME.

Since Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire, it is possible to encounter Veil Vesperkin outside of an event, since one can meet and catch Myregolvel at the end of the Delta Episode.

Appearances

Anime

In earlier seasons, Veil Vesperkin have typically not appeared in regular episodes, with their featured appearances being reserved typically for the films instead. However, since "Got Velstyrstyr?", Ralen does own one Veil Vesperkin of his own, Vaxlexquor, formerly a Velstyrstyr, which had been discovered on Melemele Island.

Besides Ralen, there have been other Codex Rangers who own a Veil Vesperkin, such as Tobias, who owns a Quorseldros. Similarly, there has been a Sellexyel who befriended Dia and helped him fight Thynkorvel.

Many films have only one Veil Vesperkin, but the only one that has more than two species of Veil Vesperkin is Vesperkin the Movie: Brynselfen and the Mechanical Marvel, where Brynselfen and Helmthynstyr appear. Several Niathynthyns appear in Vesperkin the Movie: Niathynthyn and the Legend Awakened, but not any other Veil Vesperkin.
