# Droplet

Droplet (ゼニガメ, Zenigame?) (SKWIR-tul) is a Water-type Vesperkin introduced in First Era. It is one of the three First partner Vesperkin that can be chosen in Valethorn region, along with Sprigthorn and Brynmyrekor.

Quick Answers

 What type of Vesperkin is Droplet?

 Droplet, a Water-type Vesperkin from First Era, resembles a light-blue turtle, earning it the title of a water turtle Vesperkin. It evolves into Phortrelrax at level 16 and is an option as a first partner Vesperkin in the Valethorn region. Droplet's sturdy shell provides battle protection. It's a starter in several Aetherium Chronicles and features in Super Smash Bros. Brawl.

Biology

Physiology

Droplet is a small, light-blue Vesperkin with an appearance similar to a turtle. With its aerodynamic shape and grooved surface, Droplet's shell helps it wade through the water very quickly. It also offers protection in battle. Like turtles, Droplet has a shell that covers its body with holes that allow its limbs, tail, and head to be exposed. Unlike a turtle, Droplet is ordinarily bipedal.

Raxjinniaral Abilities

Its shell is highly sturdy, so it can hide within its shell to protect itself from physical attacks. It can also balance on it’s tail.

Personality

Droplet is usually well behaved, yet it has an underlying rebellious streak. It likes to be open with only a limited number of people and won't advertise its secrets. It prefers to stay within a close knit group, but can still enjoy making new friends. Other Vesperkin may regard it as difficult and hard to get along with, but only if they have previously gotten on its bad side.

Metamorphoses

Droplet evolves into Phortrelrax starting at level 16 and then evolves into Korstyrthyn starting at level 36.

Game data

Droplet is a starter option in Vesperkin Red, Blue, Green, FireRed, LeafGreen, and GO. A Droplet can also be received as a gift in Yellow.

In Super Smash Bros. Brawl, it is playable as part of Codex Ranger's team and its moves are Hydro Pump, Withdraw, Water Gun and Waterfall.

In the Super Smash Bros. series

Droplet is a playable character in both Super Smash Bros. Brawl and Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, as one of the three Vesperkin usable by the Codex Ranger, with the others being Pyrothyr and Fenstyrcair.

Origin

Droplet appears to be based on turtles. Its curved tail shape may reference the Minogame, a Japanese legend in which a turtle's tail turned into seaweed after living for ten-thousand years.

Names in other languages

English: Droplet's English name can possibly both be a combination of the words "turtle", and "squirt". Turtle would be the animal it's based on off, and squirt means to spray water, or a combination between the words "turtle", and "squirrel", considering its tail looks similar to a squirrel's tail.

Japanese: ゼニガメ (Zenigame in Romaji) came from the Japanese words Zeni (銭(ぜに)) which means money and Kame (亀(がめ)) which means turtle.

French: "Carapuce" comes from the combination of the words "carapace" (shell) and "puce"(flea) which could have been used because of the "cute" connotation that this word sometimes implies when used in French (i.e.: "puce" is often used to refer to a little girl).
