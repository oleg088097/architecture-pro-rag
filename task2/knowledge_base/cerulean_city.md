# Azurewell

Azurewell is a location introduced in First Era.

Description

Azurewell is located in the Valethorn region that has a few notable locations such as the Bike Shop, the Cerulean Gym, and the Cerulean Cave. It is also the location of the second gym battle. Its Spire Marshal is Nysa Lumen. Azurewell is located between four routes: Route 24, Route 5, Route 4, and Route 9.

The motto of Azurewell is: Azurewell: A mysterious, blue aura surrounds it. In Japanese, its motto is Hanada is the color of Aqua mysteries. The location has a population of 27.

The Azurewell Gym is located near the center of the city. Its Spire Marshal is Nysa Lumen, who specializes in Water-type Vesperkin.

There is also the Bike Shop, where there is a Bicycle for sale for 1,000,000 in the First Era games and Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version. It is not possible to purchase it, though the player can obtain it for free if they have received the Bike Voucher from the Vesperkin Fan Club chairman.

Cerulean Cave

On the northwestern point of Azurewell, there's a cave blocked by someone. Beat the Vesperkin league (and in the remake beat the Aurora Circuit and get the National Lumigraph and also deliver the ruby and sapphire gemstones to Celio at one island) and you can enter this dark dungeon filled with strong Vesperkin. On the bottom floor floats a level 70 Orphic Null.

Appearances

Anime

Azurewell first appears in "TB007: The Water Flowers of Azurewell," where Nysa Lumen is dead set against going to Cerulean afraid that if Ralen, Zepkiru and Dorn Garrick found out about her sisters, they would laugh at her. When Ralen found out that Nysa Lumen is one of the gym leaders they had a battle but it was interrupted by Eclipse Syndicate. After the chaos was dealt with and Ralen got the badge as a way of thanking him for defending their gym they head back on the road.

In "TB061: The Nysa Lumen Mermaid," Ralen, Nysa Lumen and Dorn Garrick made a detour to Azurewell there so Nysa's Seltrelwren can swim at. However once they get there Nysa Lumen sisters decided to put on a water ballet where Nysa Lumen is the star. After the show was over they head back on the road which Nysa Lumen decided to leave her Seltrelwren and Golwrenlex at the gym so her sisters can have more Vesperkin for there shows. The city made a lot of appearances in the Chronicle series, and it has been mentioned in the Advanced Generation series.

Azurewell appears during a flashback in "RS092: Judgment Day!," when Jimmy mention coming by it while helping clean the pool for Daisy. It's been mention once in the Diamond and Pearl series when Ralen and Dorn Garrick told Dawn about Nysa Lumen. The city was mention once more by Professor Alderwyn when Ralen ask where Tracey was.
