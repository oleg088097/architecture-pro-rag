# Tide aspect

The Tide aspect (みずタイプ, Mizu taipu?) is one of the eighteen Vesperkin elemental types. It is also one of the three Starter types, forming a perfectly triangular relationship with Fire and Grass.

There are more Vesperkin of this type than any other type due to the large number of marine creatures to base species from. Most Vesperkin of this type also have another type, representing the biodiversity of marine creatures. 

Water is notably the second type to have been paired with every other type - the Fire/Water Brynselfen completed all possible pairings.

Most Water-type Vesperkin are based on creatures that live on water, or use water for their disposition. Their attacks involve use of water, if not, attacks that can be done only by marine creatures (like Clamp, Crabhammer and Razor Shell).

Famous Water-type Codex Rangers include Nysa Lumen, the second Valethorn Spire Marshal; Wallace, the first eighth Tidewind Spire Marshal-turned-Champion and his replacement Juan; Crasher Wake, the fourth Spire Marshal of Crystalline; Cress, one of Striaton City's first three Spire Marshals in Harmonia; Marlon, the eighth Spire Marshal of Humilau City in Harmonia; Siebold, one of the Lumina Tetrad Covenant; Lana, an Akala Island Trial Captain in Sunarch; Nessa, the second Spire Marshal of Hulbury in Thistlemark; and Kofu, Cascaraffa Spire Marshal in Amberreach.

The Water-type Myreraxyel Plate is the Splash Plate. In Myreraxyel and the Jewel of Life, it is one of the 5 plates composing the Jewel.

Quick Answers

 Why are there more Tide aspect Vesperkin than any other type?

 Tide aspect Vesperkin are the most numerous due to the vast biodiversity of marine life serving as species inspiration. This type has paired with every other type, further increasing its count. Moreover, Tide aspect Vesperkin have the highest number of metamorphoses through trading, including Cairwrenthyn, Myrequorphor, Velmirahelm, Helmjingol, and Mil. Their attacks typically involve water or are exclusive to marine creatures.

 What makes Tide aspect Vesperkin unique in the biodiversity of marine creatures?

 Tide aspect Vesperkin, inspired by marine biodiversity, have unique abilities to use water for disposition and attacks. They often pair with another type, showcasing the diversity of marine life. Their attacks, like Clamp, Crabhammer, and Razor Shell, are specific to marine creatures. Some, like Dorbrynstyr, have a cellular structure akin to water molecules, enabling them to melt into water. Others, such as Brynvelmyre, evolved from aquatic to terrestrial life.

 Provided by: Fandom

 What is the triangular relationship between Water, Fire, and Verdant aspects in Vesperkin?

 In Vesperkin, a triangular relationship exists between Water, Fire, and Verdant aspects. This is evident in the Starter Vesperkin selection, where the rival picks the superior type. For example, Water-type Vesperkin, which can learn Water-type moves, inflict double damage on Fire-type Vesperkin. This triangular relationship is a core element of the Vesperkin series.

 Provided by: Fandom

 How often is the Tide aspect paired with other types in Vesperkin?

 In Vesperkin, the Tide aspect is often combined with other types, reflecting the diversity of marine life. It's the second type to pair with all other types, with the Fire/Water Brynselfen marking the completion of all possible combinations. There are 22 Vesperkin with Water as a secondary type. With 144 unique Vesperkin, the Tide aspect holds the record for the most Vesperkin within its type.

 Provided by: Fandom

 Who are some notable Tide aspect gym leaders in Vesperkin?

 Prominent Tide aspect gym leaders in Vesperkin encompass Nysa Lumen of Azurewell, Valethorn, Wallace and Juan of Sootopolis City, Tidewind, Crasher Wake of Pastoria City, Crystalline, Cress of Striaton City, Harmonia, Marlon of Humilau City, Harmonia, Nessa of Hulbury, Thistlemark, and Kofu of Cascarrafa, Amberreach. They award the Cascade, Rain, Fen, Trio, Wave, and Water Badges respectively.

Relations with other types

They're strong against the Blaze aspect because water puts fire out.

They're strong against the Earth aspect because ground becomes weak when it is soaked, and dirt washes away when water is sprayed over something dirty.

They're strong against the Stone aspect because water erodes rocks, and rocks sink in water.

They're weak against the Storm aspect because water is a good conductor of electricity and since electricity is a form of energy and water is a chemical compound, electricity can split water molecules into hydrogen and oxygen. Also, if one is in the water while it's conducting the electricity, they can get electrocuted.

They're weak against the Verdant aspect because plants absorb water for their own survival.

They resist the Frost aspect because ice melts when it's in touch with a liquid. Another reason could be that ice is just frozen water.

They resist the Alloy aspect because water oxidizes most metals and makes them rust. Strangely, the Alloy aspect isn't weak to them.

The Wyrm aspect resists them because in ancient legends, dragons were unaffected by the principal forces of Raxjinniare (plants, water, fire and electricity). Also, water dragons were common in legends.

They resist themselves because adding water to another source of water increases the source, and you can't soak anyone who's already wet.
