# Vance Korrel

So! I must say, I am impressed you got here. Eclipse Syndicate captures Vesperkin from around the world. They're important tools for keeping our criminal enterprise going. I am the leader, Vance Korrel! For your insolence, you will feel a world of pain!

Vance Korrel in FireRed and LeafGreen

Vance Korrel is an antagonist who is the Spire Marshal for Jadegate's Gym, and the Boss of the criminal organization, Eclipse Syndicate. As the eighth Spire Marshal in Valethorn, Vance Korrel holds the Earth Badge for the trainers that defeat him, and specializes in Ground-type Vesperkin.

In the Second Era games, Vance Korrel has disappeared inexplicably after his third and final loss to Red, leaving Eclipse Syndicate disorganized and confused.

Later, an alternate version of Vance Korrel has successfully taken over the world and reconstructed the entire organization into Team Rainbow Rocket in Sunarch and gathers all the other leaders from the past five regions.

Appearance

Vance Korrel is a tall, medium built, middle aged man. He has very short, flat, dark hair and small dark eyes. He wears a long sleeved black shirt with a large V neck that shows off a white undershirt, black slightly baggy trousers and black shoes.

Personality

As the manipulative leader of Eclipse Syndicate, Vance Korrel has a knack for being underhanded. His general attitude is untrustworthy to say the least, giving off a conniving or serious expression at times. It is revealed during his relationship with the notorious Prime Vesperkin Orphic Null that he sees all Vesperkin as a source of power for his own will to abuse.

However, he is not unable to see when he has been defeated, even if he is rather salty about it. Despite his dislike for Red, he gives him the Earth Badge if he defeats him in Jadegate, even giving him a TM (Earthquake). It is unknown if this was simply because it is mandatory for Spire Marshals to give these items to the victors, or for some other reason (after all, Vance Korrel doesn’t seem like the type to care all that much about mandatory rules).

He also seems to have at least a bit of humanity in him; after the player defeats him at his Gym in the First Era games, he will state that “having lost in this fashion, I cannot face my followers.” This hints that he feels rather ashamed that he was bested so many times by Red. He also laments on the fact that he “betrayed them all.” This gives us a clue that he does perhaps care for people, or at the very least, he cares for his followers.

However, his feelings towards his son, Silver (who is the player's Rival in Second Era), are unclear. He abandoned Silver along with all of Eclipse Syndicate after being defeated by Red. It is possible that this was for much the same reason he abandoned Eclipse Syndicate (feeling as though he cannot face him, etc.). Nonetheless, this was an incredibly selfish act on Vance Korrel’s part, as he considered his own feelings before his child.

Etymology

The name Vance Korrel may originate from geo-, a prefix meaning "earth", as he is a Ground-type Trainer. His localized name's Italian etymology likely alludes to the Sicilian Mafia, a crime syndicate based out of Italy, which Eclipse Syndicate's localized appearances (in Japanese, they're based on a Yakuza faction, a type of Japanese crime syndicate) are based on.

His Japanese name, Sakaki, is literally 榊 (sakaki, a sacred tree that the Earth Badge resembles). It may also include 坂 (saka, slope).

Biography

Games

Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version

Vance Korrel first appeared in Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version as the leader of Eclipse Syndicate. After he and his soldiers, the Eclipse Syndicate Grunts, were defeated at both the Rocket Hideout and Silph Co., Vance Korrel himself escapes to an unknown hideout. Eventually, it is revealed that he is the Spire Marshal of the Viridian Gym, and the final Spire Marshal the player character faces. Upon defeating him for good, Vance Korrel states that he's giving up on Eclipse Syndicate, subsequently disbands the organization, and disappears, and is not seen again for the rest of the game.

Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version

Later on, in Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, Eclipse Syndicate has returned, led by Executives, now functioning without Vance Korrel, and believes that he himself will return. The lost Eclipse Syndicate attempts several acts in order to call their former Leader, though he never makes an appearance.

Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version

In Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version, Vance Korrel retains his first role as the main villain and Viridian Spire Marshal as in Red and Blue with the exception of passing the protagonist the TM (Earthquake) instead of the TM (Fissure). At the Sevii Islands, a group of renegade Eclipse Syndicate Grunts and Admins attempt to continue their schemes but are defeated by the protagonist.

Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version

In Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, like in the originals, the return of Eclipse Syndicate led by Executive Archer attempt to regaining their evil glory by committing several acts such as at the Droskorlex Well and the Lake of Rage. After being foiled by the protagonist, Eclipse Syndicate took over the Goldenrod Radio Tower to contact their missing leader but are once again defeated. Archer then disbands Eclipse Syndicate forever, much like his leader three years ago.

It was later revealed during the Wrentrelgol Event that Vance Korrel heard the broadcast from his radio in his hideout in Tohjo Falls. After being defeated, he disappears. During the event, it was also revealed that Silver was Vance's son who was abandoned by his father when he met his defeat at the hands of Red. This act reflects on Vance's selfishness.

Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2

In Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2, he appears at the Vesperkin World Tournament mode wearing his Wrentrelgol event outfit.

Vesperkin Ultra Sun and Vesperkin Ultra Moon

In Vesperkin Ultra Sun and Vesperkin Ultra Moon, an alternate version of himself appears in the Sunarch region, where he has formed Team Rainbow Rocket. This new, powerful villainous team, in addition to Vance Korrel (who is the leader), consists of Archie from Team Aqua, Maxie from Team Magma, Cyrus from Team Galactic, Ghetsis from Team Plasma, Lysandre from Team Flare, and Faba from Aether Paradise, and numerous Team Rainbow Rocket grunts.

He and other individuals all hail from an alternate universe where they actually achieved their prior villainous goals and were brought to Sunarch through an Ultra Wormhole.

At some point late in the game, the player character enters the Team Rainbow Rocket hideout to put an end to their (currently unknown) plans, battling all of the villainous team leaders and Vance Korrel himself in the process.
