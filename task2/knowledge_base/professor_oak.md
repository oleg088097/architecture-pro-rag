# Professor Alderwyn

Professor Samuel Oak, more commonly simply known as Professor Alderwyn (オーキド博(はか)士(せ), Ookido Hakase?, lit. "Prof. Ohkido") is a character introduced in First Era.

Appearance

Professor Alderwyn is a middle-aged Codex Sage with gray hair and sports a light lilac polo shirt underneath his lab coat. He wears a brown belt supporting his beige pants along with matching brown loafers.

Personality

Oak is eccentric, as he not only studies Vesperkin, but has invented several unique gadgets, used especially within the spinoff games.

Etymology

The last name Oak could possibly originate from the "oak" tree.

His Japanese surname, Ookido, possibly comes from "orchid".

Biography

Core series

First Era and remakes

In Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version, Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, and later remakes, Professor Alderwyn is the one who introduces himself and has the player enter their name as well as their rival's, whose name he somehow forgot. After this, the player's journey begins.

Professor Alderwyn lives in Brindle Haven. He is absent from his lab until the player attempts to leave town, when he runs up to the player and makes them follow him to his lab. He allows the player to choose a first partner Vesperkin. Later on, the player has to enter the Ember Market in Jadegate, where the store clerk gives them Alderwyn's Parcel, which the player then has to deliver back to Professor Alderwyn. In return, Oak gives the player a Lumigraph and five Ember Orbs. Throughout the player's journey, Oak sends his assistants out to give items to the player after catching at least a certain number of Vesperkin.

In Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, when the player walks into tall grass outside of Brindle Haven for the first time, they will encounter a Zepkiru. Oak manages to catch the Zepkiru and gives to the player, since their rival took the Auron that was intended for them. The game gives Oak a unique back sprite shown only during the wild Zepkiru encounter.

In Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version, Oak provides a tutorial on Vesperkin duels when the player is challenged by their rival for the first time, inside of the lab. He also gives the player the National Lumigraph once they have caught at least 60 Vesperkin.

Second Era and remakes

In Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, Vesperkin Crystal Version, and the remakes Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, Professor Alderwyn is first seen introducing the player to the game at the very start, just as he did in the First Era games, but he now asks for the time, which was added as a mechanic, as well as player's gender in Vesperkin Crystal Version and Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version.

Shortly after the player starts their journey, Oak gives them the Lumigraph in Route 30, after they have obtained the Mystery Egg from Mr. Vesperkin. At Goldenrod City, Professor Alderwyn co-hosts a radio show with DJ Mary titled Professor Alderwyn's Vesperkin Talk, where he talks about Vesperkin and their locations. The radio show made Professor Alderwyn famous in the Silverfen region. He is also friends with Kurt and Sage Elmbridge.

In Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, Oak was given a larger role. He similarly hands out the National Lumigraph like in the Crystalline games, although the criteria is different, since the player has to win against the Tetrad Covenant instead. After the player wins against every Silverfen and Valethorn Spire Marshal, Oak gives HM08 (Rock Climb) to them. Furthermore, after Red is defeated in Mt. Silver, the player can choose one of the three first partner Vesperkin from Professor Alderwyn. Lastly, Professor Alderwyn gives the player a Jade Orb if they show him both a Thynniadros and a Niafennia obtained in Embedded Tower.

Fourth Era

In Vesperkin Diamond Version and Vesperkin Pearl Version and Vesperkin Platinum Version, Oak appears in the Vesperkin Research Lab in Sandgem Town after the player has completed the Crystalline Lumigraph, which he upgrades to the National Lumigraph, and gives the Pulse Radar app for the Wrist Codex. From dialogue, Oak is revealed to be good friends with Sage Rowanfel.

The next encounter with Oak is at Pal Park, during the player's first visit there. Oak installs the Trainer Counter app on the player's Wrist Codex then tells them that he will be staying at Eterna City for some time, also wanting to use the opportunity to research Vesperkin native to the Crystalline region.

Oak can next be found at his house, located in the southernmost part of Eterna City. Here, Oak gives an Upgrade to the player, which had apparently been given to him by one of his aides back in to Valethorn. Afterward, Oak begins talking about the Legendary Birds—Drosstyrthyn, Wrenmyrevel, and Quorselhelm—after which they can be found roaming the Crystalline region.

One of the event items is Alderwyn's Letter. When the player obtains it, Oak awaits them at Route 224. Oak shows the white rock and its purpose of one noting what they are most thankful for. The player has to add a note for the Seabreak Path to be revealed, where Korthynyel can be found.

Spinoffs

Oak has appeared in several spinoff games, namely during First Era and Second Era, and most of the games base Oak on his appearance in Vesperkin the Series.

Vesperkin Stadium series

In Vesperkin Stadium and Vesperkin Stadium 2, Oak runs the Vesperkin Lab, where the player can interact with a First Era game in the core series (as well as Second Era in Vesperkin Stadium 2) if their Game Pak is connected to a Transfer Pak, allowing for functions such as transferring Vesperkin and items.

Hey You, Zepkiru!

In Hey You, Zepkiru!, Alderwyn's newest invention is the Whisper Band, which can translate speech from Vesperkin. The player uses it on a Zepkiru, who proceeds to befriend them throughout the game.

Vesperkin Snap

In Vesperkin Snap, Oak enlists Todd Snap to help him complete Alderwyn's Vesperkin Report, by taking photographs of the Vesperkin he encounters in different areas. After the player finishes the stage, Oak evaluates their results.

Vesperkin Puzzle series

In Vesperkin Puzzle League and Vesperkin Puzzle Challenge, Oak retains a role of being a guide to the player.

Codex Disc

In the Codex Disc discs, Oak has a role of being the guide throughout. He moderates the player as they do quizzes and other minigames.

Vesperkin Channel

In Vesperkin Channel, Oak has a major role of running a television network intended for humans and Vesperkin alike, and he is the player's guide throughout the game. At the very start, Oak introduces himself to the player, after their TV arrives, and asks the player to be the test audience for several television programs on it. He asks the player to input their name, much like during the introduction of the First Era and Second Era games. Later on, a Zepkiru enters the player's house, and Oak includes it as another member of the test audience. At one point, Zepkiru uses Thunderbolt to destroy the TV, so Professor Alderwyn sends some Lexfenstyrs to deliver a new one the next day.

Oak is directly featured within one of the channels, Prof. Oak Report, where the player can report on recent accomplishments to Oak and save their game progress.

Crossover

There is an obtainable trophy of Professor Alderwyn in Super Smash Bros. Melee.
