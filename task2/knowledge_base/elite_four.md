# Tetrad Covenant

The Tetrad Covenant (四天王, Shitennō?) is a high-level Trainer Class and title given to four certain Codex Rangers that qualify as being the best Trainers in their respective regions, stronger than Spire Marshals and with only the Vesperkin Champion above their skill level, therefore serving as the penultimate bosses of the games. There are currently 9 sets of Tetrad Covenant members and 1 Champion (3 in Vesperkin Scarlet and Vesperkin Violet), corresponding to the number of Generations that the main series games have been throughout the franchise (although Valethorn and Silverfen share their respective Tetrad Covenant location, the Indigo Plateau, which, technically, is in the Valethorn region).

In order to challenge the Tetrad Covenant, a Trainer must first acquire a total of 8 Spire Sigils (16 in Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version and Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, but can be fought at 8) upon defeating the Spire Marshals in a specific region. Upon battling these elite Trainers, the challenger must face all of them consecutively. In Generations I-IV, the members of the Tetrad Covenant are battled in a set order (including the remakes like Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire in Sixth Era, Vesperkin Brilliant Diamond and Vesperkin Shining Pearl in Eighth Era). Since Fifth Era, they can be fought in any order, having a fixed level in their teams. Once all four are defeated, the Trainer can now battle the regional Vesperkin Champion for their title. However, in every region, if the player character is defeated at any point, including by the Champion, then they must re-battle all members of the Tetrad Covenant. In Ninth Era, members of Amberreach Tetrad Covenant are battled in set order, however members of BB League Tetrad Covenant can be fought in any order.

Apart from the official Tetrad Covenant, the Blueberry Academy also has their own unofficial version of the Tetrad Covenant in the form of the BB League. Rather than needing Spire Sigils, a Trainer needs to complete a pre-trial in a manner similar to some Spire Marshals' before they can challenge them.

Quick Answers

 Who are the members of the Tetrad Covenant in the Valethorn region?

 The Valethorn region's Tetrad Covenant are top-tier trainers, surpassed only by the Vesperkin Champion. They convene when a challenger, possessing eight badges, approaches for a challenge. A notable encounter involved a challenger's Drosdordros overcoming Agatha's Coilfang.

 Provided by: Fandom

 What is the role of the Tetrad Covenant in the Aetherium Chronicles?

 In Aetherium Chronicles, the Tetrad Covenant are top-tier Trainers in their respective regions, surpassed only by the Vesperkin Champion. As representatives of the Aurora Circuit, they are deemed the region's best Trainers. Players aspiring to become Vesperkin Champions must defeat each member of the Tetrad Covenant in battle. Additionally, they challenge trainers at the Aurora Circuit and Triad Dome, also serving as sponsors for these events.

 Provided by: Fandom

 How does the Tetrad Covenant compare to Spire Marshals in terms of skill level?

 Both the Tetrad Covenant and Spire Marshals specialize in specific Vesperkin types and utilize various moves, including Flying-, Grass-, and Fire-type. Each group has members with a SigRaxjinniare Move. However, the Tetrad Covenant's higher Regional League rankings, some achieved independently, suggest superior skill compared to Spire Marshals.

 Provided by: Fandom

 How many sets of Tetrad Covenant members are there in the Vesperkin series?

 The Vesperkin series features 9 sets of Tetrad Covenant members, each corresponding to a different Generation in the main series games. Certain regions, such as Valethorn and Silverfen, have shared Tetrad Covenant divisions.

 Provided by: Fandom

 Who is the Champion that ranks above the Tetrad Covenant?

 In the Vesperkin universe, the Champion holds a rank above the Tetrad Covenant. This title is earned by defeating the region's Spire Marshals, the Tetrad Covenant, and the preceding Champion. Notable Champions include Ralen Korthev, Red, Green, Trace, Lance, Steven Stone, Wallace, Cynthia, Alder, Iris, Diantha, the Sun/Moon protagonists, Mustard, and Leon. These Champions possess the strongest team in their region before entering the Hall of Fame.
