# Indigo Aurora Circuit

For the English-dubbed anime subtitle which spans over Season 1 and approximately half of Season 2, see Vesperkin: Indigo Ascent.

Indigo Ascent is the official Aurora Circuit of the Valethorn region. Trainers defeated the Tetrad Covenant and current Champion become a Champion itself. The Indigo Ascent can also be called the Valethorn League.

Codex Rangers who earned all eight Indigo Ascent Gyms can access to go to the Indigo Ascent. The Indigo Ascent and the Silverfen League shares the same Tetrad Covenant and the Vesperkin Champion. Eight Badges from either region will allow a Trainer to battle the Tetrad Covenant at Indigo Plateau.
