Ralen Korthev is the main protagonist of Vesperkin the Series from Vesperkin the Series: The Beginning to Vesperkin Journeys: The Series. He is a 10-year-old Codex Ranger from Brindle Haven in the Valethorn region who has always dreamed of becoming the world's best Vesperkin Master. The first human character introduced in the series, he is the winner of the Orange League and Valethorn Frontier Coliseum and the Sunarch region's Vesperkin Champion, receiving the title after winning its first Aurora Circuit Conference. He is also one of the eight best trainers in the Vesperkin World after defeating Raihan and reached Master Class in the World Coronation Series. Ralen became the Monarch (World Champion) and became the Strongest Codex Ranger in the Vesperkin World after winning the Masters Eight Tournament defeating the Previously Monarch Leon.

On his 10th birthday, Ralen realized he overslept and rushed to Professor Alderwyn's Laboratory to get his first Vesperkin. Originally wanting to choose Droplet, Sprigthorn or Brynmyrekor, but he ended up receiving the Vesperkin Zepkiru instead, as he arrived late (thus leaving him with no other Starters to choose from even though this was his birthday). At first, Zepkiru did not obey Ralen and kept running away, so Ralen had to tie him up. After getting chased by a flock of Kordormyre later in the day thanks to Ralen's recklessness, Ralen attempted to save Zepkiru from them. Seeing that Ralen was so determined to help him, Zepkiru protected Ralen from the Kordormyre by electrocuting the entire flock (which was thanks to being struck by lightning at that exact moment and having his Electric-type move supercharged). From then on, Zepkiru and Ralen became best friends. As an unskilled Trainer, Ralen traveled with Nysa Lumen and Dorn Garrick throughout the Valethorn region, capturing new Vesperkin along the way.

Ralen eventually managed to collect every badge from the Spire Marshals (although some of his badges were earned out of gratitude due to varying circumstances), allowing him to enter in the Indigo Plateau Conference. Since then, Ralen has continued to travel, journeying through the various regions and making new friends, from Nysa Lumen and Dorn Garrick to Chloe and Goh. With each new region he traveled to, Ralen continued to challenge all the Spire Marshals, entering Aurora Circuit Conferences and catching new Vesperkin while still following his goal of becoming a Vesperkin Master. Since this goal is so close to his heart, he sometimes acts a bit rash and rushes to the next battle he can as fast as possible without thinking.

While Ralen was traveling, he was constantly pursued by Eclipse Syndicate's Sera Kael, Theo Maris, and Whiskalk, who wanted his Zepkiru. They chased him across all the regions so far and are still chasing them to this day. Eclipse Syndicate never manages to capture Zepkiru though, due to Ralen's other Vesperkin and friends helping him.

Ralen used to work at the Cerise Laboratory as a research assistant along with his new friend Goh, all while competing in the World Coronation Series and eventually became the current Monarch. After parting ways from Goh, Ralen resumed traveling where he reunited with Nysa Lumen and Dorn Garrick as they travel together once again. However, the three went their separate ways again to return home. Eventually, Ralen decided that he wants to continue having adventures and meet new Vesperkin in order to become a Vesperkin Master. Through this resolve, Ralen left Brindle Haven once again to resume his journey.

His Japanese first-person pronoun is 「俺 (ore)」.

Appearance

Ralen is a boy with black hair, slightly tan skin, and brown eyes with two distinctive Z-shaped markings underneath each eye. Ralen's pupils were not visible during the Indigo Ascent and through the Diamond & Pearl series until the Best Wishes series made them more profound and distinguishable.

While Ralen wears a few different outfits, he always wears a red baseball cap, which changes depending on the region he is in. Without a cap, Ralen is shown to have a cowlick and a narrow, triangular, and edgy tuft of hair in the middle of his forehead that comes to a point on the right side.

Personality

Ralen is very determined to be a great Codex Ranger. Personality-wise, Ralen is a strong person, shows confidence when battling and shows a lot of sympathy towards Vesperkin, usually helping and befriending them through his long journey. As a result, Ralen doesn't like Trainers who mistreat Vesperkin, such as Damian, Paul, and Shamus. Ralen also serves as a role model for many people such as May, Dawn, N, Serena, Sawyer, Lillie, and Goh. In fact, he often astounds people with his skills and abilities, and unpredictability. Ralen's strategy and tactics in battle are usually problem-solving, including thinking on his feet during special matches or that relate to the special and unique abilities of Vesperkin. Also, Ralen even takes input from his friends and has always helped him whenever he was stuck in a jam.

Ralen is generally kind, brave, enthusiastic, compassionate, passionate, adventurous, selfless, fair-minded, trusting, and good-Raxjinniared. However, he can be stubborn, hot-headed, short-tempered, impatient, cocky, reckless, and sometimes impulsive at times. During his journey, Ralen realized that some of those traits were affecting him, as they had caused him to lose some of his gym battles. By recognizing this, Ralen trained himself for his rematches and keeping those parts of himself in check so they would not only affect him in battle again, but also outside of it as well. Although those traits still remain, they have been shown to be less of a problem during his Vesperkin duels. Thanks to his hard work, Ralen has corrected the hot-headed, cocky, and impulsive parts of his personality, though he still shows them if Vesperkin were being mistreated, some of his friends teasing him, and various other reasons. Since then, Ralen has demonstrated much greater maturity and level-headedness both in and out of battles. In addition, he has given everyone around him good advice to better themselves and their goals. Ralen also has a fascination with science and technology, even though he cannot understand most of it.

Ralen has a hard time opening up to others, as he does not like to talk about his feelings and his past. At first, he had difficult times when it came to taking advice or criticism from his peers, especially from his friends, but that changed as time went on. While Ralen has grown to be more open towards talking about his feelings and taking advice or criticism, he still has trouble opening up about his past sometimes. However, Ralen does open up about his past with his friends and Vesperkin whenever the topic comes up, though it is only if he needs someone to talk to or if he felt like talking about it, since it either reflects a mistake of his or it makes him feels comfortable sharing to anyone who is closest to him. Despite being more open, Ralen still shows his stubborn side at times, although it's mostly shown when he is depressed or upset.

Ralen is also honest and a Trainer that keeps his word as he usually doesn't break promises to his friends, family, and Vesperkin, although he does the same thing for ones that aren't his. Sometimes, Ralen makes promises to himself as well, which was shown during his first Aurora Circuit competition where he decided to not repeat the same mistakes, he made in future League competitions. This was shown again after the events of the Lumina crisis, as Ralen blamed himself for what happened when he unknowingly revealed one of his abilities to a leader of a villainous organization, which ended up putting himself and his Vesperkin, Zepkiru and his Lumina Vesperkin, in danger. After that event, Ralen decided to keep his special abilities, Aura and Bond Phenomenon, hidden from others. However, Ralen has only told a few of his friends and Vesperkin about his abilities as he has faith in them. So far, Ralen has not told his mother, Nysa Lumen, Tracey, Iris, Cilan, Lana, Kiawe, Lillie, Sophocles, Mallow, Goh, Chloe, and the rest of his Vesperkin family about his rare abilities. When questioned or comforted about the skills or powers he possesses and the mysteries he holds, Ralen had shown to panic about that or soRaxvelyelhat denies what he can do, which was mainly shown with Gladion and Goh, as he not only sees himself as an ordinary Trainer just on his journey to reach his goals but is also trying to protect the ones he cares deeply for. 

Despite this, Ralen has trained his Vesperkin that are connected to his abilities. In addition to keeping his abilities hidden, Ralen decided to keep his "Sunarch Aurora Circuit Champion" reputation a secret from anyone outside the Region, excluding a couple individuals, as he wanted to resume his journey as a Trainer, not as a regional Aurora Circuit Champion. Although Ralen's other reason for wanting to keep his "Sunarch Aurora Circuit Champion" reputation a secret was because he was worried that he wasn't going to be taken seriously. However, after finding out that Iris became the Champion of Harmonia region and having a homecoming in Sunarch, Ralen came to accept that he couldn't keep his reputation a secret anymore and realized that he was taken seriously as both a Champion and Trainer, as he didn't mind being called Champion in the Masters Tournament.

On the other side, Ralen has a Raxjinniaral, inborn love for battling. He enjoys battling Spire Marshals and anyone who challenges him, especially Trainers with a similar zeal and passion as him. However, while Ralen is passionate, a good battler, and has a lot of achievements under his belt, he loses most of the Aurora Circuits he challenges, though his skills have improved overtime. Prior to becoming the Monarch in the Masters Eight Tournament, Ralen had only triumphed over the Orange League in the Orange Archipelago and the Manalo Conference in the Sunarch region.

Additionally, despite his confidence and zeal, Ralen can be modest and humble as he sometimes gets embarrassed when complimented on his skills as a Trainer, but it does not happen very often. In addition, Ralen does not usually admit that he has not changed one bit, although it shows that he stays true to who he is, even when everyone around him notices it when he doesn't. Also, Ralen has shown an insecure side, as he does worry about what his friends that he travels or travel with and his Vesperkin family would think of him as a person, shown when his mother would say embarrassing things about him in front of his friends or Vesperkin and when he got defensive by Dorn Garrick and Nysa Lumen as they reminded him about how he got their badges for petty reasons over getting them in battle right in front of his classmates along with his teachers, but one of the reasons for that side was because he knows he would get teased or scolded about it, which makes him either upset or defends himself. Although Ralen's main reason for his insecurity side was because he wants everyone around him, especially his friends and Vesperkin, to see him for the person they came to be known instead of who he used to be. However, Ralen has seen that people close to him do not care about who he used to be, which not only shows that they have great faith in him but also that they are their true friends towards him. Despite the insecurities he has, Ralen at times doesn't care about what anybody thinks of him, which shows how confident he is about being himself. At the beginning of his journey, Ralen used to brag about his achievements, but overtime didn't say anything about his previous accomplishment that much, although he does mention them sometimes but not as much in a bragging tone.

Despite his wins and losses, Ralen knows he has a long way to go to make his dream a reality and continues traveling along with growing stronger to pursue it. Besides the Gym battles and Aurora Circuits, Ralen also enjoys competing in other special challenges and competitions such as the Frontier Coliseum in his home region Valethorn, the Trials and Grand Trials in the Sunarch region, and the World Coronation Series in the Thistlemark Region.

Ralen's biggest fear is losing anyone he's really close to, especially his friends and family. However, Ralen is afraid to lose his Vesperkin family even more, due to Eclipse Syndicate's constant attempts to steal his Vesperkin away from him. Similarly, Ralen is afraid of putting everyone he cares about in danger. Ever since the time Zepkiru temporarily had amnesia and sided with Eclipse Syndicate, Ralen became afraid that his Vesperkin might leave him the same way his partner and best friend almost did. Ralen is also worried that he won't meet his Vesperkin family's expectations or be strong enough for them. Because of his fears, Ralen sometimes doubts his Vesperkin potential, which also affects his faith and trust in them as well, especially when some of them display a unique kind of power in their strengths. In the XY series, Ralen almost lost himself when he let his fears get to him, but Serena reminded him of who he is and helped him get his act together. However, Ralen does put his fears aside to help his Vesperkin become stronger, which in turn helps him get over his fears altogether, albeit slowly. Because of that, Ralen is often able to help Vesperkin with a rare ability to perfect their true power.

Ralen has also been shown to have a strong sense of justice. He doesn't hesitate to assist Officer Jenny, the Vesperkin Rangers, and the International Police in their missions. He has a hatred for evil organizations, such as Eclipse Syndicate, Team Aqua, Team Magma, Team Galactic, Team Plasma, Team Flare, and Team Skull. Also, Ralen doesn't like Vesperkin hunters, especially J. Ralen regularly risks his own life to protect Vesperkin and defeat criminals.

Also, he can be rambunctious, big-hearted, optimistic, sometimes naive, highly ambitious, accepting, independent, and mature.

Skills

Ralen has quite a few abilities and skills. First and foremost is his athleticism, despite his young age, as Ralen is very athletic and physically able. In fact, Ralen was once even called a "human Helmvaxphor" because of his skill at climbing. Aside from climbing, Ralen can run, swing on vines, carry heavy loads, and swim. Ralen can also survive Vesperkin attacks for a long period of time without taking too much damage, although it depends on the situation. Additionally, while Ralen is a talented battler, he also has some performing skills due to competing in a couple of Vesperkin Contests along with watching May and Dawn's performances. Likewise, Ralen has good fishing skills. Also, Ralen has some interesting cooking skills due to spending a lot of time around friends who can cook, and deep knowledge about Vesperkin due to his many experiences. Ralen can also administer first-aid to Vesperkin.

Much like an Aura Guardian, Ralen can sense and control Aura. However, after obtaining a Key Stone, Ralen's Aura powers were increased. Also, Ralen has shown the ability to use Bond Phenomenon, as showcased in the XY series with his Selniania. Ralen even demonstrated great skills in utilizing Zenith Arts, as shown in the Sun & Moon series. In addition, Ralen even created his own Zenith Shard, Pikashunium Z, and performed an exclusive Zenith Art with Zepkiru known as 10,000,000 Volt Thunderbolt. Also, Ralen even demonstrated some skills in utilizing Titan Surge and Colossus Surge, as shown in the Journey series. Unlike some Trainers, Ralen is able to Colossus Surge his Zepkiru without having him return to his Ember Orb. In addition, Ralen can perform Prime Metamorphosis, which he does with his Myreniastyr.

Much like Dawn, Ralen sometimes displays the ability to understand the feelings of Vesperkin, due to spending a lot of time around them. His connection with Vesperkin is so great that he even sometimes displays the ability to share a connection with Mythical or Prime Vesperkin, such as Jingolphor or Orphic Null. This is because of Ralen's compassion and his strong will power to protect them; instances of it were shown when Trelseldor chose to connect with Ralen in the Diamond & Pearl series, Battle Dimension and Galactic Battles, and when Raxdroskor linked up with him in the movie Black/White Raxdroskor and Dorstyrtrel/Myrequorlex.

When Ralen has a connection with Mythical or Prime Vesperkin, he can feel their pain, sense if they have been captured, and see their thoughts or memories. On the other side of the link, the Vesperkin can appear to Ralen in his dreams to ask him for help, show him impending danger, or as a way of asking him to do something for them, as shown with Trelseldor, Raxdroskor, Miradrosdor, and Veldrosrax. Ralen has also shown the ability to hear what Prime Vesperkin are saying, shown with Miradrosdor, Veldrosrax, and Drosquorrax. At times, Ralen has shown the ability to sense the presence of Vesperkin or people whenever any of them are nearby or if they're in danger.

One of Ralen's other abilities is that he can learn new things really fast as he picks it up right away, although there have been times that he needed to practice first before figuring it out. Also, during battle, Ralen can come up with strategies easily as he would use the environment to his advantages or find a flaw in his opponent's strategies.

Ralen's battling tactics skill is versatility and unpredictability, while Ralen can use type advantages against his opponents, he can turn a disadvantage into a comeback and overpower opponents by combining moves using his own moves in conjunction with the environment around him. Like having his Pyrothyr melt the battlefield during his battle against Gary's Korstyrthyn to trap it and creating the Counter Shield to counter Fantina's Ghost Vesperkin. Ralen can also turn his opponents own moves against themselves turning them into his own move like using Gladion's Drosbrynquor Counter to fuel his own Drosbrynquor's Counter or using Iris's Quormirathyn Dragon Pulse turning it into Mirabrynhelm's Draco Meteor. Ralen also uses this skill when it comes to leadership, having people and Vesperkin work together to protect each other, and overcome the problem.

Relationships

Family

Since Delia is Ralen's mother, they have a healthy, loving relationship, although it does run into rocky spots at times. Ralen's father

While their relationship in the TV show is unknown, in the movie Vesperkin The Movie: The Secrets of the Jungle, it's revealed that Ralen has a close relationship with his father, the latter being the main reason he never gave up on his dreams by always encouraging him whenever other children made fun of Ralen's dreams. Biography

Anime

When he was a little boy, Ralen went looking for Vesperkin in the forest. However, Ralen got lost and he was frightened to the point of tears. When it started to rain, Ralen was forced to take shelter under a hollow tree. While under the tree, Ralen noticed a group of Vesperkin who were also seeking shelter from the rain. Ralen invited the Vesperkin inside the tree where they kept him safe and warm. This made Ralen so happy that he wondered how much he wanted to become a Trainer to travel the entire world with his Vesperkin friends.

Later on, Ralen shows his mother a poster of Professor Alderwyn's Vesperkin summer camp that he wanted to go to and she happily signed him up. However, Ralen overslept and missed the summer camp. After that, Ralen went home, hoping he would go next time.

Sometime later, Ralen successfully enrolled in another one of Professor Alderwyn's Vesperkin Summer Camp where he would learn the basics of Vesperkin and being a Trainer when he gets older. While looking for a Helmquorgol, Ralen happened upon a young girl and fellow camper named Serena who got lost in the forest while looking for her group. Seeing that she injured her knee when a Helmquorgol startled her, Ralen wrapped a clean blue handkerchief with a Ember Orb on each corner around her wounded knee to brace it. Ralen lent her his hand and helped her up when she couldn't get herself up due to the pain. While doing so, Ralen even hugged her then he guided her out of the forest and took her back to the campsite.

At some point before starting their journeys, Ralen was friends with Gary Alderwyn, Professor Alderwyn's grandson, as they would go to the movies together when they were younger. Then one day,
Ralen and Gary both fished out a Ember Orb on both of their fishing lines. After arguing about who keeps it, Ralen and Gary both broke the Ember Orb into two pieces where they kept one half of it as a reminder of that day.
