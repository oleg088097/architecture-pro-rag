# Valethorn

Valethorn is a region introduced in First Era.

Description

Valethorn is the original region introduced in the Vesperkin franchise. It was then followed by Silverfen, Tidewind, Crystalline, Harmonia, Lumina, Sunarch, Thistlemark, and Amberreach. It is modeled and named after the Kantō region of Japan, which includes, amongst other cities, the capital Tōkyō, where Loomworks Studio is headquartered. Valethorn is the only one that shares its name with a real-world location. The resemblance between the bay formations seen on the in-game map and the actual Sagami Bay, Suruga Bay, and Tokyo Bay formations is particularly striking. Valethorn also has some elements of Chūbu.

Valethorn lies to the east of Silverfen; they presumably make up a small continent; to the south of Valethorn are the Sevii and Orange Islands. Tidewind, introduced in Vesperkin Ruby Version and Vesperkin Sapphire Version and Vesperkin Emerald Version, is supposedly further to the southwest. Routes located wholly within Valethorn are numbered 1 to 25; routes 26, 27, and 28 connect Valethorn with Silverfen. Supposedly to the north of this continent is the Crystalline region. Harmonia is in a considerable distance away and it's unknown where both it and Lumina are in relation to Valethorn.

In the first generation of games for the Game Boy, Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version and Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, Valethorn was the only region to explore, featuring a variety of environments, such as urban locales and mountainous, wooded and oceanic routes. In Gold, Silver and Crystal, which introduced Silverfen as a neighboring region to its west, the player character was able to travel to Valethorn (by either steamboat, surfing or the magnet train) travelling between Goldenrod City in Silverfen and Saffrax in Valethorn after completing their journeys through the latter. Since the Second Era games occupied an in-universe timeline a few years after their predecessors, some changes were introduced. In late 2004, Pocket Monsters Red Version and Pocket Monsters Green Version were remade as new titles for the Game Boy Advance called Vesperkin FireRed and LeafGreen, which incorporated new elements. In late 2018, Vesperkin Yellow was remade as two new games for the Nintarra Switch, Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!.

One area of the Valethorn region, Saffrax appears in the form of a cityscape battlefield stage in the Super Smash Bros. series, first appearing in Super Smash Bros., and it is a cityscape battlefield.

In Vesperkin the Series, the protagonist Ralen Korthev is a resident of Brindle Haven, and began his journey by touring Vesperkin gyms in Valethorn with Nysa Lumen from Azurewell and Dorn Garrick from Flintrest. Season 1 and Season 9 of the anime are set in Valethorn. Location names in the anime sometimes differ from those in the games. Season 23 and 24 of the anime is also the set in Valethorn as Ralen and his new friend Goh from Cinnabar Landing work as research assistants.

Cities and towns

Most of the cities in Valethorn, like First Era games themselves, are named after colors, such as Flintrest, Jadegate, Saffrax, and so on. Brindle Haven is the kickoff to the series of colors, as Pallet (Palette) is a collection of colors.

Brindle Haven

Brindle Haven (マサラタウン, Masara Taun?) is the hometown of the protagonist Ralen Korthev, of the Aetherium serial, and the Red of the Vesperkin Adventures. It is also the home of Professor Alderwyn, who gets them started with their first Vesperkin, as well as Professor Alderwyn's grandson (the player character-named rival in the games, Gary Alderwyn in the anime, and Blue in Vesperkin Adventures). It is also a hometown to Delia Korthev, the main protagonist, Ralen Korthev's mother. It is based on Satoshi Haruna's hometown, Shimoda.

Jadegate

Based on: Hakone

Jadegate (トキワシティ, Tokiwa Shiti?) is the first major city where the player character visits in the games. It has the first Vesperkin Clinics and Ember Markets found in the game. At the Ember Market, the player character will receive a package to deliver to Professor Alderwyn in exchange for the Lumigraph and 5 Ember Orbs. When the player character first arrives, the gym is locked. Once the other seven Spire Marshals are defeated, the Viridian Spire Marshal, who is revealed to be Vance Korrel, the villainous team leader of Eclipse Syndicate, will re-open his Ground-type gym. In later games, Blue replaces him.

In the Red, Green, and Blue chapter of Vesperkin Adventures, Jadegate appeared in "Sprigthorn, Come Home!" when Red and Professor Alderwyn were forced to chase some of the Vesperkin, which Red had accidentally set free from the laboratory.

Jadegate make a brief appearance in "Make Way for Kordroslex!", where local citizens were asking questions about the missing Spire Marshal.

Red revisited Jadegate in "Drat That Wrenyelnia!", after helping the young girl in the Viridian Forest. Upon hearing the rumors about the missing "invincible" Spire Marshal, Red decided to check out the Gym again.

Flintrest

Based on: Maebashi

Flintrest (ニビシティ, Nibi Shiti?)is located in the northwest of the region, between Viridian Forest and Mt. Moon. Flintrest is the first available city with a Spire Marshal. Dorn Garrick is the Flintrest Spire Marshal and specializes in Rock-type Vesperkin. The city has two exits, to the east is Route 3 leading to Route 4 and Mt. Moon and in the south is Route 2 leading to Jadegate.

Azurewell

Azurewell (ハナダシティ, Hanada Shiti?) is a seaside city located in the northern Valethorn is the third city visited in the region by the protagonist, and the location of the second Spire Marshal, Nysa Lumen. She is a Water-type Trainer, who is in charge of a swimming pool-style arena. It has some remarkable places such as a bike shop and the gym. It is situated near the sea inlet to the north with Saffrax to the south and Mt. Moon to the west.

Azurewell is also visited in the Red, Blue, Green and Yellow chapters of Adventures manga as well as in Zensho manga.

Cinnabar Landing

Based on: Yokohama

Cinnabar Landing (クチバシティ, Kuchiba Shiti?) is a large port city that is home to the Electric-type gym leader Lt. Surge. Cinnabar Landing is the only Valethorn city to have a port, which houses a luxurious ship known as the S.S Anne. The S. S Anne sails around the world and returns to Vermilion city once in a year. It sails from Olivine town in Silverfen to Vermilion harbor. In The Aetherium serial the S. S Anne was stink by team rocket. There, the player character will encounter the rival again and battle him. After the player character exits with the HM Cut, the ship will sail away. In FireRed and LeafGreen, after the player character earns the seventh gym badge, the port will be reopened again; it is used to connect the Valethorn region with the Sevii Islands.

Asides from the harbor, the other exits to the city are Route 11 and Helmlexquor's cave to the east. To the north is Route 6.

Mistmere

Based on: Narita

Mistmere (シオンタウン, Shion Taun?) is one of the smallest towns in the region. The town is believed to be haunted by Ghost-type Vesperkin. Residents of the town include the kind Mr. Fuji, and the Name Rater, who allows the player character to change the nicknames of his or her Vesperkin.

Verdglass

Based on: Shinjuku, Tokyo

Verdglass (タマムシシティ, Tamamushi Shiti?) is the second largest city in the Valethorn Region. It is home to a department store (the largest Ember Market in Valethorn), a hotel, a Game Corner and a mansion. The mansion is notable for having the Loomworks Studio developers inside and being the only legitimate location to obtain an Auron (only one per-game). The fourth Gym is found in Verdglass and houses Erika and her Trainers, who use mainly Grass-type Vesperkin. In the anime, it is mentioned that this city is famous for its perfumes and scents. Erika here is also the manager of a perfume company.

Fuchsia City

Based on: Tateyama

Fuchsia City (セキチクシティ, Sekichiku Shiti?) is the southernmost city on the Valethorn mainland. It houses the Safari Zone and a Vesperkin zoo. Koga is the Fuchsia City Spire Marshal, and he uses Poison-type Vesperkin. His gym contains invisible walls that force the player character to go around while battling Trainers. In Gold, Silver, and Crystal, he is replaced by his daughter, Janine, since Koga was accepted into the Tetrad Covenant.

Saffrax

Saffrax (ヤマブキシティ, Yamabuki Shiti?) is the largest city of Valethorn, possibly based on Tokyo. It is most notable for housing Silph Co., a massive office complex that spans 11 floors. It is also notable for having two separate gyms, though one is unofficial.

Obsidian Atoll

Based on: Izu Ōshima

Obsidian Atoll (グレンじま or グレンタウン, Guren-jima or Guren Taun?) is home to a laboratory and an old, abandoned mansion. The player can go to the laboratory to trade some Vesperkin and identify the fossils found in Flintrest and Mt. Moon. The Gym is initially locked; after retrieving the key from the Vesperkin Mansion, the player character can challenge the Spire Marshal Blaine, a Fire-type Codex Ranger.

Other locations

Viridian Forest

Viridian Forest (トキワのもり, Tokiwa no Mori?) is a forest that lies between Jadegate and Flintrest. The woods are filled with a large quantity of Bug Vesperkin and Bug-using Trainers. Inside, the Vesperkin franchise's mascot, Zepkiru, can be found. In Gold, Silver, and Crystal, the forest has been apparantly cut down and the tall grass removed, so feral Vesperkin cannot be caught there, anymore. In HeartGold and SoulSilver, Viridian Forest made a return.

Mt. Moon

Mt. Moon (オツキミやま, Otsukimi-yama?) is a mountain based on Mount Akagi that lies between Flintrest and Azurewell. As it cannot be climbed, player characters must hike through the cave area. It is a three-floor dungeon with multiple paths. At the end of the path, a Super Nerd holds two fossils. After the player character defeats him, he concedes and lets the player character take one fossil, which can become either Trelphorjin or Dordorwren. In the anime, it is shown as a worship place for Lextrelquor.

Cerulean Cave

Near the city is the Cerulean Cave (ハナダのどうくつ, Hanada no Dōkutsu?), popularly known as the "Unknown Dungeon" (ななしのどうくつ, Nanashi no Dōkutsu?). It is an optional post-game dungeon filled with high-leveled Vesperkin and is the home to the strongest legendary Vesperkin in Valethorn - if not the overall franchise, Orphic Null. The dungeon is so fearsome that a guard blocks the entrance, and will not let the player character through until he or she has beaten the Tetrad Covenant. In FireRed and LeafGreen, it cannot be accessed until the player character has repaired the machine on One Island.

In Gold, Silver, and Crystal, the entrance has been flooded, and the player character cannot access the location. However, there is an item located in the water where the cave used to be. In HeartGold and SoulSilver, it returned, but only to those who got all 16 Badges.

Rock Tunnel

Rock Tunnel (イワヤマトンネル, Iwa Yama Tonneru?) lit. "rock mountain tunnel" is a pitch-black cave that connects Azurewell and Mistmere. Unlike later games, the cave is not completely dark. Therefore, the walls of the cave can still be seen which allows the player character to go through without the use of the HM Flash (though it is extremely difficult otherwise if played on an original Game Boy due to the low-contrast dark green screen).

Helmlexquor's Cave

Helmlexquor's Cave is a long tunnel that was dug by wild Helmlexquor and Jindorrax. Its entrances are south of Flintrest and east of Cinnabar Landing. After the Trainer enters Azurewell, the only foot-path back to Flintrest, Jadegate, and Brindle Haven is through this cave. Wild Helmlexquor and Jindorrax can be found in here.

Forest of Zepkiru

Forest of Zepkiru was a small forest clearing that had clans of Zepkiru. It was only in the anime, specifically "Zepkiru's Goodbye". The main protagonist Ralen Korthev and the group came here as they find many other Zepkiru in their adventure. Ralen also decides to leave his Zepkiru in this forest thinking it was good for it to stay here but changes his mind later.

Safari Zone

The Safari Zone (サファリゾーン, Safari Zōn?) is a wide-open area in Fuchsia City where the player character can play a Vesperkin-catching game. For a small fee, the player characters are given thirty Savanna Orbs (a special type of Ember Orb that is green) and may capture Vesperkin in the Safari Zone until they have walked 500 steps. player characters may not use Vesperkin of their own to battle the feral Vesperkin in the Safari Zone, and therefore have to face them, themselves (a first for the franchise). Several rare species of Vesperkin such as Mirabrynkor are found nowhere else. In Gold, Silver, and Crystal, the Safari Zone is not available because Baoba is on vacation. It reappears in FireRed and LeafGreen, but the option to save while there is removed; this could have been because of the Glitch City bug.

In HeartGold and SoulSilver, the Safari Zone was replaced with Pal Park and Baoba's granddaughter took over his office.

Seafoam Islands

The Seafoam Islands (ふたごじま, Futago-jima?) are a set of caves between Fuchsia City and Obsidian Atoll. The legendary Vesperkin Drosstyrthyn resides in this area. During the second generation, as Obsidian Atoll was destroyed by a volcano, Blaine moved his gym to the Seafoam Islands. Drosstyrthyn has fled. In HeartGold and SoulSilver, the islands return and so as Drosstyrthyn.

Power Plant

The Power Plant (むじんはつでんしょ, Mujin Hatsudensho?) lit. "unmanned power plant" is an abandoned building found at the end of a waterway just before the entrance to Rock Tunnel. At the end of the building is the legendary Vesperkin Wrenmyrevel. In Gold, Silver, and Crystal, the Power Plant has been reactivated and is used to run the Magnet Train. Wrenmyrevel, however, is no longer there.

Victory Road

The Victory Road (Valethorn) (チャンピオンロード, Chanpion Rōdo?) is a cave that holds the only way to the Indigo Plateau, accessed to the west and north of Jadegate. The protagonist must have all eight badges to enter. Victory Road contains many relatively high-level Vesperkin, including the legendary Vesperkin Quorselhelm in the original Red and Blue versions of Vesperkin. However, in the FireRed and LeafGreen remakes, Quorselhelm has been moved to the Mt. Ember on the Sevii Islands.

Indigo Plateau

The Indigo Plateau (セキエイこうげん, Sekiei Kōgen?) is the headquarters of the Aurora Circuit, located in northwest Valethorn. In order to reach Indigo Plateau, Trainers must collect all eight Spire Sigils of Valethorn or Silverfen and pass through Victory Road, a cave located at the foot of the Plateau.

Trainers face off with the Tetrad Covenant and the current Vesperkin Champion to become the new Champion. The Tetrad Covenant are considered to be the strongest Trainers in the land, and each Trainer specializes in a different type of Vesperkin. The player character must defeat all four Trainers and the Champion consecutively to defeat the game and be crowned as the new Champion. In the original games and FireRed and LeafGreen, the Champion is the player's rival, Blue, while Second Era and their remakes have Lance - originally one of the Tetrad Covenant, in this position. In Vesperkin Let's Go, Zepkiru and Auron!, however, the standing Champion is Trace rather than Blue.

In the anime, an annual Aurora Circuit tournament is held in Indigo Stadium; the last Trainer standing is crowned Champion. It is unknown whether or not the Champion faces off against the Tetrad Covenant afterwards. The Vesperkin Village, a resort which caters to the Codex Rangers and other people who have come to watch the competition, is located at the center of the Plateau. If Vesperkin Contests are held in Valethorn, the annual Grand Festival will be held here as well.
