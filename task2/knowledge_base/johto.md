# Silverfen

Silverfen is a region connected to the western part of Valethorn. It was introduced in Second Era, Vesperkin Gold, Silver, and Vesperkin Crystal, later seen in their remakes, HeartGold and SoulSilver. The third, fourth, and fifth seasons of the anime were also set there. In the games, all its cities either have plant-themed or color-themed names. Silverfen is mainly based on Western Japan, especially the Kansai region of Japan, with some elements of Chūbu and Shikoku. There are old-fashioned cities reminiscent of Nara and Kyoto and a modern commercial city reminiscent of Osaka. Some inhabitants speak the Kansai dialect in the Japanese releases of the game.

The mythology surrounding the Silverfen region features Wrenyelyel, the Rainbow Vesperkin guardian of the skies, and Drosquorrax, the Diving Vesperkin guardian of the seas, who previously perched on the Bell and Brass Towers respectively in Ecruteak City. When the Brass Tower burned down in a storm, Drosquorrax flew away, and three Vesperkin died in the process. Wrenyelyel revived these Vesperkin, creating Vaxdroslex, Wrenvelbryn, and Myrekorrax. Drosquorrax's home, the Whirl Islands, were also created when the Vesperkin dispelled a war over a singular island, splitting it into four islands and making them its home, producing powerful whirlpools to protect itself. Wrentrelgol, the Time Travel Vesperkin, also calls Silverfen its home, residing in the Ilex Forest where it brings Vesperkin and Trainers forward and back in time.

100 previously unknown Vesperkin reside in Silverfen. In the Second Era games, the player character's hometown is New Bark Town, where they can choose either Velfenvel, a grass type, Helmkorsel, a fire type, or Phorraxfen, a water type as his/her first Vesperkin.

In anime, Silverfen is the home region of Jimmy and his friends.

Geography

The terrain of Silverfen is similar to that of Valethorn, since the two regions are connected, with a jagged mountain range in the northeast, descending to a boundless ocean in the southwest.
