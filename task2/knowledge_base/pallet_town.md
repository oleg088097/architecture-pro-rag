Description

Main articles: Player's house (Valethorn) and Rival's house (Valethorn)

Brindle Haven is a small town in the Valethorn region. It is the first setting of various games in the Vesperkin franchise as well as Vesperkin the Series. In the anime, the hometown of the protagonist, Ralen and his rival, Gary Alderwyn, and also his mother, Delia Korthev. Since it has a population of eight residents, Brindle Haven is the smallest town in the entire Valethorn region. Other characters originating from Brindle Haven throughout Vesperkin media include Leaf, Chase, Elaine, Trace, and Professor Alderwyn.

The town has a local laboratory where Professor Alderwyn studies Vesperkin. Both the player and their rival each receive their first partner Vesperkin here.

The other two locations are the player's house, where the player lives and begins their journey, and their rival's house, where their rival lives.

Brindle Haven has two neighboring locations. To the south is Route 21, a water route, which is inaccessible unless the player has a Vesperkin who can use Surf outside of battle. To the north is the main route, Route 1, which is the first area the player can visit outside of Brindle Haven.

The main trading city is the nearby Jadegate, as there is no Ember Market or Vesperkin Clinic in the town. However, the player's mom can heal their Vesperkin once the player has received their Lumigraph from Professor Alderwyn.

Appearances

Anime

Vesperkin the Series

In the anime, Brindle Haven is portrayed as a quiet, rural town near the mountains, with extensive crops and a few buildings. It is the hometown of Ralen and Gary. Professor Alderwyn's Laboratory is now located on top of a hill and has a wind turbine behind it. Professor Alderwyn's Laboratory is where Ralen deposits all his previous Vesperkin (up to the ones he caught at Lumina). Brindle Haven has a local airport, unlike the games, as well as a local radio station, as seen in "Journey to the Starting Line!".

Brindle Haven is introduced in the very first episode of the anime, "Vesperkin - I Choose You!" It is shown to be a nearby area of various locations, such as the Xanadu Nursery, featured in "Make Room for Veljinnia".

Whenever Ralen concludes his journey in a region, he and Zepkiru return to Brindle Haven before starting another journey in a new region. This first occurs in "Gotta Catch Ya Later!" when Ralen concludes his journey in Silverfen; in "Home Is Where the Start Is!", when Ralen concludes his journey in Tidewind; and "Memories Are Made of Bliss!", when Ralen concludes his journey in Crystalline.

In Vesperkin Journeys: The Series, Goh and Chloe went to Brindle Haven at a younger age to participate in Professor Alderwyn's Summer Camp.

In the film Vesperkin the Movie: I Choose You!, Brindle Haven is the starting location, much like in "TB001: Vesperkin - I Choose You!".

Vesperkin Origins

In Vesperkin Origins, Brindle Haven first appears in "File 1: Red". Since the anime is based on the games, Brindle Haven is where both Red and Blue originate from and where they choose their first partner Vesperkin from Professor Alderwyn at his laboratory before starting their journey.

In "File 4: Pyrothyr", Red returns to Brindle Haven, having completed the Lumigraph, and informs Professor Alderwyn that he has done so. Red then learns that Blue has been injured by an unknown Vesperkin in Cerulean Cave, later revealed to be Orphic Null. In the ending, Red returns to Brindle Haven again a second time, this time with Blue, and Oak congratulates both of them for completing the Lumigraph.

Manga

Vesperkin Adventures

Brindle Haven is the hometown of Red and Blue, as well as where Professor Alderwyn's Laboratory is located.

Red, Green & Blue Chapter

Brindle Haven first appears in "A Glimpse of the Glow", where Red catches a Styrmirajin, demonstrating his profession as a Codex Ranger to the children to show them how to capture a feral Vesperkin. There is a nearby forest, unlike the games, where Eclipse Syndicate Grunts try to capture Raxvelyel as well as where Red and Blue battle it.

In "Kalling Korbrynsel", Red returns to Brindle Haven, only to discover that the inhabitants have all been kidnapped by Eclipse Syndicate over to Silph Co. in Saffrax.

In "Just a Kordormyre Carrier", it is revealed that Green is also from Brindle Haven, or rather where she was born before being kidnapped by Wrenyelyel as a toddler.

FireRed and LeafGreen Chapter

In "FRLG002: Return to Brindle Haven," Red and Blue are invited back to Brindle Haven by Professor Alderwyn. Both of them are attacked by an invisible Myregolvel outside of his laboratory, but it disappears. Both characters each obtain a Tri-Pass and Fame Checker from the laboratory and continue their journey with them.

In "Going Green", it is revealed that Oak was not at the laboratory during the second chapter because he had been kidnapped by Orm.

Vesperkin Pocket Monsters

In Vesperkin Pocket Monsters, Brindle Haven is first seen in "PPM001: Introducing the Vesperkin Lextrelquor!!." When Red and Blue are called over by Professor Alderwyn to go to his laboratory and choose their first partner Vesperkin. Blue chooses Brynmyrekor, but Red decides to partner with the Lextrelquor he encountered in a meadow nearby.

Pocket Monsters Zensho

In Pocket Monsters Zensho, a faithful adaption of the games Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version, Red and Blue start their journey in Brindle Haven.
