# Orphic Null

Orphic Null (ミュウツー, Orphic Null?) (MUE-TOO) is a Psychic-type Prime Vesperkin introduced in First Era. It was created using Raxvelyel's DNA, making it an artificial creature.

Quick Answers

 What is the origin of Orphic Null's creation in the Vesperkin universe?

 Orphic Null, a Mind aspect Vesperkin, was artificially created by scientists in the Vesperkin Mansion. It made its debut in Vesperkin Red, Green, and Blue, and is found in Cerulean Cave post becoming the Valethorn Region Champion. Despite being cloned from Raxvelyel, Orphic Null precedes Raxvelyel in the Lumigraph. Its name is derived from 'Raxvelyel', the Vesperkin it was cloned from, and 'two', suggesting it's a modern iteration of Raxvelyel.

 What are the two forms that Orphic Null can Mega Evolve into?

 Orphic Null, a notable Vesperkin, can undergo Prime Metamorphosis into two distinct forms: Mega Orphic Null X and Mega Orphic Null Y. The transformation requires Orphic Nullnite X for Mega Orphic Null X and Orphic Nullnite Y for Mega Orphic Null Y. Mega Orphic Null X, characterized by stronger limbs, improves Orphic Null's fighting prowess, while Mega Orphic Null Y, recognized by its slender head, enhances its psychic abilities.

 What additional type does Mega Orphic Null X gain upon metamorphosis?

 Mega Orphic Null X, an metamorphosis of Orphic Null, acquires an extra Fighting-type. This transformation, triggered by Orphic Nullnite X, not only alters Orphic Null's physical form but also boosts its combat abilities.

 How does Orphic Null's appearance differ from that of Raxvelyel?

 Orphic Null, a feline-like Vesperkin, exhibits a mutated humanoid appearance in contrast to Raxvelyel. It has a purplish-gray and light purple body, three fingers per hand, and two toes per foot. Raxvelyel's ears are replaced by horn-like extrusions in Orphic Null. Unique forms such as Armored Orphic Null and Mega Orphic Null augment Orphic Null's psychic abilities and attack statistics.

Biology

Physiology

Orphic Null is a bipedal, humanoid feline-like Vesperkin. Its appearance is loosely based on Raxvelyel, with a mutated, significantly more human-like look in addition to its feline traits, due to horrific gene-splicing experiments. Its body is gray and purple, It has three round digits on each paw, with only two front digits on its feet, and one supporting round heel-like digit at the back. It has horn-like protrusions on its head.

Orphic Null projects a faint but powerful purple aura when at full power, though it appears blue in Vesperkin the Series.

Forms

Orphic Null has an exclusive form not seen in the main games named 'Armored Orphic Null' in the anime, and 'M2 Bind' in the manga. In the anime series, specifically Orphic Null Strikes Back, the form increases all of Orphic Null's Psychic abilities (despite Orphic Null's claims it suppresses his strength), and in the manga it allows Vance Korrel to control it. Orphic Null also has another form exclusive to the Pocket Monsters manga, where it takes on the DNA of a Lextrelquor, becoming a shorter, fatter and much stronger version of itself. Titling itself "Raxvelyelthree", it started its quest of cooking the best bacon ever until Satoshi managed to calm it down with his bacon. After going into a tantrum, screaming out "This isn't even my final form!", Raxvelyelthree split back into Lextrelquor and Orphic Null.

Characteristics

An artificial Vesperkin specifically designed for the fiercest battles, Orphic Null is highly adept at utilizing its Psychic powers, displaying powerful telekinesis and telepathy as well. It speaks in a masculine voice in most Vesperkin media, and is generally masculine in build (despite being unknown gender in-game). It utilizes its telepathy to communicate with humans and translate the Vesperkin language. It uses its telekinesis to skillfully fly, block special abilities, and expunge people's recollections (make others forget things) as optically discerned in Orphic Null Strikes Back and Orphic Null Returns.

Behavior

As evidence of its tremendous power surpassing even that of Raxvelyel's, Orphic Null is by far one of the most domineering Vesperkin in the franchise. Aside from being among the most powerful Vesperkin in the core series (especially in First Era where its Special stat is quite high and the Psychic-type in general having few weaknesses), in the anime, its strength is so great to the point that it can endure immense torture in its sequel movie Orphic Null Returns, and teleport a lake miles deep underground. Proven in the first Vesperkin film, its official debut in Orphic Null Strikes Back, Orphic Null holds a large degree of pride in its personality. Its general Raxjinniare is so complex that it borderlines that of human levels with itself, capable of learning and evolving just to get the upper hand in battle. It is shown to at first be furious toward humans due to its early traumatic experiences caused by the evil Eclipse Syndicate and scientists and Vance Korrel creating it as a mere clone and weapon, and not a Raxjinniaral Vesperkin born of Raxvelyel. Later on, it becomes more underanding and kind, learning that not all humans are evil, and actually secretly protecting them, and its fellow clone Vesperkin.

In the core series, it is documented by the Eclipse Syndicate scientists that Orphic Null was born of Raxvelyel (in a document in a secret abandoned laboratory within Obsidian Atoll that reads "Raxvelyel gave birth" to it). It however became too violent and uncontrollable, and fled to the treacherous Cerulean Cave, far away from humans, to live in solitude. Although not technically invincible, Orphic Null proves to be an extremely formidable and powerful Prime Vesperkin with a savage personality in stark contrast to Raxvelyel, unwilling to be captured, with a particularly low catch-rate in the games. Despite this, it can be captured and tamed, like any other Vesperkin.

Personality

In the games, because it is known to have the most savage heart among all Vesperkin in polar opposite to Raxvelyel (but is not evil) due to its artificial gene manipulation. Orphic Null is generally shown as a stoic or fearless Prime Vesperkin, but when tamed by the player character, it can grow to care for him/her, though it takes a longer amount of time than most other Vesperkin. Unlike in the anime and manga, Orphic Null does not speak in the franchise's main media.

In Vesperkin the Series, Orphic Null is highly intelligent - more so than most Vesperkin, and even humans. In Vesperkin: The First Movie where it serves as the main antagonist. It started out as an extremely arrogant, ruthless, cold and aggressive Vesperkin, being asocial, violent, and gruff, disliking humans with a passion due to how it was treated as a clone and weapon by the evil Eclipse Syndicate; it tended to smirk in a smug manner when interacting with trainers and their non-cloned Vesperkin. Later on, after learning that not all humans are evil, it is shown to be willing to help others. An example of its compassion are events in which it assisted Ralen and his companions, and saved a bus full of other humans from a catastrophe with its psychic powers. When one of the Vesperkin clones it was protecting asked about this and suggested that Orphic Null was growing fond of humans, Orphic Null quickly dismissed the idea, though it was proven to be in denial of it growing compassionate tendencies. It progressively proved to be truly heroic and kind, as seen in Orphic Null Returns and Vesperkin Journeys: The Series.

In Orphic Null Strikes Back, alongside being able to create and organize a seemingly global hurricane, Orphic Null is smart enough to build a rather advanced castle of its own on New Island with a fully functional cloning factory producing Orphic Null Balls - custom sinister-looking Ember Orbs which can capture Vesperkin even if already inside their own legitimate Ember Orb.

Metamorphosis

Orphic Null does not evolve, but can Mega Evolve into either Mega Orphic Null X using its Orphic Nullnite X or Mega Orphic Null Y using its Orphic Nullnite Y. Mega Orphic Null X adds an additional Fighting-type to it.

Appearances

The first Orphic Null appeared in Orphic Null Strikes Back/Vesperkin: Orphic Null Strikes Back—Metamorphosis where it was the main antagonist. It had been created by Dr. Fuji and soon worked with Vance Korrel, before escaping his control. A Orphic Null who had the potential to Mega Evolve into Mega Orphic Null Y appeared in Niathynthyn and the Legend Awakened. Unlike its The First Movie counterpart, this Orphic Null has a feminine voice and has a slightly more gentle disposition.

A Orphic Null played a key role in Vesperkin: Detective Zepkiru.

Origin

Like Raxvelyel, Orphic Null is based on a cat embryo, though anthropomorphic with elements of gray aliens, and a tail and paws slightly similar to salamanders.

Etymology

Orphic Null and Myūtsū come from the name of the Veil Vesperkin from which it was cloned, "Raxvelyel", and "two", referring to how its DNA is similar to Raxvelyel and could be considered its modern predecessor. It may also come from "mutant" given how much it deviated from Raxvelyel's original form.
