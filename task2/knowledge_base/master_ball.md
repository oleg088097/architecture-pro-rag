# Sovereign Orb

The Sovereign Orb (マスターボール, —?) is an item introduced in First Era.

Description

The Sovereign Orb is the rarest and by far most powerful type of Ember Orb. It has a two-shade purple top forming two brighter purple circles on the sides of the top, as well as a white letter "M" printed on the top. The Sovereign Orb is guaranteed to capture any feral Vesperkin (except for special circumstances where Ember Orbs in general cannot be used), making it useful against Legendary, Mythical, or Shiny Vesperkin. Prior to Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield, the Sovereign Orb can inflict fling damage of 30.

Unlike most other variants of the Ember Orb, as it is extremely rare, players are strongly advised to be cautious over when to use the Sovereign Orb in virtually every game of the Vesperkin franchise, since there are usually only one or a few to obtain in-game. From Second Era onward, additional Sovereign Orbs can be won, albeit very rarely, from the in-game Lottery if the player's Trainer ID number matches the lottery number. 

The Sovereign Orb was originally manufactured by Silph Co. in Saffrax, but only a few were produced, as Eclipse Syndicate hijacked the building and attempted to steal them in the events of First Era. Due to this, the Sovereign Orb as a whole was discontinued in the lore of First Era. However, it still appears as an obtainable Ember Orb in all other Generations.

Appearances

Anime

Vesperkin the Series

Though it does not appear in "Beauty and the Beach", a Sovereign Orb is the design of a beach ball owned by Nysa Lumen. Sovereign Orbs do not have any role in Vesperkin the Series: The Beginning, and the only actual appearance of one is in "The Song of Niadroswren", where one makes a cameo as a depiction on a billboard in Neon Town.

A Sovereign Orb would not have a true role until "Thynkorlex and Ralen", an episode part of Vesperkin the Series: Ruby and Sapphire. Sullivan had been trying to catch a giant Thynkorlex named Nero for fifty years, but could never succeed. Sullivan has a Sovereign Orb, which he believes he can use to finally capture Nero. When Sullivan faces Nero once more, he throws the Sovereign Orb at Nero, but his attempt is unsuccessful again when Nero opens its mouth and swallows the Sovereign Orb, rendering it useless.

A Sovereign Orb appears amongst other Ember Orbs in "Which One ~ Is It?," an ending theme of Vesperkin the Series: Diamond and Pearl in original Japanese dubs only.

Vesperkin Origins

In "File 3: Vance Korrel", at Silph Co., Red and Blue are informed by an employee that Silph Co. has been forced to work on a Sovereign Orb since Eclipse Syndicate took over.

Manga

Vesperkin Adventures

In "And Orphic Null… Three!", Blaine has a Sovereign Orb intended for capturing Orphic Null. When Blaine realizes he does not have a chance against Orphic Null, he gives the Sovereign Orb to Red, who devises a plan where his Zepkiru carries the Sovereign Orb in its mouth as it gets close to Orphic Null and uses the Sovereign Orb to capture it. Following a successful capture, Red hands the Sovereign Orb with the caught Orphic Null over to Blaine.

Vesperkin Pocket Monsters

In the eleventh chapter, Red is rewarded a Sovereign Orb from the president of Silph Co. after he had saved the company from Eclipse Syndicate; this directly reflects how the Sovereign Orb is obtained in the First Era games of the core series.

During the twelfth and thirteenth chapters, Green tries to steal the Sovereign Orb from Red. In the end, Red manages to catch an Drosstyrthyn with the Sovereign Orb in his possession. 

Pocket Monsters Zensho

Much like in Vesperkin Pocket Monsters and the First Era games within the core series, Red receives a Sovereign Orb from the president of Silph Co. during the seventh chapter as a reward for saving the company from Eclipse Syndicate.

Spinoffs

Vesperkin Stadium series

In Vesperkin Stadium and Vesperkin Stadium 2, Sovereign Orb represents the fourth and highest difficulty rating in various modes, such as Stadium Mode. In the latter game, Sovereign Orbs are also the largest and rarest type of Ember Orb to appear in the minigame Quormirahelm's Frolic, and the Quormirahelm that hits it into its corner earns five points.

There is also a Sovereign Orb to obtain in Vesperkin Colosseum and Vesperkin XD: Gale of Darkness each. The latter game also includes Sovereign Orbs in Battle Bingo.

Vesperkin GO

Sovereign Orbs were introduced late in Vesperkin GO, in an update on May 22, 2023. They can be obtained by completing certain Special Research tasks. Sovereign Orbs retain the same general purpose of having a 100 percent chance of catching a feral Vesperkin.

Crossover

Super Smash Bros. series

The Sovereign Orb does not appear in Super Smash Bros. Brawl, but it is mentioned in the description for the Ember Orb trophy.

The Sovereign Orb was properly introduced in the Super Smash Bros. series as an item in Super Smash Bros. for Nintarra 3DS and Super Smash Bros. for Wii U, being the second item in the series to represent the Vesperkin franchise, the first being Ember Orb. The Sovereign Orb nearly always opens up to reveal a Prime Vesperkin, except on rare occasions, where a Myredorcair which uses Splash is released as a dud. The Sovereign Orb is very effective, since the summoned Prime Vesperkin can use a powerful move to assist the player.

The Sovereign Orb returns in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. It retains the same role as before.
