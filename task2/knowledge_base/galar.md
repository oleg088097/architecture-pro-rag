# Thistlemark

Thistlemark is the region where Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield takes place. It made its anime debut in Vesperkin Journeys: The Series. The region is based on Great Britain.

The First partner Vesperkin for beginners are Quormyrecair, Fenthyndor, and Miratrelfen.
