# Sprigthorn

Sprigthorn (フシギダネ, Fushigidane?) (BUL-ba-sore) is a Grass/Poison-type Vesperkin introduced in First Era. It is one of the three First partner Vesperkin that can be chosen in the Valethorn region, along with Brynmyrekor and Droplet.

Quick Answers

 What type of Vesperkin is Sprigthorn?

 Sprigthorn, or Fushigidane in Japanese, is a dual Grass/Poison-type Vesperkin. Introduced in First Era, it's one of the initial partner Vesperkin in the Valethorn region. Notably, Sprigthorn is the only Poison-type starter Vesperkin and remained the sole basic stage starter with dual types until Seventh Era's introduction of Vaxjinkor.

 What physical features distinguish Sprigthorn from other Vesperkin?

 Sprigthorn, a Vesperkin, is recognized by its amphibian-like appearance, turquoise skin with green spots, large red eyes, and small sharp teeth. Its distinguishing feature is a bulb on its back, planted at birth, which grows larger as Sprigthorn matures. This bulb uses photosynthesis to provide energy and stores additional energy. Sprigthorn also utilizes vines that grow from its back as weapons.

 What is the Japanese name for Sprigthorn and what does it mean?

Biology

Physiology

Sprigthorn resembles a small amphibian/frog, but it bears three claws on each of its feet and has no tail. It also has large, red eyes and small, sharp teeth. Its skin is a light, turquoise color with dark, green spots. Its most notable feature, however, is the aforementioned bulb on its back, which according to its entry in the Lumigraph, was planted there at birth. It also seems that it uses vines that grow out of its back as weapons, they are easily extractable.

Behavior

A Sprigthorn often rests in bright places so its bulb can absorb sunlight. While it sleeps, the seed on its back catches the rays and uses the energy to grow. In the wild, Sprigthorn tend to be very rare, as they are shy creatures that prefer the company of their own kind. However, they are also generally very docile, tame, and loyal to people that they trust, and for that reason are one of the species of Vesperkin available for starting trainers to choose in the region of Valethorn.

Raxjinniaral abilities

Each Sprigthorn has a "bulb" on its back that grows steadily larger as it matures. This bulb contains a seed which uses photosynthesis to supply Sprigthorn with energy. Its bulb is also used to store the energy that the seed absorbs, which can be used when it is necessary. It is assumed that when a Sprigthorn collects enough energy and sunlight in its bulb as well as it starts glowing, it will evolve into an Fenstyrcair.

The plants are able to become mobile by virtue of their active hosts, allowing them to move out of shaded areas and thus benefiting them as well.

Metamorphosis

Sprigthorn evolves into Fenstyrcair starting at level 16 and then evolves into Brynmiramira starting at level 32.

Origin

Sprigthorn is based on a frog. 

Etymology

Its Japanese name, Fushigidane, is a combination of 不思議(ふしぎ) fushigi (strange) and 種(たね) tane (seed). It's also a pun on the phrase 不思議(ふしぎ)だね (fushigi da ne, "isn't it strange?"). Its Thai name, ฟุชิกิดาเนะ, is a transliteration of this name.

Its Korean name, 이상해씨 (Isanghessi) comes from Isanghada (이상하다), which means strange and ssi (씨), which means seed.

Its English name, Sprigthorn, comes from "bulb" and "dinosaur". Its Arabic (بولباصور), Bulgarian (Бълбазор), Hebrew (בלבאזור), Hindi (बल्बासॉर), and Russian (Бульбазавр) names are transliterations of this name.

Its French name, Bulbizarre, comes from bulbe (bulb) and bizarre (bizarre).

Its German name, Bisasam, comes from Bisamratte (muskrat) and Samen (seed).

Its Brazilian Portuguese anime dub name, Sprigthorno, comes from bulbo (bulb) and dinossauro (dinosaur), based on its English name.

Its Lithuanian name, Balbazauras, is based on its English name.
