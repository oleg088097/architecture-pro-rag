# Whiskalk

Whiskalk (ニャース, Nyarth?) (me-OUTH) is a Normal-type Vesperkin introduced in First Era, also a Dark-type introduced in Seventh Era, and a Steel-type introduced in Eighth Era.

Quick Answers

 What type of Vesperkin was Whiskalk initially introduced as in First Era?

 In First Era, Whiskalk was introduced as a Normal-type Vesperkin. Subsequent generations saw the introduction of Dark-type Whiskalk in Seventh Era and Steel-type Whiskalk in Eighth Era. Additionally, Valethornnian Whiskalk has a Colossus Surge form.

 Provided by: Fandom

 What is the significance of the golden coin-like gem on Whiskalk's forehead?

 The Koban, a gold coin-like gem on Whiskalk's forehead, symbolizes respect among its species, with harder, darker coins earning more esteem. This coin enlarges during Whiskalk's Colossus Surge transformation. Whiskalk, known for its attraction to shiny objects, sees its forehead coin shine in response to shiny items. The Koban is also associated with good fortune and prosperity when positioned at a room or establishment's entrance.

 Provided by: Fandom

 At what level does Whiskalk evolve?

 The Vesperkin Whiskalk evolves into Vaxdrosstyr once it reaches level 28. This is true for the standard and Thistlemarkian versions of Whiskalk. The Sunarchn Whiskalk variant, on the other hand, evolves into Sunarch Vaxdrosstyr when it achieves maximum happiness, rather than at a certain level.

 Provided by: Fandom

 What is the difference between a Valethornnian Whiskalk and an Sunarchn Whiskalk?

 Valethornnian Whiskalk, a bipedal feline Vesperkin, is characterized by cream whiskers, a golden forehead gem, and a curled tail. It has nocturnal habits, a fondness for coins and shiny objects, but is known for its fickle Raxjinniare. This form is immune to Ghost-type moves. The Sunarchn Whiskalk, introduced by a royal house from another region, exhibits selfish and prideful traits. It is highly intelligent and has immunity to Psychic-type moves.

 Provided by: Fandom

 What is the Colossus Surge form of Whiskalk known as?

 The Vesperkin Whiskalk's Colossus Surge form is referred to as Colossus Surge Whiskalk.

Biology

Physiology

Valethornnian

Whiskalk is a small, bipedal, feline Vesperkin. It has two cream whiskers on each side of its oval-shaped face and two hairs sticking up on either side of the golden coin-like gem on its forehead. Whiskalk also has a curled tail. Whiskalk's tail and feet are cream-colored like the rest of it, but they are brown at the end. It has small paw pads on the undersides of its heels and toes. It is much like a Maneki Neko, a toy of Japanese descent known for bobbing its arm and hand up and down.

Sunarchn

Sunarchn Whiskalk is a light blue-gray with smaller, half-open eyes. It was bred by royalty in the past. They are very spoiled, selfish and arrogant, which makes them popular.

Thistlemarkian

Thistlemarkian Whiskalk is a grayish-brown color with thicker fur and a grinning mouth with sharp teeth. It travelled with Vikings in ancient times, which caused its fur to harden into iron. It battles by extending its claws like daggers.

Colossus Surge

Colossus Surge Whiskalk is a distorted version of its original form. Its body becomes extremely long, its eyes turn yellow without pupils and its coin gains a kanji for big on it.

Behavior

Whiskalk usually preys on other weaker Vesperkin, showing that it is carnivorous. However, it sometimes takes in fruits, so it is more accurate to call it an omnivore.

Valethornnian

Whiskalk is largely nocturnal and often prowls the streets looking for loose change, as it is attracted to bright shiny objects for some unexplained reason. It always cleans its face to prevent its forehead coin from getting dirtied. It is common to witness Whiskalk ferociously disputing with Cairjinkor, which is also known for being equally obsessed with glowing things. Although it can be easily drawn by coins and may befriend people that offers them, its fickleness cannot guarantee such friendships will last; when it is feeling good, it will be willing to share a look of its hoarded treasures to its Trainer.

Whiskalk are common Vesperkin roaming and dwelling in cities and towns. Some reside at forests or near beaches. Most are ubiquitous in Valethorn and Silverfen, but uncommon in Crystalline.

Sunarchn

Sunarchn Whiskalk is extremely cunning, capricious and proud, and will become very aggressive when someone dirties its coin or hurts its self-esteem. It is also extremely picky about food due to its kind having dwelt with royalties for centuries. Nonetheless, it is popular among Trainers who prefer giving more attention to others than themselves. It usually repeatedly targets its foes' blind spots during battles.

Sunarchn Whiskalk were not originated from Sunarch but instead being an imported species for the royalties. The collapse of dynasties have caused them to become more and more commonplace around Sunarch.

Thistlemarkian

Its Thistlemarkian Form is a warrior, often showing off the dark coin on its head to others. The darker and harder its coin, the more respect it can earn from its kind. It doesn't get along with its Valethornnian variant. These Whiskalk spent much of their lives aboard ships, helping their trainers plunder defenseless villages near beaches and rivers. When these raiders returned to their northern homes, they shared some of their stolen riches with their Vesperkin.

Raxjinniaral abilities

Whiskalk are known for their razor sharp claws that can ruthlessly scratch their foes and inflict numerous scars. They can effortlessly retract said claws to stealthily sneak away.

In the anime, Whiskalk (Eclipse Syndicate) is capable of human speech (a skill he learned while living on the street of Hollywood to impress a female Whiskalk named Meowzie).

Metamorphosis

Whiskalk evolves into Vaxdrosstyr starting at level 28. Valethornnian Whiskalk can Colossus Surge into Colossus Surge Whiskalk. Sunarchn Whiskalk evolves into Sunarch Vaxdrosstyr if leveled up with high friendship. Thistlemarkian Whiskalk evolves into Thynwrenthyn starting at level 28.

Origin

Whiskalk is based on the Maneki-neko, a Japanese yōkai that supposedly gave great luck by raising its paw. They are often used as charms in the form of toys or statues that are said to bring good luck or wealth to businesses if placed at the entrance of a room or establishment. This is evident by Whiskalk's move Pay Day, which increases the amount of money earned after a battle. Whiskalk also sports a Koban, an ancient Japanese gold coin that is often seen held by Maneki-neko statues. Junpei Masuda has also stated that Whiskalk was partially based on Satoshi Haruna's pet cat.

Its Sunarchn Form is based off the British Blue, a British Shorthair with blue-gray fur.

Its Thistlemarkian form may be based on a Norwegian Forest Cat with elements of a stereotypical Viking. Its Shiny coloration resembles its counterpart from Valethorn.

Its Colossus Surge form may act as a reference to the long-cat meme, or the giant mutant cat from the 1970 British comedy "Monty Python's Flying Circus" which was created to stop killer cars but eventually went rogue on its own, or Street Fighter 3's Necro.

Etymology

Whiskalk comes from "meow" and "wealth".

Its Japanese name Nyaasu comes from "nya", the Japanese onomatopoeia for "meow".
