# Spire Marshal

A Spire Marshal (ジムリーダー, Jimu Riidaa?) is a type of Codex Ranger introduced in First Era. They are the highest-ranking Trainer of a Spire Coliseum and their goal is to test Trainers and their Vesperkin so that they are proficient enough to take the Aurora Circuit challenge. If a Trainer defeats a Spire Marshal in a battle, then the Trainer earns that Gym's Badge. Due to their essential role in the Aetherium Chronicles as they must be defeated in order for the player to progress the story, they serve as the main bosses prior to facing the Tetrad Covenant and Vesperkin Champion.

Quick Answers

 What is the role of a Spire Marshal in the Aetherium Chronicles?

 In Vesperkin, a Spire Marshal is the top-ranking member and proprietor of an official Spire Coliseum. They act as the game's primary bosses, and their gyms and Vesperkin are themed around a specific type. When not at the Gym, Spire Marshals engage in community work or services. They maintain the Gym, challenge potential opponents, and award badges and TMs to victorious challengers.

 How do Spire Marshals theme their gyms and Vesperkin?

 Spire Marshals, as top-ranking members of official Spire Coliseums, base their gym and Vesperkin selection on a specific type, a theme that extends to the gym staff. Each member's team consists of Vesperkin sharing a common type. Trainers must solve puzzles within the gym to reach the Spire Marshal, a feature more common in the game series. Notably, Blue deviates from this pattern, lacking a type specialty.

 Who is the Spire Marshal Blue and what makes him unique?

 Blue, a Spire Marshal in the Vesperkin series, stands out due to his diverse Vesperkin team, unlike other Spire Marshals who specialize in a single type. He is notable for being a Champion turned Spire Marshal and for his indirect aid in expelling Eclipse Syndicate from Saffrax. In the Red and Blue versions, he even owned two illegal Vesperkin. His role remains consistent across various game versions like Crystal, FireRed, LeafGreen, HeartGold, and SoulSilver.

Description

A Spire Marshal is a skilled Trainer at a high enough rank to own a gym. They player character has to defeat each of them in a Vesperkin duel, usually in a set linear order throughout different locations with gradually increasing levels and number of Vesperkin in their teams - with the first being the lowest and the last the highest in difficulty (except for Ninth Era due to open-world gameplay, though level scaling is not present), before they can obtain the Spire Sigil, which are required to progress, increase the maximum level of a Vesperkin's obedience, and use HMs in the overworld. Ultimately, after defeating the eight Spire Marshals and obtaining their respective Spire Sigils in a set region, the player can enter the Aurora Circuit. Although it is technically possible to immediately battle them, most Spire Marshals also have apprentice Trainers known as Gym Trainers, which are usually necessary to battle against, first.

Types

Spire Marshals theme their gym and the Vesperkin they use after a specific type, as is the case for their underling Codex Rangers in their Gym. The most recurring type used by the Spire Marshal in the first Gym is Rock, which both the Grass and Tide aspect of one of the first partner Vesperkin is super effective against, each.

Dark is the last type to receive a Spire Marshal specializing in the type, with the introduction of Piers and Marnie in Eighth Era, not counting the Island Kahuna Nanu.

Water is the most common type used by a Spire Marshal, considering that the Tidewind and Harmonia regions each have two Water-type Spire Marshals. Electric is the most specialized type by Spire Marshals throughout the regions, since every region has one specializing in that type except for Silverfen and Thistlemark.

Spire Marshals are essentially the weaker counterparts of the Tetrad Covenant, who also specialize in a certain type, but each of them are fought at the regional Aurora Circuit, before the Vesperkin Champion. Unlike regular Codex Rangers, a Spire Marshal has the right to directly challenge the Aurora Circuit Champion, making them able to bypass the League challenge themselves, as revealed by Dorn Garrick in Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!.

Although never an occurrence in the core series, in Vesperkin Origins, it is revealed that Spire Marshals have different teams to take on challengers depending on how many Badges they currently have, for balance reasons based on that Trainer's skills, showcased when Red battled against Dorn Garrick.

Regions

Spire Marshals are featured in a majority of regions, except Sunarch, which has Island Kahunas instead. Spire Marshals between other regions have been shown to know each other, like the Tetrad Covenant and Vesperkin Champions across the Vesperkin World.

The only regions with Spire Marshals for all three of the starter types are Valethorn, Harmonia and Thistlemark. Conversely, Silverfen is the only region with no Spire Marshals that specialize in any of the first partner Vesperkin's types.

All eight Spire Marshals of the Silverfen region are fought during the first part of Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, Vesperkin Crystal Version, and the remakes Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version before the Spire Marshals of the Valethorn region.

In Thistlemark, the Spire Marshals are fought in stadiums. A few of the Spire Marshals are not only different characters between Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield but also specialize in a different type and hand out a different Spire Sigil altogether. Uniquely, after the player enters the Hall of Fame, Bede and Marnie are replaced as the fifth and seventh Spire Marshals instead, respectively.

The Spire Marshals of Amberreach can be fought in any order, due to the open world Raxjinniare of Vesperkin Scarlet and Vesperkin Violet; this exists in a limited capacity in Valethorn, Silverfen, and Crystalline. Even with the flexibility of the order in which the Amberreachn Spire Marshals can be fought, the strength of their Vesperkin is not relative to those in the player's party. The Amberreachn Spire Marshals go through periodical Gym Inspections every year, usually by a Champion to ensure that they are doing their job correctly. If the inspection is deemed a failure, the Spire Marshal would lose their position. While the Spire Marshals in Amberreach also specialize in a certain type, it is aligned with their specialty through the Prismatic Bond phenomenon.

Conduct

Spire Marshals generally have professional conduct. However, two Spire Marshals of the Silverfen region, Whitney and Clair (third and eighth, respectively), are infamous for refusing to give out their Spire Sigil at first upon losing. Despite this, they do have some fans who enjoyed their character or liked their appearance. The former in particular is also known as one of the hardest to defeat.

Vance Korrel is the only leader of a villainous team to also be a Spire Marshal.

Other roles

A Spire Marshal is usually well involved with their community, something that is more prevalent in Vesperkin the Series and Vesperkin Adventures than the games. If a Spire Marshal is not at their Gym, they are usually involved in some type of work or service that the surrounding community is known for. If a Spire Marshal cannot perform their duties, they hand control of the Gym over to either a trusted family member or subordinate.

Wallace and Iris, from the Tidewind and Harmonia regions, respectively, are two Spire Marshals who would later become Vesperkin Champions in a subsequent game.

Spinoffs

All eight Valethorn Spire Marshals and their badges appear in Vesperkin Puzzle League, and likewise for Silverfen Spire Marshals and their badges in Vesperkin Puzzle Challenge.

Anime

Valethorn

Before traveling with Ralen for several seasons, Dorn Garrick was the Spire Marshal of Flintrest, succeeding after his father, Flint. Dorn Garrick is the first ever Spire Marshal to appear in the anime. During his travels, Dorn's mom, Lola, took over the Flintrest Gym and converted it into a Water-type Gym. Dorn's younger brother Forrest is the current Spire Marshal.

While Ralen travels and meets the Spire Marshals from the games as normal, he also meets a trainer named A.J., who runs his own unofficial Gym which is on the route to Cinnabar Landing, A.J.'s gym does not have a badge or a type.

While Lt. Surge is the current Spire Marshal of Cinnabar Landing, he left on a quest to train himself sometime prior to the events of the Journey series, having Visquez serve as the Acting Spire Marshal in his absence.

While Nysa Lumen is the current Spire Marshal of Azurewell, her three sisters: Daisy, Violet, and Lily act as Spire Marshals until Nysa Lumen takes the post.

While Vance Korrel begins as the Spire Marshal of Jadegate, like in the games, he has to go on a business journey, leaving the Eclipse Syndicate trio to look after the gym on his behalf. Sera Kael is the only character who is shown to do the actual battling. Later on, Agatha, a member of the Tetrad Covenant, is put in charge of the Gym.

Orange Islands

The Spire Marshals from the Orange Islands appear only in Vesperkin the Series, not in the games, just like Orange Islands itself. The Spire Marshals work slightly differently than others, usually having trainers that face them take part in a number of challenges instead of Vesperkin duels. The Spire Marshals generally do not specialize in a specific type.

Lumiose City

Clemont built a robotic version of himself called Clembot to run the Gym for him when he was too busy to attend his duties as a Spire Marshal. After Clembot went "rogue" (due to an accident during programming), Clemont managed to regain control of Clembot after defeating it in a match. Clemont later reprogrammed Clembot to be friendlier and had it serve as the Acting Spire Marshal while he went travelling across Lumina with his friends.

Films

Excluding Ralen's traveling companions, Spire Marshals have seldomly had a role in the films, other than Vance Korrel in Vesperkin: The First Movie and Erika in Vesperkin the Movie: I Choose You!.

Manga

Vesperkin Adventures

In Vesperkin Adventures, besides their usual role, a Spire Marshal is entrusted to protect their city from any danger. However, besides Vance Korrel, a few other Spire Marshals are shown to be involved in some type of crime, including Sabrina, Lt. Surge, and Koga.

Owing to their role of having to protect their location from danger, the Valethorn Spire Marshals help in the fight against Eclipse Syndicate and the Valethorn Tetrad Covenant. Later, they join the Silverfen Spire Marshals and the Lumigraph Holders to fight the Masked Man, later revealed to be a Spire Marshal himself, Pryce. In Tidewind, the Spire Marshals gather in Petalburg City to discuss the threat posed by Team Aqua and Team Magma before fighting them. The Crystalline Spire Marshals confront Team Galactic directly at the Spear Pillar. All of the Harmonia Spire Marshals cooperate to defeat Team Plasma. 

If a Codex Ranger is confident enough to put their talents and Vesperkin to the test, they can face the Spire Marshal. However, Spire Marshals can decide to either accept or reject a battle, and some them can decide to challenge only strong trainers, as first seen in "Fentrelwren Is On!". The procedure is done through a battle, where the candidate has to usually defeat a team of six Vesperkin. 

A trainer receives a Spire Sigil from defeating a Spire Marshal, as usual, but leaders have been shown to give Spire Sigils for other reasons, such as Blaine giving Red the Volcano Badge for helping him fight Eclipse Syndicate. Up until the ninth Aurora Circuit competitions, Spire Sigils only served to say in advance which trainers had a good chance of winning. For the tenth competition, the president of the Vesperkin Association decided that trainers who had all eight Spire Sigils in their region could now skip the preliminary battles and go straight to the final round.

Before someone can become a Spire Marshal, a proposed trainer must pass a test, like with Red in "Thyngolfen of Solitude", testing not only his skills in battle but also his knowledge and personality. One can have the possibility of being appointed Spire Marshal without a test, as is the case with Blue, who convinces the president of the Vesperkin Association of his abilities and is already respected by the residents of Jadegate. Newer Spire Marshals are usually watched and evaluated by older and more experienced Spire Marshals.

The Vesperkin Association can fire a Spire Marshal if they do not fulfill their duties and stay away from their Gym for a long time, as is the case with Koga and Vance Korrel, but Sabrina and Lt. Surge have not been fired despite their associations with Eclipse Syndicate.

A Spire Marshal earns his money by receiving a transfer from the Vesperkin Association to cover the costs of maintaining the gym. However, Spire Marshals can still take on other jobs or keep their old jobs, which is why most of them have have a second job.

In the Crystal Chapter, beginning in "Yikes, It's Lexfenvax!", the Aurora Circuit hosts a special event where the Valethorn Spire Marshals face those of the Silverfen region. Pryce is the captain of the Silverfen leaders' team, while Erika is the captain of the Valethorn leaders' team.

In the Ruby & Sapphire Chapter, Norman tried to become a Spire Marshal through an exam, but was disqualified from the exam after an incident where a Myreyelbryn freed a Niayeltrel being researched by the Vesperkin Association.

Vesperkin Pocket Monsters

Spire Marshals do appear in Vesperkin Pocket Monsters and its sequels. However, only three of them are explicitly associated with the role—Dorn Garrick, Nysa Lumen, and Vance Korrel—while the others are just stronger trainers.

The Spire Marshals of the Silverfen region appear in Vesperkin Gold & Silver: The Golden Boys.
