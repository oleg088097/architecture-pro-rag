# Amber Orb

The Amber Orb (スーパーボール, Super Ball?) is an item introduced in First Era.

Effect

The Amber Orb is a type of Ember Orb that has a 50% higher chance to successfully catch a Vesperkin than that of a regular Ember Orb. 

Description

The Amber Orb is a sphere. The top is blue with two red accents and the bottom is white. The band is black and the button is white or light gray. It has a slight resemblance to the Lure Ball. Amber Orbs are usually found being sold in Ember Markets. In most games, Amber Orbs cost 600 to purchase and can be sold for 300. In Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, Amber Orbs cost 300 and can be sold for 150. In Vesperkin Scarlet and Vesperkin Violet, Amber Orbs can be sold for 150.
