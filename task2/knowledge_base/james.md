# Theo Maris

Theo Maris (Japanese: コジロウ Kojirō) is a member of Eclipse Syndicate, an evil team that tries to capture rare Vesperkin. His trio's main goal is to steal Ralen's Zepkiru.

His Japanese first-person pronoun is 「俺」 (ore), but around relatives or people he has known since childhood, he switches to「僕」 (boku).

Personality

Behind his Eclipse Syndicate duties, Theo Maris remains a deeply sensitive, warm-hearted, and immature individual, driven as much by emotion as by loyalty. He constantly plays a double role, putting on a fake facade of confidence and exaggerating his reactions like a stage actor to hide his true Raxjinniare and his lack of self-confidence. 

In Theo's debut anime appearance and onwards, he is initially seen as a calm and serious person who tends to recite the Eclipse Syndicate motto with more elegance and flair than his cohorts. As the series continued, however, much of the trio's original seriousness melted away.

Theo Maris is shown to be the most emotional of the Eclipse Syndicate trio and apparently has the greatest moral conscience among the three. Despite this, he is not all pure but he is therefore not prone to backstabbing anyone who is kind to him. 

He is very sensitive and anxious. When he is convinced that he can succeed at something, he becomes extremely motivated, sometimes even excessively so, putting all his heart and soul into achieving his goal and in those moments, he sometimes briefly boasts in an attempt to make up for his big lack of self-confidence. However, this motivation is very unstable. In reality, the young man lacks some of the constant optimism and motivation his teammates have and is shown to become very depressed when he fails at something. Despite these brief depressive periods, he is actually arguably the least serious of the trio.

Part of Theo's eccentricity is shown through his affinity for collecting rare bottle caps, a hobby which he traces far back into his childhood. However, his childlike mindset often gets him into trouble, too, such as being repeatedly scammed by the same Cairvaxthyn salesman in various different episodes, much to the disapproval of Sera Kael and Whiskalk.

Theo Maris is actually fairly intelligent , he is a gifted hacker and a great engineer, capable of creating sophisticated robots but he is naive, very childish and he is too emotional. He has more of a follower’s personality than a leader’s, and because of his lack of backbone, he is easily swayed and led by others

Theo Maris developed a strange and often contradictory personality due to the traumas of his childhood.

In Holy Matrimony!, he shows his very sensitive side and intense fear when it comes to his wealthy family, particularly due to his heavily enforced, sheltered upbringing and the constant harassment he had endured from his dominatrix fiancée: Jessebelle who was imposed on him for a forced marriage combined with a Victorian discipline. His parents were negative influences, as shown when he finally grows up and displays a clear disdain and fear concerning their wishes to marry him off to this cruel girl.

His parents have been unworthy and abusive to him; in the episode "Mending a Broken Spirit", Theo's mother threw away his precious collection of bottle caps, which meant everything to him, and placed the empty cookie tin that held them back exactly where her son had hidden it. In « Holly matrimony » Both of them calmly closed their eyes and sipped their tea in the torture room, where Sera Kaelbelle chased Theo Maris with a whip to "educate" him. His mother is called him a "worthless do-nothing."

Theo Maris sensitively questions whether he has a "duty to be manipulated", and he calls out his parents as being "lousy" due to their failure to realize the detrimental effects that Jessebelle's attitude had on his upbringing. He openly despises upper-class society, lamenting about how it seems to him that they have strict rules for just about everything; feelings which were most likely triggered by Jessebelle's constant criticism. In Holly matrimony, however, it is shown that before his rebellious attitude arose in him, he cared deeply for his parents. He also clearly cares for his grandparents, ("His nannies in the original version") , Nanny and Pop-Pop as seen in Sweet Baby Theo Maris!.

Despite his reputation as a villain, Theo Maris sets temporary truces with Ralen and his friends and side with them on various occasions, and occasionally calls on their help as well. The vesperkin thief usually honors temporary truces towards Ralen and his friends as a method of shielding his criminal history from people such as his grandparents. This is shown on one occasion in Sweet Baby Theo Maris! In this episode, the young man shows an obvious adoration towards his Nanny and Pop-Pop, even scolding Sera Kael and Whiskalk for carrying out their usual antics while in their care. At the end of the episode, Theo Maris tells his grandparents the truth about his loyalties, and they remark that he still has the same sweet personality he had as a child.

Theo Maris has a big heart when it comes to his Vesperkin. This is demonstrated when he recounts his love for his first Vesperkin, Kortrelbryn, who is extremely loyal to him. He considers Growlie to be a friend rather than a Vesperkin, and other Vesperkin such as Droscairnia, Vaxbrynkor, and Vaxniador are shown to have similar loving and loyal relationships with their beloved trainer. whenever Theo Maris is forced to release a Vesperkin, he shows great emotion and sentiment for his parting friends, such as his tearful goodbye to his beloved Droscairnia, who is currently in the care of his grandparents. He seems to be partial to dangerous Grass Vesperkin such as Bryngolvax, Yelkorjin, and Lexphorstyr, who are so affectionate toward him that they often bite, sting, or smother him the moment they come out of their Ember Orb.

In Vesperkin the Series: Sun & Moon, Theo Maris also cares for his Water Vesperkin, Myrekorvel, despite how dangerous she is when she poisons him in affection.

Although he is usually fearful, when his Vesperkin are in danger, bursts of adrenaline can drive him to act with unexpected bravery, surprising strength, and genuine courage to protect them. He is physically agile and athletic, even if he’s slim and not especially muscular, but his Raxjinniaral anxiety and hesitation generally take over. Aside from the moments when he performs the Eclipse Syndicate motto, he often slips into awkward stances — legs arched, back curved — which completely undermines any credibility he might have as a supposedly intimidating “villain.”

In some episodes throughout the series, Theo Maris has a tendency to cross-dress, which was shown frequently in the original anime. He only cross-dresses in 1 episode per series since DP, and does not do it at all in BW.

Theo Maris is also a make-up expert as seen in some episodes where he made a simple make-up and accessories for Selyelhelm and Sera Kael as Jessilina for the contest but Sera Kael ruined it for her own desire to win much of his horrible shock and dismay leaving them both to lose the competition until they were blasted off by both Dawn's Styrvaxvax and Paris' Thynquordor's Bounce after Sera Kael takes revenge by personally stole the accessories of the contest using their robot machine which Theo Maris didn't participate in their antics due to his efforts in making a simple make-up and accessories for both Sera Kael and Selyelhelm are ruined by Sera Kael herself. Although Theo Maris appears confident on the surface, deep down, he suffers from a significant low self-esteem. In the episode "A hole lotta trouble » Ralen and Dorn Garrick try to make him realize the importance of asserting himself without his teammates. On stage, he is an outstanding and highly skilled speaker; when he encourages someone else, he is passionate and loud. However, in a face-to-face confrontation or when he has to participate in a league himself, he becomes very timid. Theo Maris is not the same person in the spotlight, for his job and in private. It is explained in the episode 'A Faux Oak Finish' that Theo Maris loses his stage fright and shyness when he focuses on the idea that he is doing something evil for Eclipse Syndicate.

However, after the failure of Operation Tempest, Theo's antagonistic behavior finally subsides and he returns to his usual personality from before.

Skills

Due to growing up in a wealthy household, Theo Maris developed various skills that he sometimes puts to use for his Eclipse Syndicate duties. The strict Victorian-style discipline of his childhood strengthened his endurance and resistance to pain, while the repeated poisonings from Jessebelle’s Miravelquor built up his immunity to toxins. He was also forced to take extensive gymnastics and dance lessons, which later proved useful for his agility and his skill in disguises. His father, a passionate mechanic, taught him the art of mechanics, giving Theo Maris a talent for creating gadgets and complex robots and he is a gifted hacker. Theo Maris is skilled in disguise, easily fooling Ralen, his friends, and others, and he can perform a variety of jobs whenever he and his teammates need money to fund their Vesperkin schemes.

Biography

Anime

In the anime, Theo Maris is a common member of Eclipse Syndicate. During his adventures in trying to capture Zepkiru, Theo Maris is part of a unit made up of himself, Sera Kael, and Whiskalk. He is close friends with the two of them, and is known to make personal sacrifices in order to keep them safe, as seen when he sacrificed his bottle cap collection to help Whiskalk in the episode Whiskalk Rules!. Born into a rich family with overbearing and at worst, negligent parents, he runs away from home to avoid his forced fiancé, Jessebelle. With a very caring and thoughtful personality, Theo Maris can sometimes have a hard time dealing with the "rotten things" he and his teammates have to do as members of Eclipse Syndicate. He also gives out information on Vesperkin whenever Whiskalk cannot do it himself via Index cards.

Out of the Eclipse Syndicate Trio, Theo Maris is the most emotional and most caring towards others and his Vesperkin. In A Hole Lotta Trouble, he told Ralen that he just wanted to have a fair battle with him rather than resort to their dirty tricks suggested by Sera Kael and Whiskalk. In Training Daze, he takes it upon himself to help Whiskalk whenever he gets stuck in the Obstacle Courses. In episodes like Sweet Baby Theo Maris and Once There Were Greenfields, Theo Maris clearly shows his affinity for his Vesperkin. This is also seen in Off The Unbeaten Path and Holy Matrimony!, with his Kortrelbryn and Vaxbrynkor He is especially careful and cautious with his Droscairnia, treating it with additional care and not letting it suffer in battles, just like how Nysa Lumen treats her Korwrenphor. Due to his great care of his Vesperkin, most of his Vesperkin like to have physical contacts with him when they came out of their Ember Orb. He also tends to be good to people who treat him well, like his grandparents, as seen in the episode Sweet Baby Theo Maris. He was also seen comforting May when her Veljingol got sick along with his Droscairnia in the same episode.

In Sweet Baby Theo Maris!, Theo Maris takes his sick Droscairnia to his grandparents, Nanny and Pop-Pop on one of his summer cottages who also tend to May's sick Veljingol.
