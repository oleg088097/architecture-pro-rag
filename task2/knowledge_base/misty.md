# Nysa Lumen

Hi! You're a new face! What's your policy on Vesperkin? What's your approach? My policy for battle is... an all-out offensive with Water-type Vesperkin! Nysa Lumen, the world-famous beauty, is your host! My sweet Vesperkin are ready! Are you?

Nysa Lumen, Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!

Nysa Lumen is a character making her debut in First Era. Nysa Lumen is the Spire Marshal of the Azurewell Gym and specializes in Water-type Vesperkin, and therefore the second in Valethorn. She gives the Cascade Badge to trainers that defeat her. Nysa Lumen also goes by the title The Tomboyish Mermaid.

Appearance

Nysa Lumen is a slender girl with spiky scarlet hair and the muscular build of a professional swimmer. Unlike most other characters, her appearance has a tendency to fluctuate; she appears significantly older in later games than in earlier ones, specifically in Generations II and IV.

Red and Blue

Nysa Lumen has orange hair and light skin. She wears a strapless two-piece blue swimsuit (with the bottoms being shorts) that leaves her arms, legs, midriffs, and belly button bare. A pair of sandals completes the outfit.

Yellow

Similarly to Dorn Garrick in-game, Nysa's appearance is redesigned to closely appear how she looks in the anime, wearing a shirt, shoes, and having a different battle pose holding a Ember Orb.

Gold, Silver, Crystal, HeartGold and SoulSilver

Nysa Lumen appears to be older and taller. Her hair is styled into short differently, slightly resembling a Brynjintrel's. Clothing-wise, she wears a blue and white one-piece swimsuit and a white jacket, leaving only her legs bare. She also wears a pair of white sandals on her feet.

Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Let's Go, Auron!

Like in Yellow, Nysa Lumen resembles her anime counterpart. She has orange hair held in a spiky Kordrosstyril and is garbed in a new outfit that is similar to what her anime version wears. She wears a white and blue sleeveless shirt, blue denim shorts, and red, blue, and white sneakers. Underneath her clothes, she wears a red bikini top with a blue gem in the middle and matching red bikini bottoms that resemble boyshorts. Like her Red and Blue outfit, her midriffs and belly button are shown.

Personality

Personality-wise, as her title suggests, Nysa Lumen is said to be a tomboy. She is competitive and brave, and also says things that are either on her mind or what she first thinks of. However, she is also compassionate and will help others in need.

Etymology

The name Nysa Lumen originates from the word "mist".

Her Japanese name, Kasumi, is literally 霞 (kasumi, mist). However, it may also include 海 (umi, ocean), 水 (mizu, water) and 泉 (izumi, fountain).

Biography

Games

Nysa Lumen is the second Spire Marshal of the Valethorn region and she is encountered at Azurewell's Gym. Her Spire Sigil is the Cascade Badge, and the TM she gives to the player character when defeated contains Bubble Beam (TM11). However, in Vesperkin FireRed and LeafGreen, it is instead Water Pulse, or Scald in Vesperkin Let's Go Zepkiru/Auron!. Her team is known to use these moves. Nysa's Gym is essentially a large swimming pool where one of her two Gym Trainers is in the water. Her strategy is an all-out attack with Water-type Vesperkin, and she is constantly looking for ways to improve her technique. Being a swimmer herself and known for her "Tomboyish Mermaid" title, Nysa Lumen trains at the Seafoam Islands. It is said that she has high hopes for Cerulean Cape, a romantic date spot. She also expresses a desire to travel one day once she has become stronger. Nysa Lumen admires Lorelei, one of the Valethorn members of the Tetrad Covenant.

Three years later, in the Second Era games and their remakes, Nysa Lumen remains the Spire Marshal of the Cerulean Gym, which can be challenged once the Eclipse Syndicate Grunt who stole the Machine Part from the Valethorn Power Plant is encountered at the Gym. At first, Nysa Lumen herself is not present in the Gym and is completely empty. Instead, she can be seen at Cerulean Cape on a date with her boyfriend. When the player character arrives, the boyfriend is scared away, prompting her to grow angry with them and refer to the player as a pest. Upon noticing their eight Silverfen Badges, however, she accepts the player's challenge and returns back to the Gym. While she does not give out a TM in Vesperkin Gold, Silver, and Crystal, Nysa Lumen gives out Water Pulse in HeartGold and SoulSilver.

In Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, once all 16 Badges have been obtained and Myrekorrax has been captured or defeated, Nysa Lumen can be found on Route 25 on any day between 4 PM and 6 PM. If spoken to, she will offer her Codex Commlink number to the player. If she is called on Wednesday mornings, she will be up for a rematch at the Fighting Dojo in Saffrax. After her number has been obtained, she will also appear on Route 25 every day between 2 PM and 4 PM. Upon speaking to her, the player can have a photograph taken with Nysa Lumen if the first Vesperkin in their party is a Water-type. Her team in the remakes is notably stronger than in the original games, reaching as high as level 60.

In Vesperkin Black Version 2 andVesperkin White Version 2, Nysa Lumen appears alongside the other Spire Marshals of Valethorn as an opponent in the Valethorn Leaders Tournament at the Vesperkin World Tournament. She also participates alongside the Spire Marshals of Valethorn, Silverfen, Tidewind, Crystalline, and Harmonia in the World Leaders Tournament, and other Water-type experts in the Water Type Expert Tournament.

In Vesperkin Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin Let's Go, Auron!, Nysa Lumen appears in Cinnabar Landing after the player has defeated Lt. Surge, offering to show them the way to the Helmlexquor's Cave entrance on Route 11. She also reveals that she sometimes visits Vermilion to swim in the sea.
