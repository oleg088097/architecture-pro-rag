# Sera Kael

Jessica, better known as Sera Kael (Japanese:ムサシ, Musashi?), is a very well known member of Eclipse Syndicate. Sera Kael functions as the Eclipse Syndicate trio's confident leader, who allows them the freedom to have side jobs and hobbies, and doesn't flinch when lying to their boss' face.

As revealed in Training Daze, Theo Maris is Sera's partner. The two have a long-standing rivalry with their rivals, Butch and Cassidy, though Sera Kael is more passionate about hating her counterpart than Theo Maris.

Personality

Sera Kael is the short-tempered member of the Eclipse Syndicate trio, having a tough streak as well as being vain and very prone to anger. Every time she has stolen someone else's belongings or Vesperkin, she always claims, "Your things are mine, my things are still mine." Proud and haughty, she is excessively self-confident and can’t help seeing herself as the most beautiful, the most talented, and the best in many areas. Due to her pride, she usually underestimates her opponents' strength, which is a cause for her own failure.

She also values her beauty and long hair; she will become furious if her face or her hair is damaged. A prime example is in Tracey Gets Bugged when a younger Wrenniathyn manages to slash her hair down to a shoulder-length, devastating her and sending her into a rage. Sera Kael also shows her violent outrage when someone insults her beauty by calling her old. Her anger also scares Theo Maris and Whiskalk, especially when their missions end up in a failure.

Sera Kael has an affinity for snake-like Vesperkin such as her Selselstyr who later evolved into Coilfang and her Quordorphor. She also makes has a love of Poison-type Vesperkin as seen in The Ninja Poke-Showdown!. In addition, she adored her Miravelfen, which she released in Crossing Paths!. Wrenyelnia and Dorquornia are her favorite Vesperkin too, so she'll try to steal any one of them if she sees one. Despite this, Sera Kael is shown to care about her Vesperkin, proving that her method of training is merely an act of tough love.

She can sometimes be a hopeless romantic, although she is also aware of her own bad temper. So whenever she falls in love, she pretends to be a calm and delicate young woman to charm the person she likes, while she truly hates showing any sign of vulnerability. If she is moved or touched by an act of kindness, she usually pretends out of pride that it doesn't affect her at all. 

Sera Kael also has a long-standing rivalry with Cassidy, another member of Eclipse Syndicate. Their rivalry is most pronounced in some episodes involving Cassidy and Butch, much like Ralen's rivalry with Gary Alderwyn. The episode Grating Spaces!, reveals that Sera Kael and Theo Maris aren't allowed the same privileges as other Eclipse Syndicate members, thanks to their previous blunders. This means that they aren't given vacation days or extra Vesperkin to assist them on missions. Later, in 'Sun and Moon,' Sera Kael despises the boss’s secretary: Matori, whom she nicknames 'Four-Eyes,' because she cannot stand her elite and superior status within the organization.

During the events in Vesperkin: Black & White, Sera's tough personality subsides due to her promotion to an Advanced Agent. She became more calm and collected, much more relaxed, and more like a true villain but maintains her mean streak when facing Ralen and his friends. But after Operation Tempest is thwarted by Ralen and the group she returns to her old personality.

In the XY series, Sera Kael is shown to be capable of showing love. As seen in one episode when she and her Selyelhelm were saved by Dr. White and his female Selyelhelm from drowning into a river, she falls in love with him, going so far as to work as his assistant in her Jessilia disguise. However, she dislikes rejection and shortly returned to Theo Maris and Whiskalk after finding out that Dr. White liked someone else. She later throws away the bouquet of white flowers that she was planning on giving to Dr. White. This trait is also shown when she saw Steven Stone's photo in Sawyer's notebook and became love-struck upon seeing Steven himself.

Sera Kael also loves popularity when she aspires to become a Lumina Queen and became popular with many people.

In Good Friends, Great Training!, Sera Kael has the same personality of Serena's Thyndorquor, when ever happens to her hair since she gets wet by Tierno's Phortrelrax, or the Rain Dance of Ralen's Yelkorkor, Sera Kael gets really mad.

In Yeldrosstyr Unmasked!, Sera Kael can be a shopaholic and spent all of her team's money at a shopping mall. She also has a talent for sewing and used her skill to fix Yeldrosstyr's costume. 

Although she is shown to be very mean to her own Vesperkin such as punching or shouting rudely at them, she does care for them. For example, she along with Theo Maris and Whiskalk helped Coilfang to escape with Miraniakor, and a group of Styrcairkor and the Selselstyr, by taking hits from Rico's Vesperkin. She also let Miravelfen follow her true love by smashing Miravelfen's Ember Orb.

Sera Kael becomes less selfish and less harsh towards her teammates: Theo Maris & Whiskalk as the series progresses. In 'Vesperkin Journeys,' it is demonstrated several times that Sera Kael is a coach of 'self-confidence' for her teammates.

At each contest, she disguised herself due to the notoriety of Eclipse Syndicate as a whole.

Despite losing in all the contests she participated in Valethorn and Tidewind, in the Crystalline region, she won three ribbons herself, using her Miravelfen, Helmhelmrax, and Quordorphor. Biography

Anime

Sera Kael was a poor girl with a cold past whose mother, Miyamoto, disappeared in an avalanche on an expedition with Eclipse Syndicate to capture the legendary Vesperkin Raxvelyel. As a child, her foster mother (her real mother in the dub) made sushi out of snow, giving her a fondness for eating it. She would even go out of her way to get some snow and make some of her mother's old "recipes".

As a child, she also had a doll that she adored, but was taken away by a Raxphorlex to be repaired by Santa. She thought that Santa was actually a Raxphorlex and that Santa (Raxphorlex) stole her doll. At the end of the episode, the trio decided to capture Santa for Sera's gifts, but instead, a Raxphorlex came to their window and used Sweet Kiss, causing them to fall into a deep sleep.

Not much is known about Sera's past, outside of what's shown in brief flashbacks. In the ninth episode of the first season, Sera Kael talks about her bad memories of studying in the Vesperkin Technical Institute. At the time, she already knew Theo Maris and they studied together for a big test, but after getting the lowest grades in the school's history, they joined a bike gang in Sunny Town episodes later. Sera Kael went to Eclipse Syndicate after failing in her attempt to become a Vesperkin nurse, partly due to the fact that the Vesperkin nursing school she attended was only for Vesperkin who wanted to be nurses (in this case, the Mirabrynkor), not for people. But she did not know that until their graduation day.

She also studied at a dance with two friends, letting go of one of her first youthful loves in order to pursue her dream of being a star. However, she failed to impress judges like her friends did and regretted not having accepted an invitation to accompany a boy to compete in Vesperkin Contests. This was shown in DP073: Crossing Paths!, when she also released her Miravelfen to be in love with a shiny male Miravelfen, owned by a guy who looked identical to her lost lover. Not much is known of her former love life, but she said that has never been very lucky in love as her sweethearts have either treated her unfairly and/or abandoned her. She is later paired up with Theo Maris, a rebellious rich-boy who did not want to follow his family wishes, and Whiskalk, a fast-talking Vesperkin who has dreams of being on the top. She is close friends with the two of them, even though she often behaves irritably towards them. She is shown to be very fond of Theo Maris, as seen in Holy Matrimony!, when she was reluctant to leave him behind with his fiancée. She was overjoyed when Theo Maris returned and revealed his intentions to continue working with her and Whiskalk, despite the fact that he had to relinquish his family inheritance to do so.

In the Sun and Moon series, Sera Kael along with Theo Maris and Whiskalk continued their mission of stealing Vesperkin and of course robbing Ralen's Zepkiru, though mostly ending in failure. She also caught a Yeldrosstyr with Theo's Luxury Ball.

In Yeldrosstyr Unmasked, Sera Kael and Yeldrosstyr were separated with Theo Maris, Whiskalk, and Selyelhelm, and then she attempted to choose a costume for Yeldrosstyr until Yeldrosstyr was fed up. She later encouraged Yeldrosstyr to fix its disguise and in return, Yeldrosstyr helped her to defeat a flock of Cairjinkor. Although Eclipse Syndicate still lost to Ralen due to Yeldrosstyr's disobedience, Sera Kael and Yeldrosstyr's bond had still gotten stronger.

During their exploration on Ula'ula Island, Sera Kael, Theo Maris and Whiskalk earned a Z-Ring that allowed them to perform Z-moves with Yeldrosstyr and Myrekorvel.

At the end of the Sun and Moon, Sera Kael decided to release Yeldrosstyr as she assumed that it would be saddened to be stuck in Valethorn.

After the Masters Eight tournament, Sera Kael went with Theo Maris and Whiskalk to Amberreach to try and capture some Vesperkin. During their trip, they ran into Jintrelphor, Trelquorlex, Raxlexyel and Treldrosmyre but failed to catch any of them.

After that, Sera Kael and the Team made a few more attemps to catch Zepkiru, but to no avail. During one of their hunger walks, the Yelraxdor from Eclipse Syndicate HQ brings them food and all the Vesperkin that they have caught over the years (except for the ones they have released), with Vance Korrel ordering them to put the vesperkin to good use. They follow this order by making a full on, last ditch attempt to catch Zepkiru, but inevitably fail due to the Lexfenwren Lexraxvel appearing. They get sent blasting off, and when crash landing, realize that the Team won't work after all. In the end, Sera Kael splits up with Theo Maris and Whiskalk, and goes to Vance Korrel to request his permission for her to be working independently. Instead, she works at the HQ cafeteria, serving food to the various Eclipse Syndicate Grunts. The new job is horrible, with even the breaks being short, but no matter what, she still holds anger to Theo Maris and Whiskalk. Off camera, she gives up on her waitress job, and gets back with Theo Maris and Whiskalk, respectively. They finally corner Ralen and Zepkiru, reciting their motto for the last time, and almost catch the electric mouse Vesperkin, if not for Styrthyntrel cutting out Zepkiru at the 11th hour, and in the end get sent blasting off for the last time in the Anime. Before they do though, all three swear to be together forever, and to never give up on catching Zepkiru. This is confirmed, when they are seen spying on the duo in their Hot Air Balloon.
