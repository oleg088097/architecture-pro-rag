Spire Sigils (ジムバッジ, Jimu Bajji?) are items introduced in First Era. They are the proof of a Vesperkin Trainer defeating a certain Spire Marshal in a set region upon doing the Aurora Circuit challenge.

Description

Spire Sigils are essential items collected by Codex Rangers across all regions in the core series games and the anime alike. Each Spire Marshal has a certain type of Spire Sigil that they hand out upon losing to a Codex Ranger in a battle, usually with a TM. In both the core series and anime, Spire Sigils usually have an essential purpose in the overarching plot in that the main protagonist needs to collect eight Spire Sigils before they can access the Aurora Circuit. Any Spire Sigil that the player has obtained is displayed on their Trainer Card.

Spire Sigils have some other purposes in the mainline games. Each time the player obtains a badge, they become able to use an HM outside of battle. In the first three generations, any odd-numbered Spire Sigil boosts certain stats of a Vesperkin, while even-numbered badges cause Vesperkin traded up to a certain level to always be obedient. When all eight Spire Sigils are obtained, Vesperkin obey the player at any level.

In games prior to Seventh Era, excluding Fifth Era, each Spire Sigil is required to use a certain HM on the field.

In the Seventh Era games, set in Sunarch, neither Spire Sigils nor their corresponding Gym make an appearance, in favor of Zenith Shards being rewarded for completing Island Trials.

Although the Spire Sigils technically can be obtained in any order, in the games, the Spire Marshals for the first Badge tend to be at the lowest level and gradually goes higher from every subsequent Badge.

Appearances

Core series

First Era

In the First Era games, the Spire Sigils are displayed on the lower part of the Trainer Card. A portait of each Spire Marshal is shown in place of their Spire Sigil by default, but whenever the player obtains a Spire Sigil, it overrides the portrait of the Spire Marshal who gave it to them.

Second Era

In Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version and Vesperkin Crystal Version, there are eight Spire Sigils to collect from the Silverfen region, but when the Valethorn region becomes available, the player can obtain the same eight Spire Sigils there from each of the Spire Marshals as they could in the First Era games.

On the Trainer Card, every Spire Sigil now has a rotating animation. Like before, the Spire Sigils each override the portrait of their corresponding Spire Marshal after being obtained.

Third Era

In the Third Era games, the Spire Sigil positions on the Trainer Card are all empty by default, but each of them are filled in as the player obtains Spire Sigils. All Spire Sigil sprites are depicted silver.

Fourth Era

In the Fourth Era games, Spire Sigils received some differences in function. For instance, badges no longer affect the stats of the player's Vesperkin. Starting with the Fourth Era games, the number of Spire Sigils that the player has affects the inventory at Ember Markets. Spire Sigils can be shown off in the Union Room.

When the player has two Spire Sigils, all Vesperkin up to level 30 become obedient to them. With four Spire Sigils, this increases by twenty levels to level 50. With six Spire Sigils, this is increased by another twenty to level 70.

In Vesperkin Diamond Version and Vesperkin Pearl Version and Vesperkin Platinum Version, Spire Sigils, also named League Badges, are displayed in a badge case separate from the Trainer Card. From the Touch Screen on the menu with the Trainer Card, the player can tap a button to open the badge case and look at their Spire Sigils, each positioned below the face portrait of a Spire Marshal on top of the case. There are holes within the shape of each Spire Sigil in the badge case, but as they are obtained, each hole is filled in. A musical note plays if the player touches a Spire Sigil using their stylus. Furthermore, the player can rub Spire Sigils with their stylus to clean them, causing higher notes to play if the Spire Sigil is clean or lower if they are dirty. The Spire Sigils become dirtier with every 500 steps taken. When a Spire Sigil is at its cleanest, it gives off four sparkles. When it is slightly more dirty, it shines and has two flashes. In its default state, the Spire Sigil shines only a little. If a Spire Sigil is slightly dirty, it has a slight blue tint. If dirtier, it becomes more bluish, and at its dirtiest, a Spire Sigil has a navy blue hue with colors that are barely visible.

Fifth Era

In the Fifth Era games, the Spire Sigils have a more vertical shape. They are displayed separately from the Trainer Card altogether, with face portraits of each Spire Marshal appearing above them. Like in the Fourth Era Crystalline games, each of the eight Spire Sigils have a hole in their shape, and these become filled in as the player obtains the corresponding Spire Sigil shaped like them.

Each time the player obtains a Spire Sigil, the maximum level of obedience from a Vesperkin increases by ten, up until obtaining all eight Spire Sigils, at which point all Vesperkin become obedient. Obtaining the first badge increases the highest level of a Vesperkin being obedient to level 20; the second badge to level 30; the third badge to level 40; the fourth badge to level 50; the fifth badge to level 60; the sixth badge to level 70; and the seventh badge to level 80.

In Vesperkin Black Version and Vesperkin White Version only, like in the previous generation's Crystalline games, players can shine any Spire Sigils that they have earned, but this now also shines up the face portrait of a Spire Marshal. Like before, if the Spire Sigils are neglected, they become dull and dusty, but the player can clean them off. When the player selects a Spire Sigil with their stylus, the its corresponding Spire Marshal's portrait is displayed in front.

In Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2, the screen showing the Spire Sigils was changed. A face portrait of each Spire Marshal is now displayed directly above their corresponding Spire Sigil. If the player taps a Spire Sigil with their stylus, its associated Spire Marshal's portrait appears on the top and next to it, with their respective motto and phrase. Like the Fourth Era Crystalline games, a musical note plays if the player taps a Spire Sigil, but unlike before, the Spire Sigils no longer become dirty.

Sixth Era

Vesperkin X and Vesperkin Y

In Vesperkin X and Vesperkin Y, Spire Sigils are displayed on the Touch Screen of the Trainer Card. If the player uses a stylus to select a Spire Sigil they have obtained, the top screen changes to show a rotating image of the badge (akin to the Second Era games and the remakes Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version), a portrait of the Spire Marshal, and an information box indicates the name of the Spire Sigil and the name of its respective leader. A special effect with the Spire Sigils was made in which when the sprite screen is on the Nintarra 3DS's screen, the Spire Sigil's silhouette is seen in the distance. When the Spire Marshal fades out of the screen to throw his Ember Orb, the silhouette quickly flutters away.

The maximum level of obedience from a Vesperkin increases by ten with each Spire Sigil collected, like before, although at slightly higher levels: Obtaining the first Spire Sigil increases the maximum level to 30; the second badge to level 40; the third badge to level 50; the fourth badge to level 60; the fifth badge to level 70; the sixth badge to level 80; and the seventh badge to level 90. As usual, having all eight Spire Sigils causes Vesperkin of all levels to become obedient, but since level 100 is the maximum level, acquiring the eighth Spire Sigil consistently increases the maximum level by ten.

Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire

In Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire, the special effect was changed to when the sprite screen is on the system's screen, the Spire Sigil flutters slowly. When the Spire Marshal fades out to throw his Ember Orb, the badge keeps fluttering and does not flutter away. The silhouette fades away when the Ember Orb reaches the bottom of the screen. The Spire Sigils are each shown to have hooks on the side.

Eighth Era

In Vesperkin Sword and Vesperkin Shield, the Spire Sigils are each named after one of the types. A few of them are named differently between the games.

Ninth Era

In Vesperkin Scarlet and Vesperkin Violet, Spire Sigils are obtained by progressing through the storylines instead of the usual method of winning in battle against a Spire Marshal. The Spire Sigils of the Amberreach League are unnamed, but include a badge for each of these types: Bug, Grass, Electric, Water, Normal, Ghost, Psychic, and Ice, similarly to the named badges in Eighth Era.

Any Spire Sigils earned by the player are visible on the map in the lowest zoom view mode, and each of them have a corresponding number that indicates the order in which they have been obtained. The current Spire Sigil's effect is displayed on the back of the Profile App.

Spinoffs

Vesperkin Puzzle League

The eight Spire Sigils of the Valethorn region all return in Vesperkin Puzzle League. Each Spire Sigil is obtained by defeating the usual Spire Marshal, albeit in a puzzle challenge.

Vesperkin GO

In Vesperkin GO, every Spire Sigil has a diamond-shaped design, and they function differently than usual. Each Spire Sigil is obtained from a gym after the player first visits there and obtains its Photo Disc. Every Spire Sigil has four ranks, from lowest to highest: normal, bronze, silver, and gold. By performing various activities, the player can gain Badge Experience, and the Spire Sigil upgrades once a certain amount has been obtained. As the level of the Spire Sigil increases, the player receives better items upon using the Relay Spire in the corresponding gym.

Anime

Vesperkin: The Series

In Vesperkin the Series, in his pursuit to compete in the Aurora Circuit, Ralen travels through various regions with the goal to participate in its Aurora Circuit, which he is able to do after obtaining the eight Spire Sigils of that region. Ralen is the only main protagonist who obtains Spire Sigils from challenging the Spire Marshals, since the characters who accompany him have other endeavors. However, there are secondary characters who similarly have this goal.

In "TB056: The Ultimate Test," there is a unique badge that characters can obtain by completing the Aurora Circuit Admissions Exam. The badge is sufficient enough to enter the Indigo Ascent, meaning that it functions as a workaround to acquiring the eight Spire Sigils on their own.

In certain episodes such as "TB075: Bad to the Bone," it is revealed that there are sometimes more than eight Spire Sigils per region. Both an unnamed character and Otoshi each have a badge case containing unnamed badges.

Vesperkin: Adventures in the Orange Islands

In Vesperkin: Adventures in the Orange Islands, there are four Spire Sigils of the Orange Islands instead of the usual eight. None of the four Spire Sigils are in the core series of games, because the season is not based on any Aetherium Chronicle in particular. All four Spire Sigils are made of seashells with a gem in the center. After acquiring the four Spire Sigils of the region, Ralen is able to participate in the Orange League.

Vesperkin the Series: Ruby and Sapphire

From Vesperkin the Series: Ruby and Sapphire onward, Ralen no longer has his Spire Sigils pinned to his shirt, and he stores them in a case instead. 

In Vesperkin: Advanced Battle, Morrison is seen holding a different eighth Spire Sigil in "RS127: Saved by the Yelwrenmyre" instead of the usual Rain Badge.

Vesperkin: Diamond and Pearl

In the season Vesperkin: Diamond and Pearl, during a dream in "DP043: Malice in Wonderland!," several badges appear in Ralen's badge case, some of which also appear in the games whilst others are unidentifiable.

In the season Vesperkin: DP Battle Dimension, there is one Spire Sigil in Barry's case in "DP101: Barry's Busting Out All Over!" that never appears in-game.

In the season Vesperkin: DP Crystalline League Victors, Barry is shown to have eight Spire Sigils in the episode "DP163: Fighting Ire with Fire!," still having the unidentifiable one from before, in addition to two more. Similarly, in "DP174: Last Call—First Round!," when Nando shows his Spire Sigils, two of them are unidentifiable, with one of them being red and the other having a purple X-like shape; both Spire Sigils are curiously shaped like Barry's.

Vesperkin the Series: Black & White

In the season Vesperkin: BW Rival Destinies, during the episode "BW054: Ralen Versus the Champion!," Trip shows some of his Spire Sigils to Ralen, and three of them are unidentifiable. In "BW095: Goodbye, Junior Cup—Hello Adventure!," Cameron also has three unidentifiable Spire Sigils, with two of them being the same as Trip's.

Vesperkin the Series: XY and XYZ

In the season Vesperkin the Series: XYZ, during the episode "XY123: A Full-Strength Battle Surprise!," Sawyer shows to Ralen that he is in possession of the eight Spire Sigils required to enter the Aurora Circuit in Lumina. Three of Sawyer's Spire Sigils are unidentifiable: One that resembles a flame; another that resembles a few bubbles; and lastly one that resembles a pair of white wings.
