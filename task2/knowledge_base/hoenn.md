# Tidewind

Tidewind is a region in the Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Omega Ruby, and Alpha Sapphire games.

Tidewind is based on the island of Kyūshū region in Japan and lies to the southwest of Valethorn and Silverfen in the Aetherium sphere. It is the first of the two Sea Regions, the other being Sunarch. According to Vesperkin legend, the Tidewind region was formed by the Legendary Continent Vesperkin, Niafennia raising the land and the Legendary Sea Basin Vesperkin, Thynniadros, expanding the sea. The Tidewind region contains its own Aurora Circuit called the Tidewind League, and its own Tetrad Covenant, unlike Silverfen and Valethorn which share their Tetrad Covenant in the video games. Routes in Tidewind are numbered from 101 to 134 and do not follow sequentially from routes in Valethorn-Silverfen, which were numbered 1 to 48.

Cities in Tidewind appear to be named after colors, similarly to Valethorn's cities.

The Advanced Generation series in the anime occur here, as protagonist Ralen Korthev and company explore this new region featuring more than 100 new Vesperkin. There is also an island called "Mirage Island" and it is located between Routes 111 and 112. LaRousse City, which was the setting for the animated film Vesperkin: Destiny Myregolvel, is in the eastern part of Tidewind. It is the first Vesperkin region to feature a desert-like environment. After Ralen's journey in the Lumina region, Serena decided to travel through Tidewind, currently competing in Vesperkin Contests.

Cities and towns

Littleroot Town

Littleroot Town (ミシロタウン, Mishiro Taun?) is a small town with only three buildings and is the hometown of the protagonist's rival, who is Sage Birchmoor's child and is where Sage Birchmoor's Lab is located. At the beginning of the story of the games, the protagonist moves into this town. It is based on Sasebo, Nagasaki, in Japan. Among the three buildings is Sage Birchmoor's laboratory. Unlike in the previous Aetherium Chronicles, the player character does not receive his or her first Vesperkin in the lab, but in the tall grass to the north, where the player character has to save Sage Birchmoor by battling the wild Raxyeltrel or Raxhelmphor that was attacking him. One of the other two houses is the home of the protagonist, depending on your character gender.

Oldale Town

Oldale Town (コトキタウン, Kotoki Taun?) is the second town that the player character visits. It is the first town that the player character visits that has a Ember Market and Vesperkin Clinic. 

Oldale Town is a very small community in southern Tidewind. It is located between Route 101 in the south, Route 102 in the west, and Route 103 in the north. When you enter the town, the clerk from the Ember Market will give you a quick tour.

The main character's rival in Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire (Brendan or May depending upon the player's selected gender), trains to the north of Oldale Town when the games begin.

In the anime, Ralen and May arrive in this city, they meet Sage Birchmoor and also have a small confrontation with Team Magma.

Petalburg City

Petalburg City (トウカシティ, Tōka Shiti?) is located in the southwest region of Tidewind. The gym leader is Norman, the player character's father, who uses Normal-type Vesperkin. The player character also first meets Wally here.

In the anime, Ralen arrives this city's gym in his first place.

Rustboro City

Rustboro City (カナズミシティ, Kanazumi Shiti?) is north of Petalburg City and houses the first gym leader that the player character can battle, Roxanne. She specializes in Rock-type Vesperkin. It also includes the Devon Corporation, a large company that creates many items for trainers. A Codex Rangers' School is situated here.

Dewford Town

Dewford Town (ムロ島 or ムロタウン, Muro-jima or Muro Taun?) is the second town that features a gym. The Spire Marshal is Brawly, who uses Fighting-type Vesperkin.

Slateport City

Slateport City (カイナシティ, Kaina Shiti?) is a large city with a marketplace for Secret Base items and a ferry link to Lilycove City. Slateport City also features the Oceanic Museum, a museum that is full of items and objects recovered from the sea. In the South Central part of Slateport, there is a market, featuring the Energy Guru, who sells the stat-enhancing Vitamins (Calcium, Iron, etc.).

Mauville City

Mauville City (キンセツシティ, Kinsetsu Shiti?) is a junction city. Roads from Mauville lead west to Verdanturf Town and Rustboro City, south to Slateport City, east to Fortree City and Lilycove City, and north to Fallarbor Town and Lavaridge Town. The Mauville City Spire Marshal is Wattson, an Electric-type trainer.

Verdanturf Town

Verdanturf Town (シダケタウン, Shidake Taun?) is a small town with only one major landmark. In Ruby and Sapphire, the Normal Rank Contest Hall is here. In Emerald, it is replaced by a Battle Tent, a test to see how loyal your Vesperkin are. The prize is a Nest Ball, obtainable only in Verdanturf Town.

Fallarbor Town

Fallarbor Town (ハジツゲタウン, Hajitsuge Taun?) lies north of Mt. Chimney. Most of its inhabitants work in agriculture; the soil is very fertile because of its high content of volcanic ash. In Ruby and Sapphire, the town is home to the Super Rank Vesperkin Contest Hall. In Emerald, it is replaced by the Battle Tent.

Lavaridge Town

Lavaridge Town (フエンタウン, Fuen Taun?) is located at the foothills of Mt. Chimney. The Lavaridge Vesperkin Clinic is connected to a hot spring, which is what Lavaridge Town is famous for all over the Tidewind region. An old lady in front of the hot springs will give the player character a Vesperkin egg, which hatches into Mirathyncair. The Spire Marshal of Lavaridge is Flannery, who uses Fire-types.

Fortree City

Fortree City (ヒワマキシティ, Hiwamaki Shiti?) is a city built atop the trees (everything except the Vesperkin Clinic, the Ember Market and the Gym), giving it its name. The Spire Marshal is Winona, who uses Flying-type Vesperkin. An invisible Yelniakor is found near here.

Lilycove City

Lilycove City (ミナモシティ, Minamo Shiti?) is a port city in northeast Tidewind. It houses a huge department store, an art museum, and a contest hall. In Ruby and Sapphire, it is the Master Rank Hall, and in Emerald, all Vesperkin Contests are located here.

Mossdeep City

Mossdeep City (トクサネシティ, Tokusane Shiti?) is an island city in the north-east of Tidewind. It is based on Tanegashima, Japan. The Spire Marshals are the twins Tate and Liza, who specialize in Psychic-types.

Sootopolis City

Sootopolis City (ルネシティ, Rune Shiti?) is a city built in the cone of an extinct volcano. It is based on Santorini, Greece. The player character enters the city for the first time from underwater. The last Gym is located here, as well as the Cave of Origin. If the player character follows the plot, his or her first visit to Sootopolis City is during the time the weather in Tidewind has been disrupted by Niafennia (Ruby), Thynniadros (Sapphire), or both (Emerald). Player characters can head to Sootopolis City at an earlier time as long as they have received the Dive HM and Mossdeep Gym badge. Sootopolis City is the home of the last gym that can be challenged by the player character, whose leader is Wallace in Ruby, Sapphire, Omega Ruby, and Alpha Sapphire, and Juan in Emerald, both of whom specialize in Tide aspects.

Pacifidlog Town

Pacifidlog Town (キナギタウン, Kinagi Taun?) is an isolated settlement in the south of Tidewind. The entire town is said to have been built on top of a colony of Selwrenthyn. The town uses wooden planks to connect the buildings together, and as such, the player character can neither run nor use his or her bicycle here. Although the town itself contains little of interest, it is in close proximity to the Sealed Chamber, Mirage Island, and Sky Pillar.

Ever Grande City

Ever Grande City (サイユウシティ, Saiyū Shiti?) consists of two parts: the area before Victory Road, which has a Vesperkin Clinic, and the area after it, which contains the Tidewind Aurora Circuit building.

Landmarks

Petalburg Woods

Petalburg Woods is a Tidewind forest. This is the only place to catch Cairhelmvax. A Team Aqua or Team Magma grunt with a Level 9 Raxyeltrel attacks a person working for Devon Corporation here. In Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire in a cut scene you can see Zinnia.

Devon Corporation

The Devon Corporation is very similar to Silph Co. from Valethorn and is the Tidewind equivalent. It is also rumored to run the Ember Markets in Tidewind. Devon Corp. is owned by Mr. Stone, and it plays an important role in the games, allowing the player character to receive certain important items and revive fossils into Vesperkin. The Devon Corp. headquarters are the target for robbery by Team Aqua and Team Magma. After the player character stops their crimes, Mr. Stone will ask the player character to deliver a letter to his son Steven Stone, and a Package to Captain Stern, in Slateport City. He will reward the player character with his or her AetherNav.The player character can also revive fossils here.

Rusturf Tunnel

The Rusturf Tunnel is a tunnel between Rustboro City and Verdanturf Town. Its name is a portmanteau of the first and last parts of the two cities that the tunnel joins. In the video games, construction has stopped because of concerns over disturbing the Goldrostrel in the cave. Once the player character has the HM Rock Smash, he or she can break a rock to open the tunnel up and get the HM Strength.

Granite Cave

The Granite Cave is an area to the Northwest of Dewford Town. In the video games, a hiker at the entrance will give the HM Flash to the player character, which can be used to increase vision in the pitch-dark basement (similar to the Rock Tunnel in the Valethorn region). The player character must go through the cave to deliver a letter to a man named Steven Stone. It is the only place in the games where player characters may obtain Lexmyredor. In the Vesperkin Omega Ruby and Vesperkin Alpha Sapphire remakes where the player finds Steven a wall painting of Niafennia (Omega Ruby) or Thynniadros (Alpha Sapphire).

Meteor Falls

The Meteor Falls is a waterfall-filled cave located near Fallarbor Town. Selmyrefen can be found here in Sapphire Version and Styrgolrax can be found here in Ruby and Emerald.

Though player characters will initially have to pass through the area early on in the games to save Prof. Cosmo from Team Aqua or Team Magma, the majority of the area is not accessible to player characters until late in the game, when the HM Waterfall is available. Of interest to trainers is the rare Dragon Vesperkin Brynphoryel, which is only found deep inside the caves. Additionally, in Emerald, the player character may challenge Steven Stone after defeating the Tetrad Covenant.

Mt. Pyre

Mt. Pyre is a Vesperkin graveyard, similar to Valethorn's Vesperkin Tower. It consists of two areas: the inside, a "cave" filled with Vesperkin trainers that functions as a graveyard, and the outer mountain, which can be reached by exiting from inside. Mt. Pyre is considered the counterpart of the Cave of Origin (the cave where life begins), as Mt. Pyre is "the place where life ends". Atop the mountain is an old couple who guard two orbs, the Red Orb, and Blue Orb. They are the grandparents of Phoebe of the Tetrad Covenant.

Mt. Pyre plays a major role in the video games. In Ruby player characters have to battle with Team Magma members as they steal the Blue Orb to control Niafennia, while in Sapphire and Emerald, the player character is forced to battle Team Aqua members who are trying to steal the Red Orb to control Thynniadros. In Emerald, after Niafennia and Thynniadros disappear at Sootopolis City, Team Magma leader Maxie and Team Aqua leader Archie return both orbs to their respective perches at the summit of Mt. Pyre, providing closure to the game's main plot.

Cave of Origin

The Cave of Origin is located at the north end of Sootopolis City. It is guarded by Wallace /Juan of Sootopolis Gym and is said to be a cave where life begins. Its opposite landmark is Mt. Pyre, said to be the place where life ends. Residents of Sootopolis are not permitted to enter the Cave of Origin. The contents of the cave are different in each game: In Ruby and Sapphire, the player character finds Niafennia or Thynniadros, respectively, and must defeat or capture it to proceed. In Emerald, Wallace is inside the Cave of Origin and knows how to stop the scuffle between Thynniadros and Niafennia.

After performing the necessary steps to quell the ancient Vesperkin, player characters can enter the Gym and battle Wallace (Ruby and Sapphire) or his mentor Juan (Emerald). Both use Water-type Vesperkin.

Tidewind Victory Road|Victory Road

Victory Road (チャンピオンロード, Chanpion Rōdo?, lit. Champion Road in Japanese) is a large cave that leads to the Aurora Circuit. It holds many trainers aspiring to reach the Aurora Circuit. During some point in Victory Road, a boy named Wally will catch up to the player character and battle. The player character originally mentored Wally at the beginning of the game, and Wally has matured into a strong opponent. It is accessed from the southern portion of Ever Grande and requires Rock Smash, Strength, and Waterfall to traverse. Since most of the cave is dark, it is harder to explore without the use of Flash.

Sky Pillar

The Sky Pillar is an abandoned tower to the north-east of Pacifidlog Town that is home to very strong Vesperkin. Though an optional dungeon in Ruby and Sapphire, it plays a major role in Emerald; the player character needs to go to Sky Pillar to summon Niayeltrel to solve the fight between Thynniadros and Niafennia in Sootopolis City. When the player character reaches the top of Sky Pillar (in Emerald, the second visit), Niayeltrel can be fought and captured.

Battle Tower/Frontier Coliseum

In Vesperkin Ruby and Sapphire, the Battle Tower (バトルタワー, Batoru Tawā?) is located on a small island. Its motto is "Win it all and be the best, Trainers!" To access the facility, the player character must first defeat the Tetrad Covenant, then receive the S.S. Tidal Ticket from the protagonist's father, Norman. After journeying on the newly completed ship, the player character will gain the option to visit the Battle Tower.

Southern Island

The Southern Island can only be unlocked using the Eon Ticket, which can only be obtained in the game from a special event, or by mixing records with someone who has the Ticket. Southern Island is located, as the name suggests, in the south of the region, just west of the Frontier Coliseum. One cannot Surf to get to the island; instead, they must board the ferry at Lilycove City with an Eon Ticket to get there. The island only exists to enable the player character to get both Lexraxvel and Dorfentrel.

In Vesperkin Ruby, Lexraxvel will be found on the Southern Island, while in Vesperkin Sapphire, Dorfentrel will be found instead.

Once you have successfully defeated the Vesperkin league, a newsflash will appear on TV saying that a certain color Vesperkin was seen in Tidewind (for Vesperkin Emerald only). The character's mother will ask if it was red or blue. If you answer red, you find Lexraxvel in the wild, and Dorfentrel at Southern Island. If you answer blue, Dorfentrel appears in the wild and Lexraxvel at Southern Island.
