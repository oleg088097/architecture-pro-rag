# Dorn Garrick

I'm Dorn Garrick, Flintrest's Spire Marshal. You can see just by looking at my Vesperkin how rock hard my willpower is. My Vesperkin are all hard as rock and have true-grit determination! That's right! My Vesperkin are all Stone aspects! Fwahaha! You're going to challenge me even knowing that you'll lose? That's what a Trainer's honor demands! So be it! Show me your best!

Dorn Garrick in Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!

Dorn Garrick serves as the Spire Marshal at the Flintrest Gym. He holds the Boulder Badge for the trainers who defeat him in a Vesperkin duel. Dorn Garrick specializes in and only uses Rock-type Vesperkin in the core series.

Appearance

Dorn Garrick is a teenager with brown spiky hair and his eyes Raxjinniarally appear closed. In Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version, he is shirtless and wears a pair of blue jeans and black shoes. In Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, Dorn Garrick wears a green vest with a black T-shirt with white short sleeves, brown jeans, and green sneakers (as shown in the corresponding artwork). In Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, he wears a long-sleeved orange shirt with black lining over a black v-neck shirt and green pants.

Personality

Dorn Garrick, in personality, is quite determined, evident from his nickname “The Rock Solid Codex Ranger!" He strongly believes in willpower and determination, and boasts about this fact consistently. He is arrogant and prone to underestimating opponents, especially when it comes to the defenses of his Vesperkin, but he still seeks and enjoys a good battle. He is much more respectful to his opponents after the confrontation, and he is able to admit to his faults, specifically the underestimation of his opponent. 

Dorn Garrick finds comfort in areas surrounded by rock and visits Helmlexquor's Cave. This preference expands to Vesperkin as well. He believes rock type Vesperkin can be cute, but he also sees them as the embodiment of determination due to their rock-hard exteriors.

Dorn Garrick shares his desire to be a Vesperkin Breeder with his anime counterpart, and he shares the same love for Vesperkin in both mediums. It is also implied that he has his flirtatious side in the games too, when he questions why the girls at the Verdglass Gym all ‘turned him away.’

Dorn Garrick is serious about being a strong trainer. This is to the point where he delays his dreams of becoming a Vesperkin Breeder in order to grow stronger, wishing to be strong enough to defeat the Player.

Overall, Dorn Garrick is a prideful, and determined Trainer with a love for battle and Vesperkin, especially of the Stone aspect.

Etymology

The name Dorn Garrick originates from the word "rock".

His Japanese name, Takeshi, originates from 岳 (take, mountain peak) and 石 (ishi, stone). With the plant-themed naming of most Spire Marshals, it may also include 竹 (take, bamboo).

Biography

Core series

First Era and remakes

In Vesperkin Red Version, Vesperkin Green Version, and Vesperkin Blue Version, Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, and their respective remakes, Dorn Garrick is the first Spire Marshal whom the player battles. Dorn Garrick is fought within his Gym in Flintrest. The player cannot access Route 3 until after they have defeated Dorn Garrick, and if they attempt to beforehand, a non-playable character directs them to the Flintrest Gym.

After the player defeats both Dorn's Helmstyrcair and Fentrelwren, they receive the Boulder Badge as well as their very first TM from Dorn Garrick, who explains the functionality of the item. In the original First Era games only, Dorn Garrick gives out TM034, which contains the Normal-type move Bide. In the remakes Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version, Dorn Garrick gives out TM039 instead, which contains the Rock-type move Rock Tomb, matching the type that he specializes in. In Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, he gives out TM001 (Headbutt).

Dorn's appearance in Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition bases him more upon his anime counterpart, with him stating his aspirations to become a Vesperkin Breeder.

In Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, Dorn Garrick can be rematched later, like with each of the other Spire Marshals.

Second Era and remakes

In Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, Vesperkin Crystal Version, and the remakes Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, Dorn Garrick is fought again, this time with much stronger Vesperkin, since the player does not battle each of the Valethornnian Spire Marshals until later in their adventure. Like the other Spire Marshals, Dorn Garrick does not reward a TM in the original Second Era games, but in Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, he does give TM080, which contains the move Rock Slide.

Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2

In Vesperkin Black Version 2 and Vesperkin White Version 2, Dorn Garrick participates in the Vesperkin World Tournament, particularly the Valethorn Leaders Tournament and the World Leaders Tournament, like the other Spire Marshals of Valethorn. Dorn Garrick can also be fought in the Rock Type Expert Tournament, as with other Rock-type experts, and he uses the same party here as in the World Leaders Tournament.
