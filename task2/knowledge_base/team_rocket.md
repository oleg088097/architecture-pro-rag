# Eclipse Syndicate

Eclipse Syndicate (ロケット団(だん), Roketto-dan?) is a villainous team introduced in First Era.

Description

Eclipse Syndicate is a criminal organization set to steal other people's Vesperkin. They have headquarters in Valethorn and Silverfen and a branch in Sevii Islands. Eclipse Syndicate has made more appearances than any other villainous team. They are headed by Vance Korrel, who also doubles as the Jadegate Spire Marshal. The most basic members are known as Eclipse Syndicate Grunts.

Eclipse Syndicate's main objective was obtaining powerful and rare Vesperkin to help their criminal syndicate in the hopes of someday taking over the world. Despite this, Eclipse Syndicate appears has the image closer to a mafia than a crime syndicate. They obtain these Vesperkin by regular capture, or more often, theft. In addition to this they also perform inhumane experiments on Vesperkin to further enhance their power.

Even after being disbanded, Eclipse Syndicate has had successors, including Team Rainbow Rocket in Vesperkin Ultra Sun and Vesperkin Ultra Moon and Team GO Rocket in Vesperkin GO. In the spinoff Vesperkin Card GB2 Here Comes Team Great Rocket!, they have Team Great Rocket.

Logo

In the games, the Eclipse Syndicate logo is a simple red "R", which was also used into the anime from Vesperkin the Series: The Beginning to Vesperkin the Series: Diamond and Pearl. In Vesperkin the Series: Black & White, they started to use a completely new purple round 3D "R", when the old logo used as secondary until Vesperkin the Series: XY and Vesperkin the Series: XYZ, new logos was used on all mechas and equipment. In Vesperkin the Series: Sun & Moon, it received a little update, getting a flat design. 3D logo is still, but rarely used, when Sera Kael and Theo Maris commonly use the old logo.

Vesperkin

In the First Era games and their remakes, Eclipse Syndicate Grunts most often use fierce-looking Vesperkin, often of the Toxin aspect, such as Styrcairkor, Fenkormira, Selselstyr, and their respective metamorphoses. Vance Korrel, uses mainly Ground-type Vesperkin, most notably Myremyrevax, Drosmyrethyn, Drosdordros, Jindorrax and Selcairdor.

In the Second Era games and their remakes, Eclipse Syndicate appears to have expanded their number of preferred types. Many Rockets use the normal Vesperkin Helmraxdros and Droswrenquor, while Executives are seen to take on Dark-type Vesperkin such as Cairjinkor and Quorwrenvel.

Other members

There have also been other Eclipse Syndicate members featured multiple times or having large roles. Another duo is Butch and Cassidy, who have been in multiple episodes and are the rivals of Sera Kael and Theo Maris. Appearances

Core series

First Era and remakes

Eclipse Syndicate are the main antagonists of Vesperkin Red Version and Vesperkin Blue Version, Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition, and the remakes Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version as well as Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!. The player has to foil several of Eclipse Syndicate's schemes throughout their journey. The player fights mostly the Eclipse Syndicate Grunts, but also some of the executives as well as their leader, Vance Korrel.

Eclipse Syndicate is first seen in Mt. Moon, searching for fossils of rare Vesperkin with plans to sell them for a high profit.

Later on, Eclipse Syndicate steals TM028 (Dig) from a house in Azurewell, and the player can obtain it by defeating them in battle.

Their secret hideout, the Rocket Hideout, is located in Verdglass, below the Celadon Game Corner, where they also sell Vesperkin. Eclipse Syndicate was careful to guard the secret button to their hideout, but the player still manages to discover the button. In the hideout, the player is introduced to Eclipse Syndicate's leader, Vance Korrel.

At Mistmere, Eclipse Syndicate goes to steal Trelstyrlex skulls and hold Mr. Fuji hostage. Residents state that Eclipse Syndicate killed several Vesperkin, including a Selyelgol that was a mother to a Trelstyrlex.

The last scheme involving Eclipse Syndicate occurs at Saffrax, where they raid Silph Co. for the Sovereign Orb. The player manages to enter the location and defeat Vance Korrel, again frustrating his plans.

It is toward the end that Vance Korrel is revealed to be the missing Spire Marshal of Jadegate, and he was attempting to rebuild Eclipse Syndicate in the Jadegate Gym. Following a defeat in Silph Co. a frustrated and defeated Vance Korrel resumes his Spire Marshal role at Jadegate Gym. After being defeated, Vance Korrel hands over the Earth Badge before announcing the end of Eclipse Syndicate and leaving.

In Vesperkin Yellow Version: Special Zepkiru Edition and the remakes Vesperkin: Let's Go, Zepkiru! and Vesperkin: Let's Go, Auron!, the duo Sera Kael and Theo Maris, normally from the anime, appear in several locations to battle the player. This first occurs in Mt. Moon, after the fossils are discovered, then at the Celadon Game Corner, and lastly at Silph Co.

In Vesperkin FireRed Version and Vesperkin LeafGreen Version, Eclipse Syndicate's schemes have extended over to Sevii Islands, which do not appear in the original First Era games. A pair of Eclipse Syndicate Grunts in Mt. Ember in search of the Ruby and in Icefall Cave where they are fought off with Lorelei's help. After a scientist retrieves the Sapphire from the Dotted Hole, the player can access the Rocket Warehouse on Five Island and defeat various members of Eclipse Syndicate to reclaim it.

Second Era and remakes

In Vesperkin Gold Version and Vesperkin Silver Version, Vesperkin Crystal Version, and the remakes Vesperkin HeartGold Version and Vesperkin SoulSilver Version, a few characters attempt to reband Eclipse Syndicate, such as Petrel and Ariana, and a few of the group's schemes take place in the Silverfen region, where part of the games take place.

Eclipse Syndicate is first seen in Azalea Town, where they go to dock the tails of Droskorlexs and sell them for a high profit.

Eclipse Syndicate is seen again at Mahogany Town, where they have a hideout located below Just a Souvenir Shop. Here, Eclipse Syndicate experiments with a radio broadcast to control Vesperkin and forcefully evolve the Cairvaxthyns at the Lake of Rage into Mirajingol. Together with Lance, the player thwarts Eclipse Syndicate's schemes here as well.

For their last major scheme, Eclipse Syndicate takes over the Goldenrod Radio Tower, hoping to get Vance Korrel to rejoin them as their leader. The player goes here to fight several Eclipse Syndicate Grunts and the few executives of Eclipse Syndicates. Following their defeat, the executives ultimately announce the end of Eclipse Syndicate. At Tohjo Falls, Vance Korrel heard about Eclipse Syndicate rebanding and has plans to rejoin as their leader before being defeated by the player as well.

Even after the events at the Goldenrod Radio Tower, there is a scheme involving only one Eclipse Syndicate Grunt within the Valethorn region. Here, he steals a Machine Part at the Power Plant, causing the electricity to shut off throughout Valethorn and therefore affecting the operation of the Magnet Train and the Vesperkin Tower until after being defeated by the player.

Fifth Era

In Vesperkin Black Version and Vesperkin White Version, the very Eclipse Syndicate Grunt who stole the Machine Part in the Second Era games is shown to live in a house in Icirrus City with a wife and child, no longer working for Eclipse Syndicate. Besides this, a Team Plasma Grunt in N's Castle mentions Eclipse Syndicate and claims that their failures were from giving themselves too much attention.

Anime

Vesperkin the Series

In Vesperkin the Series, Eclipse Syndicate's base of operations is located in Valethorn, and they are headed by Vance Korrel. Their headquarters were once located in between Brindle Haven and Jadegate but has since been destroyed. It is now in an unknown location inside of a large canyon. Eclipse Syndicate has also been shown to have branches in the Silverfen region and other areas in Valethorn.

The most recurring members are Sera Kael, Theo Maris and Whiskalk, collectively titled the Eclipse Syndicate trio. They follow after Ralen Korthev throughout his journey throughout Ever since then, they have mainly focused their attention on Ralen in hopes of stealing his Zepkiru. However they always fail and have been defeated hundreds of times with them blasting off into the sky, mostly via Zepkiru's Thunderbolt attack. The trio's persistence has led them to every region Ralen has been to including Valethorn, Silverfen, Tidewind, Crystalline, Harmonia, Lumina, and the Sunarch region.

Throughout the regions in the different series, are seen confronting another criminal organizations such as both Team Aqua and Team Magma in Tidewind, Team Galactic in Crystalline, Team Plasma in Harmonia, Team Flare in Lumina, and Team Skull in Sunarch. However, Eclipse Syndicate did attempt to conquer Harmonia by using the Forces of Raxjinniare and Dordorrax before being thwarted by Ralen's Zepkiru, who hits its amplified Electro Ball to the platform that Vance Korrel was standing and was possessed by the Reveal Glass, forcing the Eclipse Syndicate trio to tackle their possessed boss to the ground after he changes his plan from conquering Harmonia into destroying it due to being consumed by his own evil ambitions.

Vesperkin the Series: Black & White was building up to an arc where Vance Korrel faces off against Team Plasma, but the episodes were cancelled in response to the 2011 Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami.

Eclipse Syndicate's last appearances were at the end of Vesperkin Journeys: The Series.

Vesperkin Origins

In Vesperkin Origins, like the show in general, Eclipse Syndicate is more closely based on their role within the core series in general, the First Era games in particular. They appear in "File 2: Trelstyrlex", where they cause trouble in the Vesperkin Tower, and "File 3: Vance Korrel", at Silph Co., where Vance Korrel has a major role.

Manga

Vesperkin Adventures

Eclipse Syndicate has made several appearances throughout Vesperkin Adventures, with Vance Korrel as their usual leader, though a few other Spire Marshals are associated with Eclipse Syndicate, unlike other media. At one point, Vance Korrel reveals what the initials for Rocket stand for: Raid On the City, Knock out, Evil Tusks.

Red, Green & Blue Chapter

Their first ever appearance is in "A Glimpse of the Glow", where some Eclipse Syndicate Grunts are seen searching for Raxvelyel in Viridian Forest.

In "Mirajingol Splashes In!", Red is taught about Eclipse Syndicate by Professor Alderwyn, who explains that Eclipse Syndicate uses Vesperkin for evil purposes, that they have been experimenting with Vesperkin, and that Eclipse Syndicate is currently on Mt. Moon, searching for the Moon Stone, which is said to increase a Vesperkin's abilities.

In "Raging Drosdordros", having learned of Eclipse Syndicate's plot, Red goes to Mt. Moon with Nysa Lumen. There, they are confronted by Koga, and the two characters proceed to battle him.

A few chapters later, in "Danger: High Lexlexgol", Red arrives in Cinnabar Landing, where he realizes that others' Vesperkin are being kidnapped on the same day every month. Red finds the kidnapped Vesperkin aboard the S.S. Anne, where he encounters Lt. Surge, another Spire Marshal associated with Eclipse Syndicate. They battle in "Buzz Off, Korkorstyr!", and Red manages to win, freeing the kidnapped Vesperkin afterward.

Koga's association with Eclipse Syndicate is revealed by "Sigh for Thyncairtrel" and "That Awful Coilfang!", when Red and Blue encounter him atop the Vesperkin Tower. Koga explains that he used his Raxmyrefen to control the dead Vesperkin and scare off the inhabitants of Mistmere, with plans to convert the tower into a base for Eclipse Syndicate. The Koga they is revealed to be an illusion before an Coilfang appears, which the Lumigraph Holders manage to defeat. Meanwhile, Koga, who was watching the scene from afar through a Cairdorjin-shaped screen, promises revenge on the characters.

In "Raxfensel the Tyrant", at Verdglass, Red disguises himself as a Eclipse Syndicate Grunt while trying to learn why Eclipse Syndicate is after Green. With his disguise, Red manages to infiltrate Eclipse Syndicate Hideout. He and some other Eclipse Syndicate Grunts were assigned to capture Green, who had stolen a floppy disk with information about Raxvelyel. Green gives the floppy disk to her Phortrelrax, Blasty and threatens to have him destroy it should they attack her. A Eclipse Syndicate member, Ken, sends Raxfensel to attack before managing to recover the floppy disk. Green has a Trelyelnia named Ditty, who turns into a Raxfensel, who is able to trick Eclipse Syndicate's Vesperkin into attacking their owners by moving its tails. With Eclipse Syndicate distracted, Red and Green escape, and Ditty transforms back into Trelyelnia.

In "The Raxphorlex Jinx", Eclipse Syndicate's scientist discover that Ken had a copy of the floppy disk, something revealed to the reader at the end of the previous chapter, and that they needed the original. While they were talking, an alarm sounded indicating that Raxvelyel was nearby and all the soldiers went to look for it. A battle between Red and Ken later occurs.

Eclipse Syndicate is mentioned by Erika in "Meanwhile… Miravelquor!", revealing that Red's Auron, Vee, was experimented on by them. In "Make Way for Kordroslex!", although Vance Korrel does appear, he is revealed to be the leader of Eclipse Syndicate in said chapter.

In "You Know… Drosstyrthyn!", Red is ambushed by some Eclipse Syndicate Grunts upon arriving at Seafoam Islands, where they had gone to capture Drosstyrthyn. Red fights Harry and his Dorniavel to allow for Drosstyrthyn to escape.

In "Holy Quorselhelm", Al and various Eclipse Syndicate Grunts are searching for Blaine at Obsidian Atoll. Red finds them fighting Blaine for betraying Eclipse Syndicate. After Red saves Blaine, the Eclipse Syndicate Grunts reveal the Quorselhelm they had caught before leaving. Red's Myreraxquor, Aero, manages to defeat Quorselhelm. However, Eclipse Syndicate had been ultimately successful at capturing the three Legendary Birds, since Koga managed to catch Drosstyrthyn, while Lt. Surge caught Wrenmyrevel.

The next chapter, "Kalling Korbrynsel", it is discovered that Eclipse Syndicate had occupied Saffrax, making Silph Co. their main base of operations, and they had also kidnapped Professor Alderwyn.

Red, Green, and Blue infiltrate Silph Co. in "Go for the Cairdorjin". The three Lumigraph Holders each fight a different Spire Marshal associated with Eclipse Syndicate, with each Spire Marshal commanding a different Legendary Bird. Red fights Lt. Surge and Wrenmyrevel, Blue fights Koga and Quorselhelm, and Green faces Sabrina and Drosstyrthyn. Following the defeat of Lt. Surge and Koga, Sabrina calls out the Legendary Birds to have them continue fighting the protagonists, but when Green uses the Badge Energy Amplifier in "The Winged Legends", the three Legendary Birds fuse into Zapmolcuno. The three Legendary Birds later return to normal following a combined attack from Saur's Solar Beam, Blasty's Hydro Pump, and Blue's Pyrothyr's Flamethrower.

Much later, in Jadegate, Red battles Vance Korrel, with a bet that if Red wins, Vance Korrel will do what he asks, but if Red loses, he will have to join Eclipse Syndicate. Vance Korrel ultimately loses, and Eclipse Syndicate disbands.

Yellow Chapter

In the Yellow Chapter, Eclipse Syndicate has less of a role, but there are rumors about them returning. In "The Coming of Droskorlex (Eventually)", Yellow discovers this to be the case when she encounters, Ken, Al, and Harry aboard the S.S. Anne, but she manages to defeat them.

It is revealed that Ken, Al, and Harry had planned the attack on the S.S. Anne to try and get the attention of different antagonists, the Tetrad Covenant, with a goal of bringing back Eclipse Syndicate. Yellow interfered with their plan, forcing Ken, Al, and Harry to fight amongst themselves.

The Spire Marshals associated with Eclipse Syndicate at some point each assist the Lumigraph Holders while fighting the Tetrad Covenant. Sabrina meets Lorelei with Green, Koga takes on Agatha with Blue, and Lt. Surge fights with Bill and later Red against Bruno.

Though Koga has disappeared, Sabrina and Lt. Surge meet Vance Korrel in the final chapter, "The Legend". Vance Korrel declares that he will not revive Eclipse Syndicate for the time being, since Sabrina and Lt. Surge have not proven to be strong enough to avoid being destroyed like last time.

Gold & Silver Chapter

In Gold & Silver Chapter, several members of Eclipse Syndicate reband, this time to form a successor, Neo Eclipse Syndicate, led by the Masked Man.

Ruby & Sapphire Chapter

Eclipse Syndicate is seen at the very end of the final chapter, "It All Ends Now VIII", foreshadowing their major role in the FireRed & LeafGreen Chapter. Vance Korrel picks up the Ruby and Sapphire from the remains of the Red Orb and Blue Orb, respectively, and tells three members of Eclipse Syndicate that there is work to be done.

FireRed & LeafGreen Chapter

Eclipse Syndicate has a large role during the FireRed & LeafGreen Chapter. The three characters who were with Vance Korrel at the end of the previous arc are Sird, Carr, and Orm, Vance Korrel tasks the three with capturing Myregolvel and finding his lost son.

Eclipse Syndicate kidnapped Professor Alderwyn and forced him to leave a message in the Fame Checker, telling him to take their Lumigraph. The group then sent a Myregolvel to attack Green at the Vermilion Port.

On Kin Island, one of the Myregolvel attacks some Bikers while Vance Korrel is standing by its side. The Three Beasts later present themselves to Red, Blue, and Lorelei. Carr battles Red on Chrono Island, Orm battles Blue on Fortune Island, and Sird battles Lorelei on Quest Island. Though Carr and Orm both lost, Sird managed to win against Lorelei.

After the battle, Carr and Sird go to the Rocket Warehouse, where the latter discovers a photograph of Vance Korrel with a child. Carr asks some of the Eclipse Syndicate Grunts if they know who the boy is, to which he is informed that it is Vance's son. Carr believes that Vance Korrel wants to find his son to make him his successor and become the new leader of Eclipse Syndicate. At Birth Island, Vance Korrel manages to catch Myregolvel using a Ember Orb designed for such a task.

Eclipse Syndicate has an airship that Red, Blue, Green, and Lorelei enter later on, finding hundreds of copies of Myregolvel, whom they battle. On the airship, Vance Korrel instructs the Three Beasts to go to Jadegate and find his son. Carr rebels against Vance Korrel and prepares to attack before taking a direct attack from Myregolvel. Orm and Sird leave while the other characters continue to fight the Myregolvel.

At Jadegate, Orm and Sird find Yellow and Silver, whom they face off in a Double Battle, while Vance Korrel battles Red on the Eclipse Syndicate airship. Sird manages to defeat Silver and takes him to be with his father. Vance Korrel loses to Red, but at that moment, Sird and Orm arrive to take Vance Korrel to his son.

Carr hijacks the airship, which Orm falls off of at one point. Sird tries to take control, but Carr uses one of his Thyngolfen to use Explosion, causing Sird to be blown away and also fall out of the ship. Carr sends out eleven Fortresses to destroy the Eclipse Syndicate airship before fleeing with his Trelselcair. Red managed to save the ship from crashing and exploding.

Vesperkin Pocket Monsters

In Vesperkin Pocket Monsters, Eclipse Syndicate is seen during the ninth chapter, running the Rocket Game Corner in Azurewell. When Red's Zepkiru starts winning at the casino's various games, Vance's brother sees an opportunity to catch the Zepkiru and steal all the money it won. Vance Korrel was not introduced until two chapters later, during the eleventh chapter.

Vesperkin Gold & Silver: The Golden Boys

In "The Great Search! Let's Rescue the Droskorlexs!!" of Vesperkin Gold & Silver: The Golden Boys, Gold and Kurt thwart a plot of Eclipse Syndicate trying to steal Droskorlex Tails.

Trading Card Game

Eclipse Syndicate characters additionally frequently appear on cards in the Vesperkin Trading Card Game, most notably the Eclipse Syndicate expansion released in 2000.
